# Competitor Product Inventory - Singapore

When a prospect says *"I already have something from Prudential"* or *"my mother has a Star Secure"*, you need to know in 10 seconds what they actually own. This is the lookup. Six insurers, four categories, current retail lines as of January 2026.

Product names get renamed and rebranded faster than the brochures get updated, so always verify on the insurer's official Singapore site before quoting numbers. Premium, sum-assured, and feature claims in this document are NOT current quotes - they are positioning cues only.

---

## How to use this document

**Hearing a brand prefix and not sure who sells it?** Brand prefixes are the fastest tell:

| Prefix you hear | Insurer | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `AIA ...` | AIA Singapore | AIA Pro Achiever 3.0, AIA HealthShield Gold Max |
| `GREAT ...` / `Prestige ...` | Great Eastern | GREAT Life Multiplier, Prestige Life Rewards |
| `PRU...` | Prudential Singapore | PRUShield, PRUActive Life, PRUVantage Wealth |
| `Singlife ...` | Singlife (post-Aviva merger) | Singlife Shield, Singlife Whole Life Choice |
| `Aviva My...` | Singlife (legacy Aviva, closed to new business) | Aviva MyShield, Aviva MyLifeIncome |
| `Star ...` / `Gro ...` / `Complete ...` / `IncomeShield` / `VivoLife` | Income Insurance (formerly NTUC Income) | Star Secure Pro, Gro Retire Flex Pro |
| `Manu...` / `Ready...` / `LifeReady` / `Signature ...` | Manulife Singapore | ManuProtect Term, ReadyBuilder, LifeReady Plus |

**Integrated Shield Plan carriers in Singapore (7 total):** AIA, Great Eastern, HSBC Life, Income Insurance, Prudential, Singlife, Raffles Health Insurance. *Manulife does NOT sell an IP.* If a client says "I have Manulife Shield", they are misremembering — clarify what they actually hold.

**CareShield Life supplement providers (3 only):** Singlife (CareShield Standard / Plus), Great Eastern (GREAT CareShield), Income Insurance (Care Secure / Care Secure Pro). *AIA, Prudential, and Manulife do not currently underwrite their own CareShield Life supplement.* Prudential distributes Income's "Care Secure" under their brand.

**MAS Direct Purchase Insurance (DPI):** Term, whole life, and IP plans sold without commission via direct channels. Every IP insurer plus AIA, Great Eastern, Income, Manulife, Prudential, Singlife offer DPI variants. They are flagged inline below — sum assured is capped (typically S$200k–S$400k) and no advice is provided.

## When you don't recognise a name

1. Ask the client what year they bought it. Pre-2015 plans are usually closed-to-new-business — recognise the brand prefix, look up the modern successor.
2. Ask for the policy schedule or annual statement PDF. The product name is on page 1.
3. If the brand prefix doesn't match any of the six above, it's likely **Tokio Marine Life**, **HSBC Life**, **China Life Singapore**, or a foreign-domiciled offshore policy. Those aren't in scope of this doc — flag to your senior FC.
4. The Total Wealth Concept Policy Summary tool at present.financeillustrator.com lets you input any policy by hand even if the product name isn't in a dropdown.
