# Day 11: No experience, and not an extrovert

## Lack of experience

When I started, I knew nothing about sales. Zero. I didn't know how to build a business, handle rejections, create a sales pipeline, or even give a good presentation. Outsourcing, productivity, delegation, sales scripts, frameworks and prospecting were all foreign to me. Over the past nine years I've learned it all, and everything I've learned, I'll teach you.

Experience isn't what decides your success. What matters most is your aptitude, your attitude and your willingness to put in the time. If you stay coachable, keep an open mind and approach every day hungry to learn, you'll grow faster than you ever imagined. The moment you think you know it all, you stop learning, and that's when growth stops.

It's not about where you start. It's about the direction you choose. As long as you're willing to put in the effort, stay grateful for the guidance you get, and keep improving every day, experience will follow. With the frameworks, systems and mentorship I'll share with you, you'll have what you need to build a strong foundation.

Remember, I started from zero too. If I can do it, so can you.

## Must you be an extrovert?

Not at all. When I started, I had a stammering problem. I'm also an introvert by nature. I've always been the quiet type with a small social network. I wasn't the kind of person you'd expect to do well in a career that involves meeting people and selling. But being a great talker isn't what makes you successful in this field.

What matters far more is your ability to listen. Clients don't want someone who talks at them. They want someone who listens to their concerns, understands their needs and offers thoughtful solutions. Many of the most successful advisors I know, including me, aren't natural speakers or extroverts. We do well because we focus on building trust, asking good questions and really hearing what our clients say.

And you don't have to figure it all out on your own. We have plenty of sales scripts, frameworks and proven systems that guide you step by step. You don't need to be a natural at talking or selling. You just need to follow the process, stay coachable and put in the effort.

If someone like me, quiet, introverted and once struggling with a stammer, can succeed, I'm confident you can too. This career isn't about being the loudest voice in the room. It's about being the one who listens and delivers real value.

## Worksheet — honest reframe

Three questions. Write the answers down.

1. **After reading this, does "I have no experience" still hold you back? Why?**
2. **Does "I'm not an extrovert" still hold you back? Why?**
3. **Underneath both objections, what's the real concern?** Usually something like *"I'm not sure I can handle rejection, variability, or the self-starter nature."* That's a real concern, and it's the one Day 9's 6 C's already surfaced.
