## Your questions, answered

### 1. What is the FINternship Programme, and how does it relate to AIA?

The FINternship programme is a personal initiative by me to help aspiring advisors understand the job before officially joining AIA.

While it's not an official AIA programme, it aligns with AIA's values. You'll be trained under me (under my agency, [Win Financial](https://www.winfinancial.com.sg/), which is a sub-group under AIA), and upon passing your exams, you'll be onboarded into AIA as a licensed advisor.

### 2. Will I be working for you or AIA, and how is this different from joining AIA directly?

Initially, you'll work under my mentorship in the FINternship programme.

Once contracted, you'll be officially part of AIA, while remaining in my team. The key difference? My programme gives you a trial period, mentorship and early support, which you won't get by going straight to AIA.

### 3. How does AIA's support compare to your programme, and how do I transition officially?

AIA has strong training and support once you're in. My FINternship programme adds personal mentorship, early exposure and hands-on learning. When you're ready, I'll help you move fully into AIA's system. You're never doing it alone.

### 4. What is the onboarding process like, and how does training work?

It starts with an application. If you proceed, you'll go through hands-on training and mentorship even before you officially join AIA.

You can take the CMFAS exams as a private candidate with my support. After passing, we handle onboarding into my agency under Win Financial (AIA).

### 5. Who are our target clients?

We mainly serve young adults in their 20s to early 30s, who are just starting their financial journeys.

They're usually looking to budget better, save for goals, or plan their future, but they lack clear guidance.

That's where we come in: to educate and clarify, not just to sell.

### 6. How long will the FINternship programme take? Can I do it at my own pace?

Yes! It's self-paced, designed to fit into your life. Most complete it in 2 to 3 months, but there's no deadline. Whether you're studying or working, it adapts to your schedule.

### 7. I don't want to forcefully sell to my friends and family. What if they say no or want to cut ties?

Totally valid concern. Reaching out to friends and family is not required. It's just a bonus if you're comfortable.

In fact, 99.9% of my clients were strangers. I learned cold marketing, built systems, and now teach those same strategies in the FINternship programme.

You'll learn to attract clients who are already looking for help, with no hard selling needed. We support our consultants by running lead generation campaigns, hiring telemarketers, giving virtual assistant support for backend admin, and digital marketing support in general (backed by our in-house digital marketing agency).

### 8. What if I'm a student? Will I have time?

Yes, absolutely. Many of our top performers are students. I started in my first year of engineering, juggling lectures, projects and hall life.

That forced me to eliminate distractions, build efficient systems, and focus only on high-impact activities.

With just 20 hours a week, I hit MDRT in my first year, and you'll get access to the same systems.

### 9. How long does it take to clear the exams?

Each CMFAS exam takes about 2 weeks to 1 month to prepare for. Most candidates clear all four within 1.5 to 2 months.

You'll get study guides, summaries and mock exams to help you prepare faster.

### 10. How long is the process after I pass all four exams?

The onboarding and licensing process usually takes 2 to 4 weeks.

During that time, we'll walk you through product coaching, soft skills, and everything you need to start advising with confidence.

### 11. I'm studying and working. Can I take my time and be an FA part-time?

Absolutely. That's how I started too.

You'll get systems, scripts and workflows to make the most of limited time. It's 100% possible to succeed as a part-time student advisor.

### 12. What happens if I don't pass the CMFAS exams?

There are no penalties. You can retake the exams as many times as you need.

If you're a sponsored candidate, we only cover your first attempt.

### 13. Are the CMFAS exams conducted online?

No. All CMFAS exams are held in person at 9 Temasek Boulevard, #14-01/02/03, Suntec Tower Two, Singapore 038989.

### 14. If I pass all the exams and contract but don't get any clients, do I have to return the $1K payout?

Nope! As long as you pass all the exams and officially contract, you keep the $1K, even if you later decide it's not for you and resign. No repayment is required.

### 15. Is there any way I can work without a quota to hit? I already have a full-time job or other commitments.

Yes, you can join without quotas.

To qualify for the Entrepreneur Power Scheme (EPS), our commission-matching incentive, you'll need to meet some targets. For instance, the $2K a month EPS needs $2K in average commissions a month for 12 months. EPS runs for 2 years and includes a catch-up feature.

Speak to me for a full breakdown.

### 16. What is the minimum quota when I contract as an agent or student advisor?

A basic benchmark is to close at least one case a month. We'll give you the tools, mentorship and templates to hit that with confidence.

### 17. What happens if I can't hit my quotas?

There's no termination. You just won't receive the EPS bonus for that month. We also have a 3-month catch-up feature, so you can make up for a missed month and get back-pay on the EPS payouts you missed.

### 18. What happens if I leave halfway? Do I have to repay the CMFAS exam costs?

Yes. If you don't contract, you'll need to reimburse the exam fees we sponsored.

If you pass the exams and contract, but later decide it's not for you, you keep everything. No repayment is needed.
