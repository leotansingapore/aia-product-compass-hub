# Competitor Pricing & Spec Reference

**Use this as the source of truth for competitor comparisons in training content.** Every row below is grounded in either a competitor's published brochure or its public website. Pricing on every line is time-bound and should be verified in iPOS or on the competitor's site before being quoted in a real appointment.

**Two rules:**

1. **Cite the source.** Every competitor claim used in a client conversation should be traceable to a competitor-published document or URL. Don't quote a figure that traces back only to an internal Loom training.
2. **Don't quote stale percentages.** Phrases like "AIA is 11% cheaper than Prudential" or "29% cheaper than Manulife" originate from old Pro Achiever training Loom videos. Those comparisons may not hold today. If you need a like-for-like price comparison, generate a fresh iPOS quote and a fresh competitor quote on the same profile.

## Pro Achiever 3.0 (APA) — competitor matrix

**Primary source for verified rows:** `docs/product-mastery-track/week-1/_source-resources/APA-Competitor-Analysis.pdf` (where present), plus the competitor's public brochure or website.

### Post-IIP / perpetual supplementary charges

| Product | Charge after IIP | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **AIA Pro Achiever 3.0 (post-IIP)** | **0%** | AIA PA PS |
| FWD Invest Plus | **1.2% p.a. LIFELONG** supplementary charge from month 25 (year 2) onwards | FWD Invest Plus brochure |
| FWD Invest First Max | Accumulation account charge tapers: 1.6% (Y1–10) → 1.4% (Y11–20) → 1.2% (Y21+). Never zero | FWD Invest First Max brochure |
| iFAST / robo-advisor benchmark | ~1% p.a. perpetual on account value | Platform fee schedules; Leo confirmed for use |

**The pattern across competitors:** small annual fee that never switches off. APA's supplementary charge ends after the 10-year IIP and drops to 0% thereafter.

### FWD Invest Plus — initial account charge by premium term

| Premium payment term | Initial account charge (Y1 → end of IIP) |
|---|---|
| 15–19 years | 1.8% p.a. |
| 20–24 years | 1.4% p.a. |
| 25–29 years | 1.2% p.a. |
| 30 years | 1.0% p.a. |

Effective total in the early years for Invest Plus: roughly **3.0%** (1.8% initial + 1.2% supplementary). After year 1 / month 25, the 1.2% supplementary kicks in and **never leaves**.

### Welcome bonus — AIA Pro Achiever 3.0 canonical bands

| Premium band (annual) | IIP 10 | IIP 15 | IIP 20 |
|---|---|---|---|
| $2,400 / year | 5% | 8% | 10% |
| $4,800 / year | 23% | 33% | 45% |
| $7,200 / year | 38% | 48% | 60% |
| $12,000+ / year | 53% | 63% | 75% |

**Practitioner reality:** focus FC memorisation on the **10-year IIP × $400/mo, $600/mo, $1,000/mo** rows — those are the figures actually quoted in pitches. The 75% theoretical max is only at 20-year IIP × the highest band and rarely lands in practice. Verify in iPOS before quoting.

### Welcome bonus — competitor comparison

| Lock-in / band | AIA Pro Achiever | Manulife (LifeFinity / similar) | FWD Invest Plus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10-yr lock-in, <$12K | 5–53% (band-dependent) | 0% | — |
| 10-yr lock-in, ≥$12K | 53% | 5% | — |
| 15-yr lock-in, <$12K | 8–63% (band-dependent) | 10% | 17–21% (Reward Band 1–5) |
| 15-yr lock-in, ≥$12K | 63% | 15% | 17–21% (Reward Band 1–5) |
| 20-yr lock-in, <$12K | 10–75% (band-dependent) | 15% | Higher bands |
| 20-yr lock-in, ≥$12K | 75% | 20% | Higher bands |

**Caveat:** FWD's reward-band system pays *per year of premium term* and scales with longer commitments (e.g. 30-yr term × ≥$48K = ~34% at Reward Band 5). Don't say "AIA is always 4x" — say **"at the same band, AIA's welcome bonus runs 3–5x the Manulife figure"** and bring the brochure to prove it.

### Surrender / withdrawal penalties

- **AIA Pro Achiever IIP 10:** ~5% by year 10, drops to **0% from year 11 onwards**.
- **Manulife LifeFinity / similar:** Year 1–2 = 100% surrender penalty. **5–40% penalty still applies as far out as year 10.** Minimum Investment Period extending up to **19+ years** in some configurations.

## Ultimate Critical Cover (UCC) — competitor matrix

**UCC canonical specs (per UCC PS + Brochure p.13):**
- **73 underlying CIs** across **150 condition-stage entries** (early / intermediate / major)
- **Unlimited Major-stage claims** (built in, no rider required)
- Early + Intermediate stages capped at 500% combined / **S$350K per-life on the same CI**
- Restoration to 100% if policy in force 12 months after date of latest diagnosed CI
- Three term options: To 65, To 75, To 85
- $3M aggregate cap across all AIA CI policies

### Prudential PRUActive Protect

| Spec | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Critical illnesses covered | **37 CIs** | prudential.com.sg PRUActive Protect brochure |
| Multi-claim | Via separate **"Protect Me Again" rider** (not built in) | Prudential brochure |
| Coverage term | 10–99 years, customisable | Prudential brochure |
| Related-illness restriction | Yes — subsequent claims for related conditions (e.g. heart progression) blocked | Prudential brochure |
| Claims-based pricing (PruShield medical, not CI) | Active | [prudential.com.sg/products/health-insurance/medical/claims-based-pricing](https://www.prudential.com.sg/products/health-insurance/medical/claims-based-pricing) |

**The factual contrast:** Prudential's "related illness" rule blocks subsequent claims in the same condition family. Under UCC, coronary heart disease → bypass → heart attack → transplant are each claimable separately after the 12-month reset.

### Great Eastern — GREAT Critical Cover: Complete

| Spec | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Critical illnesses covered | **53 CIs** | greateasternlife.com |
| Sum assured range | **$50K – $350K** | GE product page |
| Death benefit | **$25K only** — minimal legacy value | GE product page |
| Coverage term | Up to **age 99** | GE product page |
| Multi-claim | 3-claim cap **with Protect Me Again-equivalent rider** | GE product page |

**The factual contrast:** lower sum-assured ceiling, near-zero death benefit, multi-claim only with rider, IP pre-auth suspended at a major private hospital.

### Singlife — Multi-Pay Critical Illness

| Spec | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Critical illnesses covered | **60 CIs** | Singlife Multi-Pay CI brochure (2023) |
| Stage applicability | Many conditions listed "Not applicable" for Early / Intermediate stages | Singlife brochure |

**The factual contrast:** 60 vs UCC's 73 — missing 13 conditions. Real-world early-stage coverage is narrower because many conditions are "Not applicable" at early/intermediate.

## AIA CI products — same-company comparators (NOT competitors)

**Important:** Beyond Critical Cover (BCC) and Absolute Critical Cover (ASCC) are **AIA products**, not competitors. When the client already holds BCC or ASCC:

- **UCC + BCC**: both held together. UCC adds breadth (73 CIs across 150 condition-stages) and unlimited Major-stage claims; BCC retains its built-in relapse coverage and age-85 premium refund (per BCC's AIA PDF). Layered AIA protection.
- **UCC + ASCC**: same approach — inter-AIA layering, not replacement.

For BCC's exact specs (illness count, refund mechanic, claim cap), cite BCC's own AIA product PDFs.

## What to do when an FC needs a fresh comparison

1. **AIA side**: pull a current illustration in iPOS for the exact profile (age, smoker, sum assured, term, IIP, rider mix). Save the BI to client folder.
2. **Competitor side**: pull the competitor's current brochure / product page (links above). Do not rely on a screenshot from 2022.
3. **Document both sources** in the case note. If you quote a competitor figure in the meeting, you should be able to show the prospect the page it came from.
4. **For percentage savings claims** ("X% cheaper than competitor Y"): generate the comparison fresh. The historical Loom training figures (11% cheaper than Prudential, 29% cheaper than Manulife, 22% cheaper than Singlife, 6% cheaper than GE) are time-bound — they may not hold on today's product variants.
