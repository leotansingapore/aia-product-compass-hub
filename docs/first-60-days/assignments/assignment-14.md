> **The gist.** Your Marketing Kit is the deck you put in front of every prospect - the thing that introduces **you**, your **services**, and **AIA**, in the order that matters. This assignment is where you build your own copy of it, print your referral letter, and bookmark the reference decks you'll pull up mid-meeting. It's a checklist: duplicate, edit, print, bookmark. [Day 39](/learning-track/first-60-days/day/39) covers *why* the kit matters; this is *where you build it*.

## Why the kit matters

Without a kit, your first appointment leans on talk alone. With one, every prospect leaves with something tangible and a clear sense of who you are. The order it sells in:

> **Sell yourself -> sell the company -> sell your services -> the product is "by the way".**

People buy because they trust you and believe you'll act in their interest. The kit is how you earn that trust on paper before a single product comes up.

## Step 1 - Duplicate and edit the Marketing Kit deck

Open the **[Marketing Kit template](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/19J7VoxCXoEhQR6PZr5g9NozK22XDgyeV/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=117605838416133603235&rtpof=true&sd=true)**, then **File -> Make a copy** into your own Drive.

Now make it yours. Don't just swap the name on a generic template - rework the flow so it's your story:

- Your photo and your real "why I chose this career" - the specific version, not a generic line.
- Your service proposition, values, and the life journey you walk clients through.
- Keep the AIA corporate, agency, fund-manager, and claims sections - those sell the company.
- If it fits your style, build the CST (Why / What / How Much) into the same deck so the kit doubles as your presentation tool.

## Step 2 - Copy, rename, and print your referral letter

Open the **[referral letter](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1F1ehfxh38qh2VTrKrp8Mh-PpDd78Gp8K/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=117605838416133603235&rtpof=true&sd=true)**, **File -> Make a copy**, and change it to your name and details. Then **print a physical copy** to bring to meetings - a printed letter in hand does more than a link ever will.

## Step 3 - Bookmark your reference decks in Chrome

These are the decks you pull up mid-meeting when the conversation turns to a specific need. Open each one, confirm you have access, and **bookmark it in Chrome** where you can reach it fast (a dedicated bookmarks folder works well):

- **[Basic Medical Insurance deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1t2d9qon3RoK6GWbK_r54-TvIGlFLYCCX/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=117605838416133603235&rtpof=true&sd=true)** - use this when presenting medical insurance products.
- **[Claim samples](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/15tqgBlQNIbVldVDSZnlgjF5daEl1myt2oOY9ruEMExg/edit?usp=sharing)** - real claim stories; a single claim story lands harder than ten features.
- **[Investing success + track record](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1-sPSsB7zH_-e3PnagGKjwr2toHfkrAkWWO7w2-NiBH4/edit?usp=sharing)** - the proof behind the wealth conversation.
- **[APA competitor analysis](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1o6rnafqQPV6z4n0NcKWOBTFs_6kntjKUolSB3-v_zOc/edit?usp=sharing)** - how the APA stacks up.
- **[Retirement Planning deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1zJBkSwfQlWoeus1-GqG2GD9SFh5qCToG/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=117605838416133603235&rtpof=true&sd=true)** - use this with pre-retirees.

### The checklist

- [ ] Duplicated the Marketing Kit deck into my own Drive
- [ ] Edited the flow, story, photo and order so it's mine, not the template
- [ ] Copied the referral letter, changed it to my name
- [ ] Printed a physical copy of the referral letter
- [ ] Opened all five reference decks and confirmed I have access
- [ ] Bookmarked all five in Chrome, somewhere I can reach fast in a meeting

## What good looks like

- The kit reads as *yours* - your story and photo, not a template with the name changed.
- The sell order is intact: you, then the company, then your services, with product last.
- A printed referral letter in your name is ready to bring to a meeting.
- All five reference decks open for you and are one click away in Chrome when a prospect raises a specific need.
