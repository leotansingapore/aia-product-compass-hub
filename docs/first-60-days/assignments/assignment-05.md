> **The gist.** Run two logged cold-calling sessions, then set two real first-meeting appointments with a senior FC sitting in as a silent shadow. The goal isn't a clean close - it's an honest baseline, and the fastest coaching loop you'll get in your first 30 days. Log each part as you go; you don't have to finish it all at once.

**The shape of it:** run Session 1 (log every dial) -> debrief and change one thing -> run Session 2 -> set 2 appointments -> write your self-diagnosis.

## Why this matters

Most new FCs don't fail at meetings - they fail at getting meetings. Cold calling is the fastest unblocker once warm market runs dry. The two appointments are the other half: you only get better at running a meeting when someone who's run hundreds watches you run one. Two shadowed appointments this week is the single biggest coaching loop you'll get in the first 30 days. The volume math behind all of this is in [Day 19](../../first-60-days/week-4/day-19.md).

For these practice sessions you just need a phone and your dial list. The full lead-gen setup - business line, cold leads, ads, daily tracking - is a one-time checklist you'll run when you kickstart lead gen after RNF.

## The two cold-calling sessions

Each session is a 90-minute block, calendared, phone-down-except-to-dial. Aim for 30-50 dials. Run one opener consistently in Session 1, then debrief (15 min with Leo or your mentor, log in hand), change one thing - the opener, the question order, the tone - and run Session 2 to see what the numbers do.

Log every dial: number, pickup Y/N, outcome. A dial "landed" only if the prospect stayed on for 2+ minutes AND agreed a next step (callback, meeting, sending info), OR gave you a clean "no" that says they're permanently out. Polite passes with no next step are failed dials - log them anyway, the conversion ratio is the data.

## Set two appointments

From the cold-call pipeline (or warm/semi-warm if cold didn't produce enough in time), set 2 first-meeting appointments with real prospects, each with a senior FC sitting in. Brief the senior FC beforehand: who the prospect is, how they came in, your read on their hot buttons, and where you expect to fumble.

Run the full [5-Phase Sales Meeting](../../first-60-days/week-4/day-21.md) - that's the structure your senior FC grades you against. Don't try to close product in a first meeting.

The senior FC's job is silent observation - no interjection unless you explicitly pass to them, then a 10-minute debrief immediately after the meeting, in the car or coffee shop, not 3 days later when memory has faded.

## What a passing submission looks like

- Session 1 + 2 numbers logged honestly - even if they're ugly
- Two appointments set, each with the prospect's details and the senior FC confirmed
- A specific self-diagnosis (*"I talk too much in phase 3"*, not *"I need to improve"*)

## One more thing

You won't get this volume of shadowing again once onboarding ends, so make these two meetings count. Ask the senior FC for two things: the one move they'd steal from you, and the one thing that would have made you worse if they'd said it out loud mid-meeting. Both are gold.
