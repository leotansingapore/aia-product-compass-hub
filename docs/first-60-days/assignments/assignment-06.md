> **The gist.** Build a one-page picture of the life this career is for - dense with images, light on text - then put it somewhere you'll see it daily. Not decoration: a decision-making anchor for the hard weeks. It's what you look at when you don't feel like making the last three dials, and the image reminds you why the dials exist.

**The shape of it:** watch the walkthrough -> pick a template -> fill 4 of the 6 categories with specific images -> set it as your wallpaper -> share it to the group chat.

## Build it

Watch [Leo's vision board Loom](https://www.loom.com/share/3dbda27b81f24a089e766702348c3076) first - it shows the format and the traps to avoid. Then pick any [Canva vision-board template](https://www.canva.com/templates/s/vision-board/) and edit it for yourself. Figma, Keynote, or a physical collage works too. One page, dense with images, light on text.

Hit at least 4 of these 6 categories, and be specific and visual - a photo of *the exact apartment view* beats the word "home", a photo of *the trip* beats "travel more":

- **Lifestyle** - where you live, what your day looks like
- **People** - who's in the picture with you, specifically
- **Money** - what "enough" looks like as an image, not a round number
- **Career** - MDRT, Court of the Table, a team you lead, a milestone title
- **Growth** - books read, skills mastered, a body kept
- **Give-back** - what you do with the margin this career creates

Two framing rules that make a board work: keep it specific and present-tense ("I am debt-free by Dec 2027", not "I want to be"), and keep it to one page - one image you see every day beats a deck you open once.

Then put it where you'll actually see it: phone wallpaper, laptop desktop, or printed on the wall above your desk. Pick at least one. The board is worthless if it lives in a folder.

## What passing looks like

- One page, 4+ of the 6 categories filled, dense with specific images.
- Specific and present-tense, not stock-photo aspirational.
- Set as your wallpaper (or printed) - it lives somewhere you see daily, not in a folder.

## Submission

1. Upload the PDF / image / Canva export here, or paste the shareable Canva link in the notes.
2. Share it to your **onboarding group chat** for team visibility.
3. A team call reviews everyone's boards ~1-2 months after submission - that's when you hear what peers put on theirs, which sharpens your own.

Treat v1 as the starting image, not the final one - most FCs sharpen the specifics around month 6 and again at year 1. The only bad vision board is the one that doesn't get made.
