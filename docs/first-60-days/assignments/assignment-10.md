> **The gist.** Run **30 warm-market surveys** off your [Project 200 list](/learning-track/pre-rnf/assignments/project-200), across your study weeks, while the "I can't sell you anything yet" framing is still honestly true. It's the reps - not the reading - that make the script yours.

**This one fills up over time.** Run your surveys in blocks across your CMFAS / RES5 study window, then come back and log them as they grow. Build your [Project 200 list](/learning-track/pre-rnf/assignments/project-200) first - the surveys come off those names.

## Why 30, and why before RNF

Complete **30 surveys before your RNF goes through.** Pre-RNF you literally cannot sell, which makes "I'm just doing a survey" honest in a way it never will be again - once licensed, the same line reads as a soft pitch.

The first five feel awkward; you over-explain and freeze on the close. Around survey 6 to 15 the script settles and the rapport intro shrinks from 90 seconds to 30. By survey 30 it's yours - so when your RNF drops, you call the next 170 names with no friction. Do fewer than 30 and you carry the awkwardness into your real warm market and burn your easiest closes.

## How to practise (climb the ladder)

Don't dial cold into your real semi-warm market on day one. Each stage builds the muscle for the next.

| Stage | Reps | Who you call | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Solo** | 5 to 10 read-throughs | Yourself - mirror, voice memo | Words flow without freezing |
| **2. Cohort roleplay** | 3 to 5 | Another pre-RNF FC | Swap roles, throw objections |
| **3. Hot warm-up** | 3 to 5 calls | Family, partner, best friends | A gentle live audience to settle nerves |
| **4. Real semi-warm** | The remaining ~20 | Project 200 Priority A and B | Where the real practice happens |

Cluster the calls - 5 to 7 in a focused 90-minute block beats one a day. Your voice warms up by call 2 and peaks around call 5.

## Watch for these

- **Don't turn Q4 into a pitch.** It's a question. The appointment ask comes after they answer it.
- **Don't skip the social-add.** That's how 30 surveys becomes 100+ touchpoints over the next year.
- **Don't do all 30 with hot market.** Hot market exhausts; semi-warm is where the script is actually tested, so most of the 30 must be semi-warm.

## What a passing submission looks like

At least 25 of 30 surveys logged - each with the person's name, your relationship to them, and the outcome - plus a credible plan to clear the last five before RNF. Your booked-appointment rate should visibly improve between the first and second halves of the list.
