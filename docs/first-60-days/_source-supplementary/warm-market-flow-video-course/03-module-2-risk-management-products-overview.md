## III. GUARANTEE PROTECT PLUS (GPP) - DETAILED

### Key Features:

**1. Participating Whole Life Plan**
- Participates in PAR fund profits
- AIA invests premiums through bonds
- Declares bonuses over time
- Client offloads investment responsibility to AIA

**2. Multiplier Coverage System**
- Base sum insured × multiplier (2x, 3x, or 5x)
- Multiplier drops at age 65 or 75
- Example: $100,000 base × 5 = $500,000 coverage until drop-off age

**3. Limited Premium Payment**
- Pay for 15, 20, or 25 years
- **Selection Guide:**
  - Ages 20-30s: 20 years recommended
  - Ages 40s: 15 years recommended (finish before retirement)
  - Shorter tenure = better returns

**4. Income Draw Down Feature**
- Available at age 65-85 (after multiplier drop-off)
- Can withdraw as 10-year income stream instead of lump sum
- Makes GPP function like tail-end annuity

**5. Riders Available**
- **CPL (Critical Protector Life):** Major CI coverage
- **Early Critical Protector:** Early stage CI coverage
- **Waiver Riders:** Premium waiver upon CI diagnosis
- **Payer Benefit:** For child policies (waives if parent gets CI)

### Sample Illustration (30-year-old male, non-smoker):

**Scenario:** $500,000 death coverage with full CI
- 3x multiplier until age 65
- 20-year payment term
- Premium: ~$7,300/year
- Total paid over 20 years: $147,000
- Cash value at 65: ~$115,000

**Important CI Structure:**
- Base plan = Life coverage with cash value
- CI rider = Non-participating, NO cash value
- Total premium includes both components

### Multiplier Comparison Strategy:

**3x Multiplier:**
- Age 70 cash value: $149,000
- Sum drops to ~$294,000 at age 66

**2x Multiplier:**
- Higher premium (~$8,900 vs $7,300)
- Age 70 cash value: ~$224,000 (差$75,000 more)
- Sum drops less dramatically to ~$341,000 at age 66

**Sales Tip:** Show client 3x first, then demonstrate 2x value to upsize premium naturally.

## IV. SECURE FLEX TERM (SFT)

### Product Positioning:
- Pure term insurance (no cash value)
- Complements GPP in packaging strategies
- Most affordable way to hit high coverage targets

### Key Features:

**1. Coverage Options**
- **Tenure:** 5, 10, 20, or 30 years (renewable term)
- **Level Term:** Cover until age 65 or 75 (fixed premium)
- Terminal illness benefit included
- Guaranteed renewable until age 100

**2. Sample Premium (30-year-old male, non-smoker)**
- $1 million death coverage until 65: **$882/year**
- $1 million death + $250K CI until 65: **~$1,500/year**

**3. Vitality Integration**
- First year: 10% discount
- Subsequent years: Up to 15% discount based on status
  - Gold: 1% additional
  - Platinum: 2% additional

**4. Rider Options**
- **Major CI:** Up to $250,000 (accelerated benefit)
- **Early CI:** Available for 20/30-year renewable terms (NOT for level terms)
- Early CI accelerates from major CI, which accelerates from death benefit

**Important Note:** Level term (till 65/75) CANNOT add early CI rider - only major CI available.

## V. ULTIMATE CRITICAL COVER (UCC)

### Product Type:
Multi-pay CI plan with reset benefits (no cash value)

### Core Benefits:

**1. Base Plan Coverage**
- **150 conditions:** Early, intermediate, and major CI
- **Unlimited reset benefit:** Claim up to 5 times (5% of sum per claim)
- **Waiting period:** 1 year between claims
- **Death benefit:** 5% of sum insured

**2. Optional Enhancer Rider** (highly recommended)

**a) Catastrophic CI Benefit (200% payout)**
Applies to "Big 5" conditions when meeting catastrophic definitions:
- Cancer (stage 4 with distant metastasis)
- Stroke
- Heart attack
- Organ transplant
- Paralysis

**b) Ultimate Relapse Benefit**
- Additional 100% payout for recurrence of Big 5
- Capped at 50% per claim
- 2-year waiting period

### Sample Premium (30-year-old male, non-smoker):
- $100,000 coverage until 65: **$874/year**
  - Base: $741
  - Enhancer: $133
- Until 75: **$1,436/year** (43% increase due to higher risk period)

### Comparison: ASC (Absolute Critical Cover)
- Similar features but relapse benefit pays 100% (not 50%)
- Generally higher premium

## VI. PRO PROTECTOR (PLP) - Investment-Linked Policy

### Hybrid Nature:
Investment + Insurance combined in one product

### Key Features:

**1. Death Benefit Options**
- **PLUS:** Sum insured + policy value (separate benefits)
- **MAX:** Higher of sum insured OR cash value (more common)
  - As cash value grows, insurance cost decreases
  - At breakeven point, death benefit = investment value only

**2. Flexibility**
- Can increase coverage 2x at certain milestones
- Dollar cost averaging through fixed premium investment
- Choice of investment funds (client bears investment risk)

**3. Riders Available**
- Critical illness
- Early critical illness
- Disability income
- Premium waiver

### Sample Illustration (30-year-old male, non-smoker):
- $300,000 death/TPD/CI coverage
- Premium: ~$600/month
- At age 54: Investment value exceeds sum insured (tipping point)
- At age 65 (8% return assumption): $817,000 cash value
- ROI: 3.25x premiums paid ($252,000 → $817,000)

**Important:** Hold to optimal age (typically 55-65) for best returns. Holding too long increases insurance costs.

## XIII. KEY SALES CONCEPTS & TECHNIQUES

### 1. Budget Reality Principle

**Critical Insight:** Clients can typically afford **DOUBLE** their stated budget without discomfort.

**Example:**
- Client states: "$500/month budget"
- Actual comfortable budget: ~$1,000/month
- If income $8,500, expenses $5,000 = $3,500 surplus
- Spending $1,000 still leaves $2,500 buffer

**Application:** Don't accept first budget - educate on value and show better coverage options.

### 2. Value Before Price Philosophy

**Always VALUE first, then PRICE**

Never lead with premium. Instead:
1. Build comprehensive benefit understanding
2. Show real-world scenarios
3. Demonstrate coverage gaps
4. Anchor price perception through estimation exercises
5. Then reveal actual premium

### 3. Multiplier Upsizing Strategy

**Technique:** Present 3x multiplier GPP first, then show 2x comparison

**Script:** "Mr. Client, by adjusting to 2x multiplier and paying just $1,600 more per year, your cash value increases by $75,000 at age 70, and your coverage drop-off is much less severe. Would this additional value be worth the investment?"

**Result:** Natural upsizing without being pushy

### 4. Term Insurance Value Proposition

**For death coverage discussion:**

"Mr. Jason, I understand $1 million seems like a lot right now. But consider two things:

1. **Future insurability:** As your income grows from $100K to $150K-200K, you should be insured MORE. We're locking in your ability to have this coverage while you're young and healthy.

2. **Inflation reality:** A million dollars today won't be worth the same in 20-30 years. We're actually being conservative.

Plus, at just $882/year - less than an Anytime Fitness membership - can you really afford NOT to protect your family's financial security until retirement?"

### 5. Product Selection Framework

**Think: Medicine for Patients**

- No product is "bad" - only misapplied
- Different clients need different "prescriptions"
- Financial advisor = Financial doctor
- Understand client situation deeply before recommending

**Client Signals:**
- "I want money back" → GPP, PLP (cash value products)
- "Just protect my family" → SFT (pure protection)
- "Worried about cancer relapse" → UCC (multi-pay)
- "I like control/investments" → PLP (ILP)

## XIV. OBJECTION HANDLING SCRIPTS

### Objection: "Term insurance - if nothing happens I get nothing back!"

**Response:** "That's actually the point, Mr. Client. Think of it like car insurance - you don't buy it hoping to crash your car! You buy it for peace of mind. 

For just $882/year, you're transferring a $1 million risk to AIA. If something happens, your family receives enough to maintain their lifestyle for 10 years. If nothing happens, that means you've lived a healthy life to retirement - isn't that actually the best outcome?

Plus, consider this: would you rather pay $882/year for $1M coverage, or $7,000/year for $200K coverage with some cash back? Which actually protects your family better?"

### Objection: "Why do I need so much coverage?"

**Response:** "Let me explain the math. You earn $100,000/year. If something happens to you tomorrow:

- Your family loses: $100K/year × 10 years = $1 million
- Plus your housing loan: $400,000
- Total financial impact: $1.4 million

The coverage isn't 'high' - it's realistic. We're not insuring your life's value (priceless), we're replacing your financial contribution so your family doesn't have to sell the house, pull kids from school, or drastically downgrade their lifestyle.

Insurance isn't about you - it's about the people who depend on you."

### Objection: "I'm young and healthy, I don't need this now"

**Response:** "Actually, being young and healthy is EXACTLY why you need this now. Three reasons:

1. **Premiums are lowest** at your age. Every year you wait, premiums increase permanently.

2. **You're insurable now.** Next year, if you develop diabetes or high blood pressure, you might face exclusions or be uninsurable. You can't buy insurance AFTER you need it.

3. **Life doesn't wait.** Cancer, accidents - they don't check your age first. One in four will get cancer by 75. The question isn't IF you need protection, it's WHEN is the best time to secure it.

And the answer is: while you can."
