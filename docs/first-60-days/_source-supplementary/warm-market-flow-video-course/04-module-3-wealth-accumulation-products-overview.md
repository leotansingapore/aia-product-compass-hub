## Part 1: Retirement Needs Calculation & CPF Life

### Financial Calculator Fundamentals (Time Value of Money)

**Key Parameters:**
- **PV (Present Value)**: Value of money today
- **FV (Future Value)**: Value of money in the future
- **i (Interest Rate)**: Growth rate/inflation rate
- **n (Period)**: Time duration in years
- **Compounding**: Annual, monthly, etc.

**Practical Example:**
- Client needs $3,000/month today for retirement
- Current age: 30 years old
- Retirement age: 60 years old
- Period: 30 years
- Inflation: 3% per year
- **Result**: $3,000 becomes $7,281 in 30 years (same purchasing power)

**Mobile App**: Download the financial calculator app from your app store for client presentations

### Retirement Planning Milestones

**Age 55:**
- First major milestone where CPF funds can be accessed
- Validation point for retirement planning
- CPF Ordinary Account (OA) and Special Account (SA) transfer to Retirement Account (RA)
- Special Account closes

**Age 65:**
- Official Singapore retirement age
- Zero active income expected
- CPF LIFE payouts begin
- Rely on annuities, CPF LIFE, or passive income

**Age 90:**
- Planning horizon (conservative estimate beyond 85-year average life expectancy)

### CPF LIFE Basics

**Account Structure:**
- **Before 55**: OA, SA, Medisave
- **At 55**: SA closes, funds transfer to RA
- **Age 55-65**: RA accumulates for 10 years
- **Age 65+**: Monthly payouts begin

**2025 CPF LIFE Plans:**

| Plan | Lump Sum Required | Monthly Payout (from age 65) |
|------|-------------------|------------------------------|
| Basic Retirement Sum (BRS) | $106,000 | $930 |
| Full Retirement Sum (FRS) | $213,000 | $1,707 |
| Enhanced Retirement Sum (ERS) | $426,000 | $3,300 |

**Key Insight**: Even ERS payout of $3,300/month (~$110/day) is often insufficient for comfortable retirement. This creates the need for additional retirement planning products.

## Part 3: Platinum Wealth Venture (PWV)

### Product Overview

**Key Features:**
- **Premium Payment Period**: 5 years only (shortest in market)
- **Holding Period**: 7 years minimum
- **Returns**: Can receive dividends quarterly (starting 3 months after inception)
- **100% Premium Allocation**: No premium allocation charges
- **Projected Dividend Rate**: 7.5% based on past performance

### Unique Selling Propositions

1. **Short commitment**: Only 5-year payment period vs. traditional 10-20 years
2. **Quick returns**: Dividends start in 3 months, not after 20-30 years
3. **Flexibility**: Choose to receive cash or reinvest dividends
4. **Bonus Units**: 3-5% bonus for premiums above $42,000/year

### Fee Structure

**Supplementary Charges (First 7 Years):**
- Years 1-7: 3.6% of regular premium policy value

**Investment Bonus (Years 8-11):**
- 2.5% of annual premium
- Example: $20,000 premium = $500/year bonus for 4 years = $2,000 total

**Performance Bonus (Year 8 onwards):**
- 0.4% of policy value annually
- Paid perpetually as long as policy is maintained
- Example: $120,000 policy value = $480/year

### Dividend Mechanics Example

**Scenario**: $20,000 annual premium, Global Adventurous Income Fund

1. **Units Purchased**: $20,000 ÷ $1.08 = 18,518 units
2. **Quarterly Dividend**: 1.9 cents per unit
3. **First Dividend Payment**: 18,518 × $0.019 = $351.84
4. **Frequency**: Every quarter (4 times per year)
5. **Annual Dividend**: ~$1,400

**Client Options:**
- **Pay Now**: Receive cash to spend
- **Reinvest**: Buy more units to compound growth

### Three Strategic Approaches

**1. Dividend Now**
- Receive all dividends as cash from day one
- Provides immediate passive income
- Lower final policy value

**2. Dividend Later**
- Reinvest all dividends until retirement age
- Switch to cash payout at age 65
- Higher accumulated value
- Larger retirement income stream

**3. Dividend Future**
- Invest in Elite Adventurous for capital growth (8-10% target)
- Fund switch to Global Adventurous Income at retirement
- Maximize growth phase, then generate income
- Best for younger clients with long time horizon

### Sample Illustration

**Client Profile**: 40 years old, planning to retire at 65
- **Annual Premium**: $30,000
- **Payment Period**: 5 years
- **Total Premiums**: $150,000
- **Strategy**: Reinvest dividends, assume 8% growth
- **Value at Age 65**: $585,900
- **Monthly Retirement Income** (at 7% withdrawal): $3,410
- **Combined with CPF LIFE FRS** ($1,707): Total $5,117/month

### Positioning Against Fixed Deposits

With declining FD rates, PWV appeals to:
- Elderly clients seeking better returns on cash
- Investors looking for stable dividend income
- Those wanting growth + income flexibility

### Minimum Premium

- **$18,000 per year** minimum

## Part 4: Platinum Retirement Elite (PRE)

### Product Concept

**Purpose**: Dedicated retirement income plan with guaranteed payout structure

**Key Differentiators:**
- Can start as **single premium** or 5-year payment
- Choose specific **retirement age** (50, 55, 60, 65, 70)
- Set **target monthly retirement income**
- Select **payout period** (10, 15, 20 years, or to age 100)
- **Step-up income option**: Increase 5% annually to counter inflation

### How It Works

**Pre-Retirement Phase:**
- Invest in Elite Adventurous/Balanced/Conservative
- Funds grow at assumed 8% annually
- No withdrawals during accumulation

**Post-Retirement Phase:**
- Can switch to more conservative fund (Balanced/Conservative)
- Systematic drawdown begins
- Funds deplete to zero at end of chosen period
- **Fund continues earning 8%** even during drawdown phase

### Drawdown Mechanism

**Mathematical Model Example:**
- Age 65, accumulated value: $549,624
- 8% annual return = $43,970/year
- Withdrawal: $57,276/year ($4,773/month)
- Net depletion: $13,306/year
- **Duration**: Lasts 15 years (to age 80)

**Capital Preservation vs. Depletion:**
- 4% withdrawal rate = capital preservation
- Higher withdrawal = capital depletion over time
- PRE uses depletion model to maximize income

### Sample Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Five-Pay Premium**
- Age 40, retire at 65 (25 years growth)
- Annual premium: $30,000 × 5 years = $150,000
- Value at 65: $549,624
- 15-year payout: $4,773/month
- Total received: $878,940 (5.9× return)

**Scenario 2: Single Premium**
- Current age: 55, retire at 65 (10 years growth)
- Single premium: $120,000
- 15-year payout period
- Monthly income: $4,000
- Total received: $720,000 (6× return)

**Scenario 3: Step-Up Income**
- Same $30,000 × 5 years
- Choose 5% annual increase
- Starting income: $3,500/month
- Income grows annually: $3,675 → $3,859 → $4,052...
- Protects against inflation

### Comparing Flat vs. Step-Up

| Feature | Flat Income | Step-Up (5%/year) |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| Initial monthly payout | $4,773 | $3,500 |
| Year 10 payout | $4,773 | $5,525 |
| Total premium required | Lower | Higher |
| Best for | Immediate needs | Long retirement |

### PRE for Children's Education

**Strategy**: Start young, withdraw for university

- Child age 1
- Premium: $6,000 × 5 years = $30,000
- Grow at 8% for 20 years
- Value at age 21: $85,400
- **Use for**: University tuition fees

**Alternative**: If child gets scholarship
- Continue compounding to age 65
- Becomes retirement plan: $8,000/month income

### Single Premium Advantage

**Lower Fees**: 
- Single premium charges: 5%
- Five-year premium charges: 30%, 25%, 15%, 10%, 5%

**Example Comparison:**
- Five-year $30k premium = $150k total → charges higher
- Single premium $120k → achieves same outcome, lower fees

### When to Recommend PRE

**Ideal Clients:**
- Age 30-55 (not for very young clients)
- Have lump sum cash available
- Want guaranteed retirement income structure
- Prefer systematic planning over flexible products
- Seeking SRS tax benefits (single premium works well)

### Paycheck vs. Playcheck Approach

**Paycheck (Foundation):**
- CPF LIFE (FRS): $1,707/month
- Stable, guaranteed by government

**Playcheck (Enhancement):**
- PRE: $4,773/month
- PWV: $3,400/month
- Makes retirement enjoyable, not just survivable

**Total Strategy:**
- Required: $6,000/month at retirement
- CPF provides: $1,707
- **Gap to fill**: $4,293 → Use PRE or PWV

## Part 5: Platinum Wealth Elite (PWE)

### Product Positioning

**Hybrid Product**: Term insurance + Investment savings
- **Death Benefit**: Higher of sum assured OR policy value
- **Coverage**: Death, Terminal Illness, Total Permanent Disability
- **Cash Value**: Grows through investment returns
- **Flexibility**: Premium top-ups and withdrawals allowed

### Key Concept: Sum at Risk

**How Death Benefit Works:**
- Sum Assured: $500,000
- Policy Value after 10 years: $600,000
- **Death benefit payout**: $600,000 (higher of two)
- **Sum at risk**: $0 (because policy value exceeds sum assured)

**Implication:**
- As investment grows, mortality charges decrease
- Eventually, minimal insurance cost (self-insured by cash value)
- Creates "term insurance with returns" scenario

### Fee Structure

**Premium Charges:**
- Single premium: 5%
- Regular premium:
  - Year 1: 30%
  - Year 2: 25%
  - Year 3: 15%
  - Year 4: 10%
  - Year 5: 5%

**Administration Charges**: Apply monthly

### Leverage Factor - Critical Selling Point

**Example Calculation:**
- Premium: $12,000/year × 10 years = $120,000
- Sum Assured: $1,000,000
- **Leverage**: 8.3× ($1M ÷ $120k)

**Compare to Term Insurance:**
- 40-year-old, 30-year term for $1M
- Annual premium: ~$1,967
- Total paid: $59,010
- At end: **Get nothing back**

**PWE Alternative:**
- Annual premium: $12,000 × 10 years = $120,000
- At age 70 (30 years later):
  - Still covered: $1,000,000
  - Investment value: $339,000
  - **Can withdraw** $339,000 for retirement
- **Outcome**: Double the cost, but get 2× premium back + coverage

### Underwriting Classes

**Standard Rate:**
- Simple health declaration (yes/no questions)
- No medical examination
- Higher premium

**Preferred Plus Rate:**
- Requires medical check-up
- Must meet health criteria:
  - BMI < 28
  - Normal cholesterol levels
  - Normal blood pressure
  - No cardiovascular disease history
- **Discount**: Up to 11% lower premium

**Example Savings:**
- Standard: $80,000
- Preferred Plus: $69,000
- **Savings**: $11,000

### When to Offer Medical Underwriting

**Good Candidates:**
- Young, healthy, fit clients
- Non-smokers
- Normal weight range
- Confident about health metrics

**Avoid For:**
- Clients with known health issues
- Smokers
- Significantly overweight
- Those who prefer simplicity over savings

### Strategic Uses

**1. Mortgage Protection**
- Match sum assured to mortgage amount
- If death occurs: Mortgage paid off
- If survive: Cash value for retirement

**2. Legacy Planning**
- Ensure wealth transfer to beneficiaries
- Cash value grows tax-advantaged
- Death benefit provides liquidity

**3. Policy Conversion**
- Old whole life policies with no critical illness
- Convert cash value to PWE
- Increase sum assured
- Add modern features

**4. Retirement Income**
- Age 40-70: Keep $1M coverage
- Age 70: Withdraw cash value (e.g., $339k)
- Convert to income stream
- No more dependents, use for self

### Sample Illustration

**Client**: 40-year-old male, non-smoker
- **Sum Assured**: $1,000,000
- **Premium**: $12,000/year × 10 years
- **Fund**: Elite Adventurous
- **Age 70 Results** (assuming 4% return - conservative):
  - Total premiums: $125,000
  - Investment value: $339,000
  - Sum assured: $1,000,000
- **Age 70 Results** (assuming 8% return - projected):
  - Investment value: $600,000+
  - Far exceeds sum assured
  - Mortality charges near zero

### Single Premium Option

**Advantages:**
- Lower total cost
- Single 5% charge vs. declining charges over years
- Immediate high cash value
- Suitable for clients with lump sum

**Example:**
- Single premium: $80,000
- Sum Assured: $1,000,000
- **Leverage**: 12.5×
- Even better than 10-pay option

**Client Scenario:**
- Received $300k endowment payout
- Wants protection + returns
- Allocate $80k to PWE
- Immediate $1M coverage
- Remaining $220k for other investments
