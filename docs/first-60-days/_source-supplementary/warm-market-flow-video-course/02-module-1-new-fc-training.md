## **1. CAN-SALES TRACK (CST)**

### What is Can-Sales Track?
- **Definition**: A memorized, structured sales presentation for new consultants
- **Purpose**: Provides a systematic, non-invasive way to approach prospects professionally
- **Origin**: Traditional sales training method where presentations are practiced until natural

### Key Components

#### **The Three Core Questions**
1. **Why Plan?** - Establishing the need for financial planning
2. **What to Plan?** - Types of coverage and planning areas
3. **How Much to Plan?** - Budget allocation and coverage amounts

### The "Why Plan" Section

**Income Breakdown Example** (S$50,000 annual income):
- 10% Food
- 10% Transport
- 10% Parents
- 10% Miscellaneous
- 20% CPF (compulsory savings)
- **40% Remaining** (often spent)

**Two Types of Income Loss**:

1. **Temporary Risk**
   - Job loss, retrenchment, resignation
   - Solution: Emergency fund (3-6 months expenses)
   - Allocate 20% of income

2. **Permanent Risk**
   - Death, disability, critical illness, accidents
   - Example: $50,000 income × 20 years = $1,000,000 needed
   - Cannot easily borrow this amount

**Three Risk Management Methods**:
1. **Risk Avoidance** - Not practical (accidents can happen to anyone)
2. **Risk Retention** - Dangerous (insufficient personal savings)
3. **Risk Transfer** - **BEST METHOD** using insurance
   - Use 10% of income to protect 90% of income

### The "What to Plan" Section

**Three Planning Tiers**:

1. **Risk Management** (10% of income)
   - Life insurance
   - Accident coverage
   - Hospitalization

2. **Wealth Accumulation** (10-20% of income)
   - Savings goals
   - Retirement planning
   - Children's education
   - Endowment and investment plans

3. **Wealth Preservation** (10-50% of liquid assets)
   - Legacy plans
   - Multi-generation planning

### The "How Much to Plan" Section

**Coverage Rules of Thumb**:

| Coverage Type | Amount | Example (S$50K income) |
|--------------|---------|------------------------|
| Death/Disability | 10-20× income | $500K - $1M |
| Critical Illness | 5-10× income | $250K - $500K |
| Hospitalization | Based on preference | Private vs Government |
| Accident | 5-10× income | $250K - $500K |
| Medical Reimbursement | Comprehensive | Includes clinic visits, TCM |

**Important Notes**:
- Company insurance only covers 60 days hospitalization leave
- Hospital plans should continue after retirement
- AIA has 958 healthcare partners

## **4. PRESENTATION CONCEPT: Circle of Life (28,000 Days)**

### The Framework
**Total Life**: 80 years = approximately 28,000 days

### Four Quadrants (7,000 days each)

**Quadrant 1 (0-20 years)**: Learning Phase
- School, university, NS
- Dependent on parents
- **Economically Passive**

**Quadrant 2 (20-40 years)**: Building Phase
- Career development
- Start family
- Buy first car/property
- **Economically Active**

**Quadrant 3 (40-60 years)**: Peak Earning Phase
- Career stabilization
- High expenses (children, mortgage, aging parents)
- Possible second property/car
- **Economically Active**

**Quadrant 4 (60-80 years)**: Retirement Phase
- Slow down pace of life
- Enjoy life, travel
- **Economically Passive**

### Key Insight
**The Problem**: 
- Top half (Quadrants 1 & 4) = No income (economically passive)
- Bottom half (Quadrants 2 & 3) = Have income (economically active)
- People work hard for 40 years but fail to plan for the passive years

**Additional Risks**:
- Living longer than expected (120 years?)
- Becoming ill before 60
- Retrenchment
- Premature death

**Solution**: Financial planning fills the gap

## **5. FINANCIAL PLANNING OVERVIEW**

### The Four Essential Facts

To provide good financial advice, you need:
1. **Income** - Salary, bonuses, other sources
2. **Expenses** - Monthly breakdown
3. **Assets** - Property, shares, fixed deposits, CPF
4. **Liabilities** - Loans, mortgages, debts

### Soft Facts (Goals)
- Retirement age and lifestyle
- Legacy wishes
- Coverage preferences
- Risk tolerance

### Budgeting Rules

#### The One-Third Rule
- **1/3 Short-term**: Bills, subscriptions, immediate expenses
- **1/3 Medium-term**: House, car, wedding, holidays
- **1/3 Long-term**: Wealth accumulation, risk management

### Singapore Financial Landscape
- **Inflation considerations**
- **Various investment instruments**
- **CPF integration**

### Financial Planning Pyramid
**Foundation**: Risk Management
- Accident coverage
- Hospitalization

**Middle Tier**: Accumulation
- **Passive investments**: Funds (where we help)
- **Active investments**: Stocks, forex, property (requires time, knowledge, expertise, capital)

**Top Tier**: Wealth Management & Legacy Planning

### Common Objection Handling
**Client**: "I do my own investments through moomoo/Tiger broker"

**Reality Check**: 
- Out of 10 clients who say this, only 0.5-1 actually follow through
- Most get busy with promotions, family, work
- They pay for courses but lack time to implement
- After 10 years, most have done nothing

**Your Role**: Help them get started with systematic, managed solutions

### Retirement Planning Basics
**Key Inputs**:
- Current age
- Retirement age
- Inflation rate
- Investment return
- Life expectancy (use 90 years for safety, though Singapore average is 83)
