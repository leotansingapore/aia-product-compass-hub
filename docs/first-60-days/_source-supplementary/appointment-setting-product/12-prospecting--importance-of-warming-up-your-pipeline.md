## Lecture Notes

Generic Vs Efficient Sales Process

Strangers rarely buy in the first or second meeting. Be constantly aiming to nurture, build trust and rapport even before they meet you for the sales appointment.

![](https://hgdbflprrficdoyxmdxe.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/knowledge-files/editor-images/1771987468502-g73kla4ilo6.png)![](https://hgdbflprrficdoyxmdxe.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/knowledge-files/editor-images/1771987487289-9t7qxq7j5ud.png)

**Generic sales processes:**

1.  Generic call, there are many ads out there
    
2.  Generic meeting without selling the appointment
    
3.  Constant reschedules as the lead won’t prioritize you
    
4.  Rushed sales meeting, try to build rapport within 20 minutes, but gets rejected even though that is already quite long
    

Result: constantly wondering, why am I working so hard, but no results?

**Efficient sales process**

1.  Add value, trust, credibility, familiarity, even before the meeting
    
2.  Nurtures every lead, because every lead will buy mentality
    
3.  Higher closing rate, better efficiency (e.g 80% closing rate, rather than 20%)
    
4.  Less wastage of leads
    
5.  Hot, warm, semi warm, cold (you need to warm up your cold the most)
