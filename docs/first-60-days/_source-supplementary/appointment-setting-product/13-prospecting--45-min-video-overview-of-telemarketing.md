### Importance of Telemarketing and Cold Calling

-   **Lead Generation Methods**:
    
    -   **Buying Leads**: Involves paying for leads through platforms like Facebook or Instagram. Effective but costly.
        
    -   **Telemarketing**: A cost-effective method that only requires time and effort, especially useful when starting a business.
        
-   **Starting with Telemarketing**:
    
    -   Cold calling is essential for generating appointments when starting out.
        
    -   **Referred Leads**: Over the years, referred leads have accumulated, but these are often cold and require nurturing.
        

### Managing Rejections

-   **Mentality Towards Rejections**:
    
    -   Rejections should be seen as steps closer to getting a "yes."
        
    -   Expect a high rejection rate (90-95%) and mentally prepare for it.
        

### Telemarketing Process

-   **Initial Steps**:
    
    -   **Using PayNow/PayLah**: To identify lead names using their phone numbers.
        
    -   **DNC Screening**: All leads must be screened through the Do Not Call (DNC) registry to avoid legal issues. This check should be refreshed every 90 days.
        
-   **Call Execution**:
    
    -   Focus on volume and efficiency. Aim for 35 dials per hour.
        
    -   After the call, follow up with a text message to nurture the lead.
        
    -   Keep the lead sheet clean by deleting "no" responses after a certain period.
        

### Script Usage and Customization

-   **Cold Calling Script**:
    
    -   Begin with a simple introduction and a brief about the financial literacy campaign.
        
    -   Customize the script according to personal style and effectiveness.
        
    -   Include qualifying questions to assess the lead's interest and situation.
        
-   **Follow-Up Text**:
    
    -   The follow-up text should reinforce the initial call and aim to set an appointment.
        
    -   Provide limited time options for meetings to simplify the scheduling process.
        

### Setting Appointments and Managing Leads

-   **Google Calendar**:
    
    -   Appointments should be set on Google Calendar using a standardized format (Location, Lead Name, Phone Number, and Your Name).
        
    -   Mark new leads with a specific color (Tangerine) and invite the supervisor to the appointment.
        

### End of Day (EOD) Reporting

-   **EOD Format**:
    
    -   Report the number of dials made, meaningful conversations, appointments set, and hours spent dialing.
        
    -   Track the dial rate to ensure efficiency.
        

### Miscellaneous Tips

-   **Overcoming Inertia**:
    
    -   Start calling without waiting for everything to be perfect. Practice and experience will improve results over time.
        
-   **Using Technology**:
    
    -   Tools like ChatGPT can assist in script writing and follow-up message creation.
        
-   **Lead Management**:
    
    -   Use a separate number for telemarketing to keep personal and work communications distinct.
        
    -   Archive inactive leads instead of deleting them outright to maintain a clean and manageable system.
        

### FAQs and Common Concerns

-   **Dealing with Suspicious Leads**:
    
    -   If a lead asks too many questions or seems skeptical, avoid providing too much detail and consider ending the conversation politely.
        
-   **Company Representation**:
    
    -   Do not mention the actual company name (AIA) to avoid potential complaints or negative repercussions.
        

### Action Steps

-   **Getting Started**:
    
    -   New numbers for calling should be obtained immediately.
        
    -   Begin calling as soon as possible, following the outlined SOPs and using the provided scripts.
