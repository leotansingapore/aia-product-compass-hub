## Lecture Notes

NASA ( New Agent Success Advantage ) is a company lead gen to help new advisors get a headstart in cold calling.

We aim to provide 80 to 100 leads per new Consultant over the period of 3 months. The cost of sales leads is between $5 - $10 per lead and this cost will be charged to the Direct Leader.
