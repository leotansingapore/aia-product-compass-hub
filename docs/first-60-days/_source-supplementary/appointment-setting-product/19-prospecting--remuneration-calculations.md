## 1\. Income Components

Your salary as an AIA advisor consists of two main components:

**First Year Commission (FYC)** — Commission earned directly from policy sales.

**Entrepreneur Partnership Scheme (EPS)** — An allowance scheme provided by AIA on a one-to-one matching basis. If you choose an EPS of $3,000/month, you must match it with $3,000/month in FYC production.

---

## 2\. Commission Rates by Product

**ProAchiever (ILP)**

-   Year 1: 35% of APA
    
-   Year 2: 15%
    
-   Year 3: 8%
    
-   Years 4–5: 5% (recurring/rollover)
    
-   Year 6 onwards: 1.5% (perpetual, for the life of the policy)
    

**GPP (Life Policy)**

-   Year 1: 40%
    
-   Year 2: 20%
    
-   Year 3: 5%
    
-   Year 7 onwards: 1.5%
    

**Personal Accident (PA) Plans — Solitaire Comps**

-   30% perpetual commission — this never decreases
    
-   PA commissions stack year on year (Year 1 commissions carry into Year 2, then Year 3, etc.), creating a compounding income stream unlike other products

## 4\. Activity Needed to Hit $3,000/Month FYC

-   Standard case size: $2,400/month APA
    
-   Average commission rate: 35%
    
-   Target: ~12 appointments per month (3 opening appointments per week)
    
-   Closing ratio: ~30%
    
-   Expected FYC: ~$2,500–$3,000/month
    

**Reverse engineer your pipeline:** From the number of closing appointments needed, work backwards to determine how many calls, contacts, and prospecting activities are required. Getting the pipeline up is critical.

---

## 5\. Income Trajectory Over Time

**Years 1–2:** EPS allowance of ~$36K/year from AIA supports your income.

**Year 3:** EPS allowance drops off — expect a temporary dip in income.

**Year 4 onwards:** Income begins rising again due to stacking renewals, even with the same level of effort.

The key mechanism: **Renewals stack.** By Year 5, you're receiving renewal commissions from Years 1, 2, 3, and 4 simultaneously — on top of your current year's FYC.

## 6\. Career Benefit (Unique to AIA)

AIA provides a **Career Benefit** multiplier applied to your total renewal commissions. This is a feature most competitors do not offer.

**How it works:** Take your total renewals (from Year 2 to Year 6+), multiply by the career benefit percentage — that becomes additional passive income on top of your renewals. This effectively almost doubles your renewal income over time.

**Two pillars of passive income:**

1.  Renewals themselves (which grow every year)
    
2.  Career Benefit (which multiplies those renewals)

## 7\. The True Value of a Single Case

Don't be discouraged by seemingly small monthly commissions. A $2,400/month APA case generates far more than the 35% first-year commission suggests:

**Total commission calculation (with bonuses & career benefit):**

-   Year 1 FYC: 35% × 1.42 (bonus boost) = ~49.7%
    
-   Year 2 renewal: 15% (boosted by career benefit)
    
-   Year 3: 8% (boosted)
    
-   Years 4–5: 5% each (boosted)
    
-   Year 6+: 1.5% perpetual
    

**Renewals from Year 2 onwards are boosted by ~80% via career benefit, and first-year income is boosted by ~42% via bonuses.**

Total effective commission over the life of the policy ≈ **111% of APA**.

**Example:** A $2,400/month APA case is actually worth approximately **$2,683/month** in total commissions over 6 years. A $10,000 APA case? Roughly ~$11,000 in total commissions.

---

## 8\. Key Takeaways

-   **Think long-term.** A large portion of your income is "back-paid" in Years 2–6. The first-month commission is just the beginning.
    
-   **Don't be disheartened** by heavy prospecting for seemingly small immediate returns — the compounding effect of renewals, career benefit, and PA stacking is substantial.
    
-   **Consistency matters.** The same level of effort every month and every year leads to rising income due to the renewal stacking mechanism.
    
-   **This career rewards longevity.** The longer you stay, the more career benefit multiplies your passive income — this is by design to reward loyalty to AIA.
