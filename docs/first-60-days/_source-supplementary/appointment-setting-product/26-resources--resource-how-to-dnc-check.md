## Overview

Before contacting leads, you are required to screen them against Singapore's DNC (Do Not Call) Registry. This process filters out numbers that are registered on the DNC list, ensuring you only call leads who are legally contactable.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Prepare Your Lead List

-   Copy your entire lead list.
    
-   Open a new spreadsheet (e.g., Google Sheets or Excel).
    
-   Paste the leads into the spreadsheet.
    
-   Save/download the file as a **CSV file**.
    

**Important:** Make sure the file name has **no spaces or special characters** — the DNC system will reject files with special characters in the name. Use a simple file name like `leads.csv`.

---

### Step 2: Upload to the DNC Registry

-   Log in to the **DNC Registry portal** (TNC/DNC system).
    
-   Navigate to: **Check Registry → Bulk Filtering**
    
-   Click the **File** button and select the CSV file you just saved.
    
-   Click **Proceed** (ensure you have enough credits for the screening).
    

---

### Step 3: Wait for Results

-   The system takes approximately **5–10 minutes** to process.
    
-   Once processing is complete, the results will be available for download.
    

---

### Step 4: Download & Interpret Results

-   Click to **download** the results file.
    
-   The file will show a status next to each number:

## Key Reminders

-   Always screen your leads **before** making any outreach calls.
    
-   Only contact leads marked **NR** (Not on Registry).
    
-   Calling someone on the DNC list is a regulatory violation and can result in penalties.
    
-   Keep your CSV file names clean — no spaces, no special characters.
    
-   Ensure you have sufficient credits in the DNC portal before uploading.
