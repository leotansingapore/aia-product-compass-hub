## Lecture Notes

Before starting telemarketing, ensure that you have a new business phone number, and do use this HP number to call.  
  
You can buy one from [Simba](https://simba.sg/home "https://simba.sg/home") at just $10/month.

Please ensure that you also have a business whatsapp number. Try not to have your personal number and your business number in the same whatsapp. This is to ensure that your leads are "all in one place" this makes it easier for you to follow up.

Do also start to "warm up" your whatsapp number, by using your own phone to text your own number.
