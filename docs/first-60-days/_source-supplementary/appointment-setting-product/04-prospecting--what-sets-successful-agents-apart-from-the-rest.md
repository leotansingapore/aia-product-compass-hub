## Lecture Notes

*“Approach Each customer with the idea of helping him or her solve a problem or achieve a goal, not selling a product or service”*

~ Brian Tracy

The art of prospecting is the key factor that differentiates successful agents from those who struggle. Most of us are not fortunate enough to have strong connections or wealthy parents to fall back on. Relying solely on a warm market will only take you so far, as it eventually runs dry. Chances are, you don’t have a large pool of clients ready to buy—those who are ready have likely already made their purchases.
