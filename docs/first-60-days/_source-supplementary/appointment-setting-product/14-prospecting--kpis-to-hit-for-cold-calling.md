## Lecture Notes

Its all about the numbers game.

**Dials per hour:** 40-50 dials per hour is a good rate. do aim for that. This also includes calls which didn't pick up.

**Pick up rate**: usually about 20% pick up per hour. Do try to use two phones at the same time to improve on this :)  
  
**Appointment Set rate:** usually, its about 5% of the people that you call that will be interested.

**Show up rate:** usually around 1 in 3, to be pessimistic. So, after setting the appointment, not everyone will actually show up for the appointment. So, your messaging process has to be prompt, try to follow up and confirm the meeting at least multiple times before the appointment.

**Closing Rates:** around 1 in 3 people will buy from you.

**Activity ratio:** 1 case closed = 3 Appointments = 9 Appointments Set = 180 pick ups = 900 dials

Assuming you dial 50 numbers per hour, that's about 18 hours of calling. So do try to call at least **3 hours per day, with a dial rate of 50 numbers per hour.**

If you use two phones, that will bring the number of hours down :) I used to do that primarily.  
  
So you might ask, what if one person picks up first? Then, just put down the other number, as simple as that.
