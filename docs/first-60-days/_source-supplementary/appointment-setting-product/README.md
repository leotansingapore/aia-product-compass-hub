# Appointment Setting Product Module (Supplementary)

**Source product:** `/product/appointment-setting` (aia-product-compass-hub)
**Sections:** 28 of 28 (no video URLs — this module is text-essays, not transcripts)

This is the standalone "Appointment Setting" academy module — the canonical text-essay collection that frames prospecting mindset, cold-call mechanics, and pipeline activity standards. It complements (does not duplicate) the Warm Market Flow video course already in `_source-supplementary/`.

**Why it lives here:** First 60 Days FCs aren't licensed yet (no CMFAS/M9/HI/RES5), so the *practice* path is warm-market rehearsal of the canned CSTs. However, the **mindset** essays in this module (30-day rule, loving rejections, why salespeople slump, the 5 essential daily activities, the 3 key activities in a sales cycle) apply directly to first-60-days reps. The **cold-call mechanics** essays apply once the FC is licensed (Next 60 Days Day 22 onward).

## Highest-leverage sections

1. **Section 7 — Important Levers in Raising Your Sales Outcomes** (27 KB). The single longest essay. Diagnostic framework for sales output: activity volume × conversion rate × case size × frequency. Use when an FC's numbers are stuck and you need a forcing function.
2. **Section 27 — Touch Points** (7.5 KB). The full client-touch cadence — what to do after a sale, before the next review, in birthday/policy-anniversary moments. Strong service-delivery content.
3. **Section 20 — Cold Calling Training** (7.2 KB). The cold-call script + objection structure. Applies in *next-60-days* (post-licensing), not first-60-days practice.
4. **Section 19 — Remuneration Calculations** (5.9 KB). The case-size × commission × persistency math new FCs need to model their income.
5. **Section 11 — Loving Rejections** (4.7 KB). Mindset essay on rejection as the pricing of activity — pairs with the 100+ rejections/sale ratio in [[../../project_next_60_days_ratios|the Next 60 Days ratios memory]].
6. **Section 16 — Identifying Bottlenecks in Your Pipeline** (4.9 KB). Diagnostic — where in the funnel are you actually losing prospects? Useful from Week 5 onward.
