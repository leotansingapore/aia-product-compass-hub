## AIA - The ABCD Four Promises – Pre-FHR Opening

# The ABCD Four Promises

> *To be delivered before starting the Financial Health Review (FHR)*

---

## A — Assurance / Promise

This meeting is not to sell you anything. I am here to find out more about you and your goals. If we should proceed with proposing any solution, it is because I had shared something that is of value to you and that you felt comfortable with me.

---

## B — In Your Best Interest

In the event that I am able to proceed with the proposal stage, you can have my promise that what I plan for you will be exactly what I will do for myself if I'm in your shoes.

---

## C — Confidentiality Clause

I like to assure you that whatever we are going to share today will be kept in strictest confidence.

---

## D — Full Disclosure

With that in mind, I hope that you can provide me with as much information as you can, so I can make my best recommendation to meet your needs.
