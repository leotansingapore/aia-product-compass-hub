## AIA - Full Call Script (Opening → Objections)

# Market Survey Call Script

> **Minimum 30 to be completed before Application Phase**

---

## Opening

Hello, good morning, is this John?

John, this is.... Do you have a moment to speak?

How are you this period? *(Rapport Build)*

---

## Agenda

As you may have learnt, I am *"intending to embark on the career of a Financial Advisor"* / *"currently interning with a Financial Services Agency"* and I am tasked to do a simple market survey with the people that I know. Would you be able to help me by just giving me 5 minutes of your precious time?

*(Proceed with Market Survey Q1, Q2, Q3)*

---

## Setting the Appointment *(Using Question 4)*

Thanks XXX. By the way, *"when I am officially licensed to start my practice"* / *"let's say if I decide to embark on the practice to become a Financial Advisor"*, one of my priorities is to extend my service to my closest friends. You are one of those whom I highly regard.

Till then, may I get in touch with you for a 30-minute session to share with you on the type of solutions I can offer? You have my assurance that during our meeting, I will not ask you to do anything you don't want to and any future meetings will depend entirely on whether you feel my services would be of help to you. Is that fair?

Good. Would it be best to get together at your office or your home or through Zoom? That will be great! Would weekdays or weekends be better for you? Would office hours or evenings be better for you? Could I see you on XXXday at XX, or would XXXday at XX be better?

---

## Closing Off

Really appreciate your help. Will it be alright if I can keep you posted of any AIA promotions that is relevant to you? May I have your email address and date of birth so I can connect better with you?

*(Optional: Last but not least, may I have your permission to add you on Instagram or FB so we can keep each other updated on our happenings?)*

---

## Objection Handling

**"Not Interested"**
> Mr...., I can understand you're not being interested in something you haven't had an opportunity to see but, so that you can judge for yourself, would you be free for a short time on...

**"Not in the Market"**
> Mr...., I would have been surprised if you had said you were in the market for life insurance right now. However, I do have some ideas that will be handy for you when you're ready to go ahead. Would you be free...

**"No Money"**
> Mr...., I can understand you trying to hold down unnecessary expenses. However, I do think you'd find what I have to say of interest and, of course, you'd be under no obligation. Would you be free....

**"No Need"**
> Of course, Mr..., you'd be the sole judge of whether this particular idea would be of value to you. Since it will take only a short time for me to explain it to you, would you be free...

**"Too Busy"**
> Mr...., I guessed that you'd be busy and that's why I telephoned for an appointment rather than call on the off-chance of seeing you. Would you be free...

**"Wasting Your Time"**
> Mr...., on the chance that you'll see value in the idea, I'd be happy to spend the time with you. Would you be free for a short time on...

**"What's the Idea?"**
> Would you be free... Mr...., to explain it properly, I will need to show you some illustrations and discuss them personally with you.

**"Is it Insurance?"**
> It's the protection of your family / a savings plan with difference etc. that I would like to talk to you about. With some illustrations that I'm sure you'll find interesting, I can explain it to you in a brief interview. Would you be free...

**"Post it Out"**
> I'd be happy to do that Mr..., but what I have in mind will be useful only if it's tailored to your individual needs. That's why I'd like to see you in person. Would you be free...

**"For the Obstinate Objector"**
> Well, Mr..., could we leave it this way: I'd like to meet you and if I'm in your neighbourhood during the next few months, I'd like to drop in and say "hullo". If you have a few moments at that time, I'll be glad to show you what I have in mind.
