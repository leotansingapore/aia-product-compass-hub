# Warm Outreach – Soft Approach
**Best for:** Friends, community members, church mates, social circle — people you know but have not spoken to professionally

---

## The Script

Hi [Name]! Hope all is well on your end. :)

I just thought of asking — are you currently working with any financial consultant for your planning needs?

If not, or if you are open to exploring, I would love to see if we can work together. I have been working with some of our [community/group] friends for years now. They have entrusted me with their financial planning and I am truly blessed to be part of their journey.

No pressure at all — I just thought of reaching out to see if you would be open for a quick sharing. :)

---

## Why This Works

- Starts with a question, not a pitch — far less intimidating
- Asking if they currently work with anyone is non-threatening and opens dialogue naturally
- Social proof via community builds credibility through shared identity
- No pressure at all explicitly removes the awkwardness of being sold to
- Ends with a light, open invitation — not a close

---

## Key Principles

- **Lead with a question** — let them volunteer their current situation
- **Social proof from shared community** — mention mutual friends or group members (with permission)
- **Never assume they need help** — frame it as exploration
- **One ask only** — just ask if they are open, do not jump to scheduling in message 1

---

## Tips from Jana

> For warm market contacts — especially community or church friends — I never lead with a product. I lead with curiosity about their situation. The moment they say they do not have anyone yet or they are open to exploring, that is your green light.
