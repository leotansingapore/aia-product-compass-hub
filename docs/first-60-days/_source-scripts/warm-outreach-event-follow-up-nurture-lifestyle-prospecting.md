## Jana (Auctus) - Event Follow-Up — Missed the Event (Jana)

# Event Follow-Up – Missed the Event
**Best for:** Contacts you invited to a lifestyle event (fitness class, lunch, gathering) who could not make it

---

## The Script

Hi [Name]! Hope you are feeling much better now!

I am sorry you could not make it to the [event name] — we missed you there!

I actually had a personalised door gift prepared for you, so I would still love to pass it to you. Let me know when it is convenient and I can meet you at [location]!

Also, while we catch up, I can briefly share about a new [product/fund] that was recently launched — thought it might be something you would find interesting as well. Let me know if this week or next works better for you. :)

---

## Why This Works

- The door gift is a brilliant non-salesy reason to meet in person — creates genuine goodwill
- Briefly share softens the business pitch — it is secondary to the catch-up
- Giving two time options makes it easy to say yes
- The entire message feels like care, not selling

---

## Key Principles

- **Events create warmth** — always follow up within 48 hours
- **The gift creates reciprocity** — it opens doors without being transactional
- **Business is secondary** — lead with the relationship and the gift, not the product

---

## Jana Prospecting System

> I attend one social event per month to meet new people. I also run lifestyle events like fitness classes that allow natural, non-transactional connections. The door gift strategy is key — it gives me a genuine reason to follow up with people who could not make it.

**Weekly schedule:**
- Monday and Friday: Dedicated prospecting blocks
- Daily: Small consistent outreach habit
- One Saturday per month: Social events or gatherings
- Quarterly: COI (Centre of Influence) meet-ups

## Jana (Auctus) - Nurture Follow-Up — Long-Lost Contact (Jana)

# Nurture Follow-Up – Reconnecting with Old Contacts
**Best for:** Contacts you have not spoken to in a while who reach out or resurface

---

## The Script

Hi [Name]! Happy New Year! It has been forever — hope you are well!

*(Wait for their reply. If they bring up finances or life goals, respond warmly:)*

Thank you for trusting me with this. That is actually a great mindset already — wanting to be wiser with your finances.

[Amount] is a good starting point, and we can definitely explore options that fit your goals and comfort level.

Would you be able to meet for a consultation sometime next week? Let me know which day works and we will take it from there. :)

---

## Why This Works

- Responding warmly to their initiative removes all pressure — you are just following their lead
- Starting point framing validates small amounts and makes it feel achievable, not embarrassing
- Asking about a specific day creates momentum without being pushy

---

## Key Principles

- **Do not pitch first** — let them bring up the topic
- **Validate any amount** — never make someone feel their budget is too small
- **Keep it light** — this is a consultation, not a close

---

## Tips from Jana

> The best leads often come from people who reach out to you. Stay warm and consistent with your network, and when they are ready, they will find you.
