## Texting EQ Guide

### The Ultimate Texting Rulebook
*From Texting EQ by Wilfred Wong*

**Tip 1:** Start the convo by linking it back to a previous conversation. Don't sound random.
> "Hey bro the other time you were saying you work in Tanjong Pagar right? Exactly where is your office?"

**Tip 2:** Create enough interest with every text. Every text gives the prospect MORE REASON to meet us.
> "I've been wanting to show you how I helped some of my clients to increase their investment returns with their existing portfolio."

**Tip 3:** End off every text with an easy-to-answer question.
> "Are you working from home or office?"

**Tip 4:** Make them feel SAFE for the upcoming consultation.
> "At the end of the day, sometimes I advise people not to invest at all if we see no need — when we meet we can discuss and you can decide for yourself."

**Tip 5:** Talk about common ground to ENCOURAGE the conversation.
> "Some of my clients are also doctors and I help them with their investments since they usually work long hours."

**Tip 6:** Actively FOLLOW UP. 90% of the time, people fail to reply. Just give them a bump by name.
> "Hi Sarah!"

**Tip 7:** Reply QUICKLY (immediately, or within 3-4 hours). It shows reliability and service.

**Tip 8:** Be CASUAL. Don't use complicated jargon.
> ❌ Don't use words like "Optimisation"
> ✅ Use emojis and "Hahaha" if it sounds like you!

**Tip 9:** Texting approaches work BEST after a few engagements/touchpoints beforehand. Touchpoints warm up the prospect.

**Tip 10:** Never use the word FREE. Instead:
> "When is a better time for a 30 mins call? Would next Thursday or Friday be better?"

**Tip 11:** Minimise the prospect's thinking when setting CTA.
> ❌ "When are you available next month?"
> ✅ "For next month I have 10th Jan 6pm and 23rd Jan 8pm, which is better for you?"
