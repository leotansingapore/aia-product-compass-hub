# Warm Market — Conversation Openers (by Life Stage)

**Category:** `cold-calling`  
**Audience:** `warm-market`  
**Role:** `consultant`  
**Tags:** texting-eq, openers, warm-market, life-stage  

## Investment / Returns Angle

### For friends who might be interested in investing:

> "Hmmm actually I want to show you how I've been doing investment planning for our [mutual group] friends especially now when the markets are CRAZY 😂 if we do the right things right, we can potentially earn good money over the next few years
>
> BUTTTT like that you say sometimes I also tell some people not to invest at all because they're not financially ready
>
> Catch you for 20 mins then you decide for yourself?? HEHEH"

---

**Why it works:**
- References mutual friends (common ground)
- Shows excitement without being pushy
- Safety valve: "sometimes I tell people NOT to invest"
- Short time commitment: "20 mins"
- Gives them authority to decide

---

## BTO / Couples Angle

### For friends who are getting a BTO or getting married:

> "Are ya'll gonna BTO?"
> → leads to BTO planning and budgeting conversation

> "Hey! Since you're getting married soon — have you guys started looking at how to budget for the flat + wedding + insurance together? A lot of my clients in the same stage found it super helpful to map it all out."

---

**Why it works:**
- Ties into a real life event they're already thinking about
- Positions you as helpful, not salesy
- Natural lead-in to financial planning conversation

---

## Fresh Graduate Angle

### For friends who just graduated:

> "You graduated already?"
> → "Have you finished your investment and insurance planning?"
> → "Why not I show you what I do for my other clients!"

---

**Why it works:**
- Graduation is a natural transition point
- They're likely thinking about adulting & finances
- Low-pressure: "show you what I do" not "sell you something"

---

## Reprospecting / Second Attempt

### For friends who rejected you before:

> "You know a few years ago I reached out to you to talk about financial planning right?
>
> Actually I was a stupid noob back then who didn't know anything 😂
>
> So it's a good thing you rejected my request.
>
> BUT over the years especially these 2 years I've been planning for many couples to have a detailed plan towards financial freedom.
>
> After your wedding can I show you what I usually do for people? But no pressure."

---

**Why it works:**
- Self-deprecating humour disarms them
- Acknowledges they were right to say no before
- Shows growth and credibility
- "No pressure" = safety valve
