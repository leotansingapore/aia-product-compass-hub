# Warm Market — Handling Ghosting & Non-Replies

**Category:** `follow-up`  
**Audience:** `warm-market`  
**Role:** `consultant`  
**Tags:** texting-eq, follow-up, ghosting, warm-market  

## Playful Bump

### When they ghost you:

> "Eh bro.. Don't ignore me leh HAHA don't like FA just say…"

Usually they reply: "Paiseh bro HAHA damn busy with work"

Then: "Me TOO" → schedule for a few weeks/months later when both are more free.

---

**Key mindset:** Don't take it personally. They're busy, not rejecting you. Keep the tone light and humorous.

---

## Taking the Ball Back

### When they say "not now":

Prospect: "Not at the moment bro. Maybe when I am on long leave."

You: "Okay no worries! Check in with you around November!"

---

**Key principle:** Always take the ball back. Make sure the right to reschedule remains with YOU, not the prospect. Set a specific follow-up month.

---

## Gentle Re-Engage

### Following up with someone who forgot to reply:

> "Hehe let's catch up and review soon!! Next sat/sun we go for a meal?"

Then later:
> "OH YA — actually I just spent an hour studying your case just now 😂
> And I wanna show you something my clients were sharing with me
> Catch you before the month ends?"

---

**Why it works:**
- Opens with casual social catch-up (not financial planning)
- Shows you've invested time in THEIR case
- Creates curiosity without revealing everything
