# Week 6: Financial Fundamentals - Cheat Sheet

> **The one idea:** Every client conversation runs on four numbers - TVM, Rule of 72, CPF allocation, and real-vs-nominal return. Know them cold or you cannot recommend anything on top.

## Rule of 72 - the table to memorise

> **Years to double = 72 / interest rate.** Single most-used arithmetic in client meetings.

| Rate | Years to double | Anchor use case |
|---:|---:|---|
| 2% | 36 | General CPI - prices double every 36 years |
| 3% | 24 | Conservative inflation assumption |
| 4% | 18 | CPF SA / MA floor |
| 5% | 14.4 | Endowment territory |
| **6%** | **12** | **Balanced portfolio - the default** |
| 7% | 10.3 | Growth portfolio |
| 8% | 9 | Aggressive / education inflation |
| 9% | 8 | Equity ceiling for planning |
| 10% | 7.2 | Don't promise this |

**Worked drills (sub-10-second mental math):**
- 35yo with $50K CPF SA at 4% to age 65 -> 30 years / 18 yrs-per-double = 1.67 doublings -> ~$160K
- 8yo's $100K Australian degree at 8% education inflation in 13 years -> 1.44 doublings -> $272K
- $500K life cover at 3% inflation 30 yrs out -> 1.25 doublings -> buys what $210K buys today
- 5-year delay at 6% return -> 0.42 doublings lost -> ~25% less final capital

## CPF - the numbers worth memorising

| Number | What it is |
|---|---|
| **37%** | Total CPF rate, under-55 (17% employer + 20% employee) |
| **62 / 16 / 22** | Of that 37%, share to OA / SA / MA (under-35 band) |
| **~23%** | Monthly OA contribution as share of gross salary, under 35 |
| **$6,800** | Monthly Ordinary Wage ceiling (rises to $8,000 post-2026) |
| **$102,000** | Annual CPF cap (salary + bonus combined) |
| **$37,740** | Max CPF per year per job at the current cap |
| **2.5% / 4% / 4%** | OA / SA / MA floor rates |
| **+1% / +1%** | Extra on first $60K (under 55) / $30K (over 55) |
| **55 / 65** | RA created / CPF LIFE payouts begin |

**Three CPF LIFE plans:**
- **Basic** - lower payout, larger bequest
- **Standard** (default) - moderate payout, moderate bequest
- **Escalating** - starts lower, +2%/yr for life - the only inflation-hedged option

**Three Retirement Sums at 55:** BRS (~$106K) -> FRS (~$213K, ~2x BRS) -> ERS (~$426K, ~3x BRS). Payouts at 65: ~$930 / ~$1,730 / ~$3,300/month respectively. Payouts are non-linear - doubling the RA does not double the payout.

## Real vs nominal return

> **Real return ~ Nominal return - Inflation rate.** Exact: `(1 + nominal) / (1 + inflation) - 1`.

| Asset | Nominal | Real (vs 2% CPI) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bank account | 0.5% | **-1.5%** (silent destruction) |
| Fixed deposit | 2% | 0% |
| CPF OA | 2.5-3.5% | 0.5-1.5% |
| CPF SA / MA | 4% | 2% |
| Endowment | 3-4% | 1-2% |
| Balanced equity | 6-8% | 4-6% |

**Category inflation rates - do NOT use 2% across everything:**
- General CPI: 2% | Food: 2-3% | Housing: 2-4%
- **Healthcare: 4-6% | Education: 5-8% | Private hospital: 6-10%**

## The trade-off triangle - the scam detector

Safety + Return + Liquidity - pick any two, never three.

- High safety + High liquidity = Fixed deposits, CPF OA (low return)
- High safety + High return = Government bonds, CPF SA (low liquidity)
- High return + High liquidity = ETFs, equities (lower safety)
- **All three = scam or misrepresentation.** No exceptions.

## Investing vs speculation - the line clients miss

| | Investing | Speculation |
|---|---|---|
| Horizon | 5+ years | Days to months |
| Method | Fundamentals, diversification | Timing, momentum, hype |
| Expected outcome | Positive over decades | Most speculators lose |

**Client move:** separate the two pots explicitly. Cap "fun money" at 5% of portfolio.

## TVM live-meeting bar

5 variables: **n, i, PV, PMT, FV.** Mode: **BGN** for insurance. Sign convention: **OUT = negative, IN = positive.**

| Time gate | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **10 sec** to start entering values | Calculator open, fingers moving |
| **90 sec** total from question to client-friendly answer | The live-meeting bar |
| **60 sec** per Day 28 practice problem | Solo-drill bar |
| **30 sec** to re-run with new variable | The pushback test ("what if I save $1,000?") |

## 5 TVM error patterns - personal mitigation

| # | Error | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sign error (PMT +/-) | Sticky on meeting folder: OUT = -, IN = + |
| 2 | Mode mix-up (BGN vs END) | Default to BGN at start of every meeting |
| 3 | Period mismatch (yrs vs months) | If PMT is monthly, write `/12` next to `i` |
| 4 | Nominal vs real confusion | Say "let me adjust for inflation" out loud |
| 5 | Forgetting to inflate target | Run inflation step FIRST when goal is in today's $ |

## Common failure modes

- **Quoting today's dollars for a 30-year retirement plan.** $3K/mth today is ~$5,400/mth at 65 (2% inflation, 30y). Plan in future dollars or run out at year 15.
- **Telling clients "bank is safe."** Safe nominally, -1.5% real every year. $100K becomes ~$65K purchasing power in 30 years.
- **Using 2% inflation for education.** Education runs 5-8%. A $100K degree in 13 years at 8% is $272K, not $130K.
- **Pitching CPFIS to young adults.** Most underperform OA's 2.5% floor net of fees. Recommend SA top-up instead - you earn no commission, which is exactly why it builds trust.
- **Confusing nominal payout sizes between CPF LIFE plans.** Doubling RA does not double payout. Don't tell clients "BRS is better value."
- **Running TVM with eyes glued to the screen.** The 90-second bar is meaningless if the client cannot see your face. Drill the calculator until you can run it while watching them.

## The cost-of-waiting line (gentle, never guilt)

> "Honestly, waiting 5 years sounds reasonable when life feels full. Mathematically, at 6% it costs about 25% of your retirement capital - maybe $200K at 65. You can decide if the 5-year break is worth $200K to you. I want you to know the number so the decision is informed."

Then silent. Don't press.
