# Week 7: The Approach & Digital Prospecting - Cheat Sheet

> **The one idea:** Project 100 is the input; the 4-stage Social Media Journey is the lane; the 7-touch follow-up is the engine. Skip any of the three and the pipeline starves by Month 4.

## Project 100 - the conversion funnel

| Stage | Number | Note |
|---|---:|---|
| Names on list | 100 | Goldilocks - under 50 starves, over 200 unmanageable |
| Reached | 60-70 | Phone or message back |
| Market surveys completed | 30-40 | Agree to a conversation |
| Fact-finds | 20 | Real meeting booked |
| Closed clients | 8-15 | ~$1K FYC each, ~$25K CLV each |

**Priority quota:** <= 25 Hot, >= 40 Semi-Warm, rest Cold / Referred. Most new FCs underestimate their pool 5-10x. Scroll full WhatsApp + LinkedIn + Instagram contacts - anyone with shared memory belongs on the list.

**Maintenance:** add 5 names/week. Re-sort monthly. Reactivate "not now" quarterly. Archive closed to a separate list.

## The 3 warm-market rings - where the career actually lives

| Ring | Size | Truth |
|---|---:|---|
| **Hot** | 10-25 | Easiest to meet, burns fastest. Month 1-2 only. |
| **Semi-Warm** | 50+ | Drifted friends, ex-colleagues, classmates. **Where Year 1-5 income lives.** Lowest competition. |
| **Cold** | unlimited | Roadshows, ads, true strangers. Necessary later, brutal first. |

The friend you haven't spoken to in 7 years is more valuable than the cousin you message daily. The cousin says yes once. The drifted friend has untouched relationship equity and no other FC has reconnected with them.

## Warm market opener library - pick by relationship, not by template

| Opener | Use when |
|---|---|
| **Market survey** | Genuinely warm, would pick up your call |
| **Seeking help / "kena forced"** | Close friends who appreciate honesty over polish |
| **Policy summary angle** | Friend has multiple policies they don't track |
| **"First call" angle** | Distant friends - "rather it be me than a stranger" |
| **Privilege to serve** | Friends you respect, formal register |
| **Quietly here** | Very distant warm - haven't spoken in years |
| **2-Liner Hook** | Semi-warm, prefer text, resource as bridge |

**The pre-licence cheat code:** "I'm pre-licensed, so I literally can't sell you anything for the next few weeks. Part of training is doing market surveys with 20 people I trust. 30 minutes, no product talk - can I buy you coffee?" Window closes the day your license drops.

## 4-stage Social Media Journey

| Stage | Their question | Your job |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Skeptical Stranger** | "Is this person real?" | Clean profile, show up as human |
| **2. Curious Follower** | "Does this person know their stuff?" | Publish consistently - authority + personality |
| **3. Trusted Choice** | "Should I go to this person?" | Respond to DMs, engage genuinely |
| **4. Only Choice** | (no question - just action) | Protect the trust, keep serving |

Most new FCs try to jump 1 -> 4 via a direct pitch. It doesn't work. Walk every stage.

## 5 silent questions every cold prospect asks before DMing you

| # | The silent question | What answers it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Can I trust others value your work? | Testimonials in **pre / during / after** format |
| 2 | Do you actually know your stuff? | Real client problems, no jargon |
| 3 | Why are you doing this? | Your defining-moment story |
| 4 | Will we get along? | Personality, hobbies, quirks (filter, not leak) |
| 5 | How can I approach without feeling awkward? | Low-friction CTA - *"DM me 'plan'"* |

**Audit drill:** open last 10 posts. Score each question 0-2. Lowest score is next week's content fix.

## 7-touch follow-up after every first meeting

| # | Day | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 0 (within 2 hrs) | WhatsApp | Thank-you + recap |
| 2 | 2 | Email | Proposal / Financial Health Check |
| 3 | 4 | WhatsApp | "Any questions?" |
| 4 | 7 | Call/WA | Propose second meeting |
| 5 | 14 | WhatsApp | One relevant resource (no pitch) |
| 6 | 21 | Short call | Relationship-first check-in |
| 7 | 30 | WhatsApp | "Open door" - move to nurture |

**60%+ of conversions happen after touch 5.** Most new FCs quit at touch 2-3. The follow-up IS the sale.

## Touchpoint intake - 4 questions that buy you 4-10 years of cadence

In the first meeting, extract:

1. **"How old are you? When's your birthday?"** -> annual touch forever.
2. **"When's your POP?"** -> extrapolates enlistment + ORD + uni entry (NSF prospects).
3. **"When's your ORD?"** -> post-NS shift anchor.
4. **"When are you entering university?"** -> semesters + graduation.

**NSF durations to memorise:** BMT 2mo | SCS 6mo | OCS 9mo | ORD ~2y from enlistment | Uni starts Aug after ORD | Graduates ~May year 4.

**Working-adult swap:** work anniversary | policy-review window (11 months post-purchase) | life-event radar (engagement, kid, parent diagnosis, promotion, mortgage).

**Calendar SOP same day:** title format `Wish [message] - [name] - [number] - [your initials]`. All-day events. Birthdays recur yearly forever. Semester wishes recur yearly x 4. One-off milestones (POP, ORD) no repeat. Share to mentor.

## Weekly prospecting rhythm

| Activity | Daily | Weekly |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dials / touches | 20 | 100-140 |
| Appointments from dials | 1 | 5-7 |
| New names added to Project 100 | - | 5 |
| Engagement block (commenting on others) | 15 min | - |
| Content post (Sun ideation / Tue draft / Thu publish) | - | 1 |
| Project 100 review | - | Sunday evening |

End-to-end math: ~100 "not nows" -> 1 closed sale.

## Numbers worth memorising

- **149 rejections per close** at typical Y1 ratios. Treat "no" as the price tag of the close, not a verdict.
- **3-5x close rate** on referred leads vs cold. Borrowed trust closes 3x more.
- **5-10x reply rate** on a value-first DM vs a cold "I'm an FC, can we meet?"
- **30+ market surveys** is the floor before RNF clears. Below 30, tonality is still stiff.
- **2 hours/weekend** sets up profile + bio + cover on 3 platforms. One-off cost.
- **6 months** before content shows real DMs. Most new FCs quit at month 2.

## Common failure modes

- **Only working the Hot ring.** Exhausts by week 6, FC concludes "I've run out of people" without touching the Semi-Warm ring where the career lives.
- **Sending the "training reps" framing to senior contacts.** Some warm contacts read it as a chore. Use the "rather it be me than a stranger" angle instead.
- **Asking "do you have an agent?"** Answer is always yes. Ask *"when was the last time your agent actually sat down with you for a proper review?"* instead.
- **Building the list of 100 then not calling.** The list is not the problem - the calling is. Day 26's 10X Rule + Day 19's counting-rejections fix it.
- **Posting and ghosting.** Check within 2 hours, reply to all early comments. Otherwise engagement is cold.
- **Sending a calendar link in the first DM reply.** Converts at 1/5x the rate of "answer briefly + ask a genuine follow-up question." Earn the call.
- **Treating Project 100 as a one-time build.** It's a living document. Add 5 weekly, re-sort monthly, reactivate quarterly.
- **Closing a "no" with "ok, no worries."** Reads as door shut. Use the open-door close - name a future scenario explicitly.

## Taxi Cab Theory - the mental model behind nurture

Every prospect is a taxi. When they're not ready, the light is off. When life shifts - marriage, baby, job change, parent's diagnosis, bonus - the light flips on. **You cannot force the light on.** You can only be the one standing on the corner when it does.

> *"You're not trying to make them buy. You're trying to be the one on the corner when their light turns on."*

Write that on your monitor.
