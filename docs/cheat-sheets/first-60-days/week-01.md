# Week 1: Foundation & Identity Shift - Cheat Sheet

> **The one idea:** You don't rise to your goals - you fall to your systems. Week 1 is where the *operator* gets fixed before the machine (clients, products, scripts) is handed over.

## The four high-income skills this career compounds

A standard 9-5 builds one of these. This career builds all four on real people with real stakes - which is why income compounds for 20+ years instead of plateauing at 5.

| Skill | Where it pays in life |
|---|---|
| **Human nature** | Every relationship - personal, commercial, family |
| **Negotiation** | Deals, boundaries, salary, marriage |
| **Money** | Every financial decision for the next 60 years |
| **Sales** | Any moment you need someone to say yes |

**The 60-day arc:** Weeks 1-5 inner game (how you think) -> Weeks 6-7 fundamentals (financial knowledge) -> Weeks 8-10 client game (real prospects). You will not meet a real prospect until Week 8. Trust the sequence.

## Why financial planning matters - the frame to use in every meeting

**The one-third income rule.** Most people have one bucket (a bank account). A real plan has three.

| Third | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Short-term** | Today | Bills, food, transport, entertainment |
| **Mid-term** | 1-5 years | House, wedding, kids, car |
| **Long-term** | 10+ years | Retirement + risk management |

**The reframe to give clients:** instead of *"what's left for long-term after I pay for everything?"* -> *"once the long-term third is secured, what's left to spend?"* That single reorder is the whole game.

**Permanent vs temporary risks.** Emergency fund handles temporary (3-6 months income). Insurance + retirement plan handles permanent (death, disability, CI, hospitalisation, old age). Transfer what you cannot afford to retain.

**Coverage rules of thumb (memorise):**

| Risk | Multiple of annual income |
|---|---:|
| Death / TPD | 10x |
| Major CI | 5x |
| Early CI | 2x |
| Hospitalisation | Max private/restructured tier |

## The four assurances of the career

The pop-culture image of an FC is wrong. The real architecture pays in four currencies, all of which compound:

1. **Autonomy** - you set hours, ceiling, and client list. Year 1-2 is structured. Year 4+ you control the calendar.
2. **Personal development** - six capabilities: personal finance, influence, communication, moral compass, entrepreneurial spirit, discipline.
3. **Job security via passive income** - renewal commissions stack across hospitalisation, whole-life, ILP, term/CI. By Year 5+ of consistent production, **more than half of total income is true passive renewals**. AIA layers Career Benefit (~100% boost Y2-6) and Agent Provident Fund (~1.5% per month from Y7) on top.
4. **Job fulfillment** - the moment a CI claim keeps a child in school is what brings FCs back at Year 20.

**Financial freedom = passive income > monthly expenses.** Not a net-worth target. A cash-flow threshold.

## Growth mindset - the "not yet" reframe

Fixed-mindset responses contain *"I'm"* (an identity claim). Growth-mindset responses contain a *specific next action*.

| Fixed | Growth |
|---|---|
| "I'm not cut out for sales" | "Which objection did I fumble?" |
| "I'm not a finance person" | "Not yet. What do I need to read?" |
| "She has better connections" | "What's she doing differently?" |

**Track reps, not mood.** Dials made, appointments booked, meetings run - honest. Feelings lie.

**Doing-the-hard-thing principle.** The activities that feel worst in the short term produce the best results long-term. Cold-calling is unpleasant for everyone - that is *exactly why* the few who do it pull ahead.

## Purpose - the load-bearing wall

Three things keep you in the chair when Month 4 hits: discipline (short-term, runs out), financial pressure (collapses in bad months), and **purpose (compounds)**. Only one still works in Month 4.

**Why/How/What structure for your "why":**

| Layer | Question | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Why** | What change am I here to make? | "Most Singaporeans are under-equipped - I want to close that gap, one household at a time." |
| **How** | Through what methods? | "Honest education, long-term relationships, no-pitch first conversations." |
| **What** | Concrete products | "Insurance, retirement planning, investment guidance." |

**The pre-framing pitch (3 parts, ~60 sec):** your Why (1 sentence) + What you aspire to be (4 points) + What you're doing now (4 points). Owns the underdog status and converts it into trust.

**The moral compass test for every recommendation:** could you explain this to your client's children, 10 years from now, without flinching? If yes, sell it. If no, don't.

## Forming habits that compound - the four laws

| Law | Create good habit | Break bad habit |
|---|---|---|
| **Cue** | Make it obvious (call list on desk by 8:55am) | Make it invisible (phone in drawer) |
| **Craving** | Make it attractive (pair with coffee) | Make it unattractive (unfollow accounts) |
| **Response** | Make it easy (script open, numbers pre-filled) | Make it difficult (app timer lockout) |
| **Reward** | Make it satisfying (visible tracker) | Make it unsatisfying (tally time lost) |

**Identity-based beats behaviour-based.** *"I'm a disciplined FC"* survives a missed day; *"I'll make 10 calls today"* does not.

**The success formula:** **Activities x Skills x Knowledge = FYC.** If any factor is zero, the product is zero.

| Category | Minimum weekly rep |
|---|---|
| **Activities** | 5x weekly planning; 50+ outreach touches |
| **Skills** | 1x mentor role-play; 3+ real meetings |
| **Knowledge** | 3 hours: products, CPF, markets, objections |

## Numbers worth memorising

- **10x / 5x / 2x annual income** - Death/TPD, Major CI, Early CI coverage rules of thumb
- **1/3 - 1/3 - 1/3** - short-term, mid-term, long-term income split
- **3-6 months** - emergency fund target for temporary risks
- **1% better daily compounded over a year** - 37x improvement
- **10-70-20 rule** - top 10% self-sustain, middle 70% are made by coaching/habits, 20% leave inside 2 years
- **Year-5 honest comparison** - consistent FC is often 2-4x starting income; corporate peer is 10-20% up
- **~100% Career Benefit boost** to renewal income across Years 2-6 (the critical compounding window)

## Common failure modes

- **Evaluating the career on Year-1 income** - the trap that causes most quits. Year 5 is the honest comparison.
- **Reaching out to closest 3 friends first** - too easy, no practice. Use the middle tier (old classmates, ex-colleagues, annual-contact cousins) where you're socially valid but not emotionally complicated.
- **Treating insurance as a Year-1 short-term expense** - it lives in the long-term third, protecting the 40-year version of the client.
- **Saying "I don't want to sell to friends"** - that sentence isn't humble; it means you don't have conviction in the work. If you can't do warm, you can't do cold either.
- **Hoping motivation will carry the system** - willpower is not a strategy; environment design is. Set up the cue/craving/response/reward loop once, then stop relying on willpower.
