# Week 5: Operating Rhythms & Daily Discipline - Cheat Sheet

> **The one idea:** You become what you track. A new FC who tracks only commission is frustrated by Month 2. One who tracks Activities x Skills x Knowledge - inside a weekly rhythm and a five-lever funnel - can diagnose problems before they become disasters and put a number on every retirement gap a client mentions.

## The three personal rituals - rhythms beat goals

A goal is a target; a rhythm is a behaviour. Targets without behaviours drift. Install these now, before clients arrive.

| Ritual | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Sunday review** | 30 min, any window Sun evening | Look back at the week, pick 3 specific measurable priorities, calendar-block them before anything else |
| **Daily opening** | First 15 min of every workday | Review today's 3 priorities, pick the single most important task, decide what you will *not* do today |
| **Friday wrap** | Last 10 min of the workweek | Note what closed, what got dropped, send Monday-you notes, clear the decks |

**Signal you got it right:** Monday feels like a continuation, not a cold start. The weekend starts without an open loop buzzing in your head.

**Learning rhythm for the 60 days:** 15 min/day on the current lesson + Sunday re-skim of the week's 6 lessons + 1 real-world practice rep per week. Over 8 weeks: 56 micro-lessons deeply read, 8 integrations, 8 reps.

**A ritual you don't defend isn't a ritual - it's a suggestion.** Calendar-block, decline conflicts with one line ("I have a weekly review at that time - earlier or later?"), audit every 4 weeks. The 4-week audit asks the *harder* version of each question - not "did I run my dials?" but "am I still calling people who could say no in a way that hurts?"

## 10X Rule in daily action - apply selectively

10X works for **inputs where volume produces skill + results**: outreach, content, role-plays. Does NOT work for client meetings, proposals, product study, admin - quality matters more there.

**Realistic 3-5X targets** (worked example): outreach 20/wk -> 80-100/wk (16-20/day); LinkedIn 0 -> 2-3/wk; role-plays 0 -> 2/wk.

**The MAD framework (Minimum Activity Day):**

| Day type | Outreach | Content | Role-play |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Full | 20 | Draft/post | 1 |
| Partial | 10 | Half-draft | 0 |
| **MAD (minimum)** | **5** | Skip | 0 |
| Off (deliberate, sick/holiday) | 0 | Skip | 0 |

**The deadly day is the ambiguous one** - intended to work but drifted to zero without deciding to stop. **Two-day rule:** never miss twice in a row. Once is a data point; twice is a pattern. Miss target -> diagnose effort layer, return to baseline, keep target. Don't punish-double next day.

## The personal scorecard - Activities x Skills x Knowledge = FYC

**The formula is multiplicative.** Zero in any factor produces zero output. Diagnose the weakest link first.

| Factor | Tracked as | If it's the weakest link... |
|---|---|---|
| **Activities** | Outreach touches, meetings, content posts, referral asks | Do more. Review Day 19, Day 26. |
| **Skills** | Outreach-to-apt ratio, meeting-to-close ratio, avg premium | More reps + mentor feedback. Record meetings. |
| **Knowledge** | CMFAS papers, product deep-dives, objections logged | Block 30-60 min/day for structured study |

**Worked example:** Month 3 FC has Activity 8/10 (80 outreaches/wk), Skill 3/10 (1-in-10 close ratio), Knowledge 5/10. Diagnosis: skill is the bottleneck. Wrong fix is more outreach. Right fix is 3x weekly closing role-play with mentor.

**Year-1 ratio benchmarks:**

| Ratio | Acceptable | Good | Great |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Outreach -> Appointment | 1 in 25 | 1 in 15 | 1 in 10 |
| Appointment -> Meeting held | 1 in 2 | 2 in 3 | 3 in 4 |
| Meeting -> Close | 1 in 5 | 1 in 3 | 1 in 2 |
| Average premium / close | $150/mo | $250/mo | $400+/mo |

## The five-lever funnel - QO x ASR x SUR x CR x LTV

Activities x Skills x Knowledge tells you what to build in yourself. The funnel tells you *where* this week's effort is leaking.

| Lever | Healthy Y1 baseline | Lift it by |
|---|---|---|
| **Quantity of Outreach (QO)** | 200 dials/wk | Time blocks, dial discipline, two-phone stacking |
| **Appointment Set Rate (ASR)** | ~5% of pick-ups | Opener quality, niche fit, nurture warmth |
| **Show Up Rate (SUR)** | ~1 in 3 | Confirmation messages, clear meeting value |
| **Close Rate (CR)** | ~1 in 3 of shows | Conviction, cushion-first objection handling |
| **LTV** | $5K+ first close | Service, review cadence, referrals |

**Compounded:** 200 x 5% x 30% x 30% x $5K = **$5K/week ~ $20K/month**. Honest ratios, not stretch numbers. **QO is the most controllable lever** - ASR/CR improve ~10-20% with script work; QO doubles by adding an hour. When in doubt, fix QO first, then climb the funnel.

## Skills x Traits x Beliefs - the bottleneck diagnostic

Once the funnel tells you *where* the leak is, this tells you *what kind* of fix it needs.

| Layer | Symptom | Intervention |
|---|---|---|
| **Skills** | "I know what to do but I fumble it under pressure" | Reps, role-plays, recorded meetings, mentor feedback |
| **Traits** | "I do it for a week, then drift" | Systems that force consistency - calendar blocks, scorecards, accountability partners |
| **Beliefs** | "I avoid the activity that would fix this" | Reframe + evidence - find one person whose belief was different and whose results were different |

**The brutal-honesty question:** if you knew the right fix last week and still didn't do it - is it a skill, a trait, or a belief that's stopping you? The honest answer determines whether next week's intervention is reps, systems, or reframing.

## FYC x 4 and remuneration math

**Benchmark:** first-90-day FYC sets the trajectory. Aim for ~4x by Year 4. $30K in first 90 days -> ~$120K/year by Year 4.

**Commission rates (sanity-check against latest AIA schedule):** APA (ILP) 35% Y1 / 15% Y2 / 8% Y3 / 5% Y4-5 / 1.5% perpetual. GPP (Whole-Life) 40% / 20% / 5% / - / 1.5% perpetual. **Solitaire PA: 30% perpetual every year.**

**Conservative Year-1 model at $3K/month FYC:** FYC $36K + EPS 1:1 match $36K + PA ~$3K + Bonus (~42% of FYC) ~$15K = **~$90K Year-1 total**. To hit $3K/month FYC at $250 avg premium: ~2-3 closes/month = ~10-15 meetings/month = ~30-60 outreaches/month. That's your scorecard's lower bound.

## TVM - the 5 variables and the speed test

Every financial conversation sits on this. Top producers run a full TVM calc in front of a client in **under 90 seconds**.

| Variable | Meaning | Sign |
|---|---|---|
| **n** | Periods (years or months) | integer |
| **i** | Interest rate per period | %/period |
| **PV** | Present Value today | money in = +, money out = - |
| **PMT** | Regular payment | + or - |
| **FV** | Future Value at end | + or - |

**Sign convention is the #1 source of errors.** Money flowing OUT of you = negative. Money flowing IN = positive. Insurance premiums use **BGN mode** (payments at start of period); loans use END.

**Time horizon -> reasonable rate assumption:**

| Timeline | Rate |
|---|---|
| Under 3 yrs | 1-3% (cash/bonds) |
| 3-7 yrs | 3-5% (balanced) |
| 7-15 yrs | 4-6% (growth) |
| 15+ yrs | 5-7% (equity-heavy) |

**Live client move:** 40-year-old wants $1M at 65. FV=$1M, n=25, i=6, BGN -> PMT ~ **-$1,520/month**. "At 6% you'd need $1,520/month for 25 years. If that works, yes you get there. If not, we adjust target, timeline, or vehicle. Which?" Instant credibility.

## Compounding and the Rule of 72

> **Years to double = 72 / interest rate**

2% -> 36 yrs . 4% -> 18 yrs . 6% -> 12 yrs . 8% -> 9 yrs . 12% -> 6 yrs

**The early-start premium.** Alice contributes $500/month at 6% from 25-35 ($60K in) then stops. Bob contributes $500/month at 6% from 35-65 ($180K in). Alice ends ~$500K. Bob ends ~$470K. **Alice contributed 1/3 of Bob's amount and ends with more.** Use iResource's calculator to show clients the cost of a 3-year delay (typically 20-30% of final retirement capital).

**Cost-of-stopping framing** for clients pausing a long-term plan: do the math, give the number, no guilt. *"Stopping now costs you roughly $385,000 by 65. That's the car - and 20 more cars on top. Your decision."*

## DCA - time IN the market beats timing the market

DCA = fixed amount, fixed intervals, regardless of price. Low prices buy more units; high prices buy fewer. Average cost per unit ends below the average market price.

**Worked example.** $1,000/year x 5 years, prices $5/$1/$3/$2/$3. Invested $5K; units ~2,367; Year-5 value $7,100. **42% gain despite price ending at $3.** Year 2's crash bought 1,000 units cheap. Lump sum at $5 (top) loses $2K; at $1 (bottom) gains $10K. DCA = predictable +$2,100.

**The behaviour reason matters more than the math reason.** Retail investors underperform the market by 2-4% annually because of emotional decisions, not fees. DCA automates buying through downturns and prevents over-buying at peaks. Works best at 10+ year horizons.

**Client sentence to memorise:** *"Time in the market beats timing the market - and DCA is how ordinary people get time in the market without making emotional mistakes."*

## Numbers worth memorising

- **3 priorities (not 10)** per week, calendar-blocked first
- **15 min/day + Sunday re-skim + 1 real-world practice rep/week** = the learning rhythm
- **5 outreaches** = MAD-day minimum; **20** = full-day target
- **200 dials/wk x 5% x 30% x 30% x $5K LTV ~ $20K/month** - the funnel compounded
- **FYC x 4 by Year 4** - first-90-day production sets the trajectory
- **$3K/month FYC = ~$90K Year-1 total** (after EPS match + PA + bonus)
- **72 / rate = years to double** (6% -> 12 yrs)
- **2% inflation default** for Singapore retirement planning
- **BGN mode** for insurance, **END mode** for loans

## Common failure modes

- **Tracking only commission** - lagging output that swings with luck. Track Activities/Skills/Knowledge inputs you control.
- **Wrong diagnosis on a low FYC** - assuming it's activity when the close rate is the bottleneck, or assuming skill when the real issue is a belief problem (*"cold calls don't work for someone like me"*).
- **Ambiguous days** - intended to work but drifted to zero. MAD day with 5 outreaches beats it every time.
- **Quoting an 8% rate for a 3-year goal** - aggressive assumption on a short horizon. Match rate to timeline.
- **Selling DCA without rebalancing** - a "60/40 portfolio" left alone for 10 years drifts into something else entirely. Managed ILP rebalances automatically; DIY rarely does.
- **Doubling up the day after a miss** - punishment, not recovery. Return to baseline immediately.
- **Cramming the lessons the weekend before they're due** - the advisors who finish strong are the ones who studied 15 minutes a day, not those who binged.
