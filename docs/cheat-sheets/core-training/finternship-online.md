# FINternship Online - Cheat Sheet

> **The one idea:** FINternship Online is a structured, 21-week pre-licensing programme that compresses the work of a typical 3-5 year career into a single intern cycle. The goal is not exam memorisation - it's that by Week 21 you can run a full 5-phase appointment cold, with conviction, on warm or cold leads, with the agency's scripts, leads pipeline, and VA support behind you.

---

## The programme arc - 21 weeks at a glance

| Phase | Weeks | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundation** | 0-3 | Vision board, mission, mindset, financial planning fundamentals locked |
| **Productivity + Skills** | 4-8 | AI productivity, persuasion principles, success habits, high-income skill stack |
| **Sales Mechanics** | 9-13 | Product packaging, pitching APA/PLP/GPP/SFT/HSG/UCC, prospecting, CRM, appointment-setting |
| **Story + Habits** | 14-18 | Story craft, 11 habits, leverage (people / systems / branding) |
| **Compression + Output** | 19-21 | 75 years of work in 25 years, stop-grinding-start-winning, real production |

Each session is ~2 hours with a 5-minute break at the midpoint. Recorded. Watch-back is mandatory if you miss a live.

## The 5 core products to master (Week 9 anchor)

| Product | Abbrev | Type | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| ProAchiever | **APA** | Investment-linked | Pure investment, dividend mode at 65 |
| Premier Life Protector | **PLP** | Investment-linked | Hybrid (protection + investment) |
| Guaranteed Protect Plus | **GPP** | Whole life | Protection-focused, ~2.3-3% guaranteed |
| Secure Flexi Term | **SFT** | Term | Protection only, no cash value |
| HealthShield Gold Max | **HSG** | Hospitalisation | Medical / hospital coverage |
| Ultimate Critical Cover | **UCC** | Critical illness | Multi-pay reset CI |
| Solitaire PA | **SPA** | Personal accident | Accident coverage |

**Main course (drill first):** APA, PLP, GPP. **Side dishes (learn after):** HSG, SFT, UCC, SPA.

### The 3 packaging options on the same budget ($600/mo example)

| Package | Components | $600 split | Investment headroom |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Investment + Whole Life | APA + GPP + HSG + SPA | $500 to GPP, $100 to APA | Barely viable |
| 2. Hybrid | PLP + HSG + SPA | Balanced | Mid |
| 3. Investment + Term | APA + SFT + HSG + SPA | $100 to SFT, $500 to APA | **Best returns** |

For most clients focused on both growth and coverage, Package 3 wins. Whole Life vs Term = Buying vs Renting analogy.

## The 5 levers of sales output (Week 12 anchor)

| Lever | What it is | Where the leverage is |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Outreach volume | How many you contact | Easiest to move - time + money |
| 2. Appointment set rate | % who agree to meet | Script + niche fit |
| 3. Show-up rate | % who actually attend | Confirmation cadence |
| 4. Close rate | % who buy | Skill - hardest to move |
| 5. Case size / LTV | $ per client over time | Follow-up + referrals |

**The insight:** new FCs obsess over closing. Set rate is 10x more leverageable. Double 1% -> 2% is easy. Doubling 50% -> 100% close rate is impossible. *"If you don't have flow in the system, you can't practice anything else."*

## Activity benchmarks - the numbers that gate progress

- **Full-timer minimum:** 35-40 activity points/month. **7-12 appointments/week.**
- **Undergrad minimum:** 15 points/month. A few appointments/week.
- **Benchmark of intensity:** Gabriel hit 163 points in a single month.
- **Ads ROI baseline:** $2K spend -> $12K+ FYC = 6x. Insurance leads are not like e-commerce. You have the *accountability to follow up* - ROI is essentially guaranteed if you actually follow up.
- **Income trajectory at maturity:** Y1 ~$120K -> Y2 ~$250K -> Y3 ~$300K -> Y4 ~$330K (Leo's path as a student, no mentor).
- **Tracker:** `trackmywhy.winfinancial.com.sg` - district FYC + case + live-case transparency.

## The 6 principles of persuasion (Week 6) - cheat-line each

| Principle | One-line application |
|---|---|
| Liking | *"Treat every client as a long-lost friend - the warmth shows in your tone."* |
| Reciprocity | Buy a $3 drink. Track every milestone (birthday, POP, ORD, BTO). They remember 5 years later. |
| Authority | Marketing kit, AIA's 5,000+ staff, 958 healthcare partners, you = financial doctor |
| Consistency | Get small yeses early. Each yes lowers the cost of the big yes. |
| Social proof | Real claims case studies. Quote dollar amounts. Show conversation screenshots. |
| Scarcity | Time-bound: *"This bonus tier applies if we lock in by month-end."* Never fake it. |

## The 4 traits of top advisors (Week 2)

1. **Conviction** - Sales is a transfer of conviction. If you don't believe, neither will they. Procrastination fighter framing: *"Everyone else asks them to spend. We ask them to save for themselves."*
2. **Consistency** - Heartbeat analogy: small beats every day, not one big spike. Discipline beats motivation.
3. **Courage** - Discomfort calendar. Cold calls. Rejections. Fear-setting drill.
4. **Character** - Long game. Annual reviews forever. The 10-20 year policy is a 10-20 year relationship.

## Numbers worth memorising

- **Programme length:** 21 weeks. 2hrs / session + 5-min break.
- **Time horizon framing:** 75 years of work compressed into 25. Front-load.
- **25x rule:** annual lifestyle expenses x 25 = invested-asset target for FIRE.
- **The compounding gap:** Start at 20 vs 30 = ~$300K difference in retirement savings.
- **Activity points:** 35-40/mo full-timer, 15/mo undergrad. 7-12 appointments/week full-timer.
- **iPoS KPI:** under 5 minutes (4:58).
- **Ads ROI baseline:** 6x ($2K -> $12K+ FYC).
- **The 5 levers:** outreach, set rate, show-up, close, LTV - in that order of leverage.
- **The 3 sales:** sell yourself > sell the company > sell the product.

## Common failure modes

- **Watching videos passively.** The recordings beat the videos. Role-play, record, send to mentor, apply feedback.
- **Obsessing over close rate.** The leverage is at the front of the funnel. Fix set rate first.
- **Memorising scripts word-for-word.** Internalise the principles. Scripts go stiff. Principles travel.
- **Going for products before pipeline.** *"Knowledge without action produces nothing."* Go for meetings even before you know every product.
- **Skipping the discomfort calendar.** If you haven't felt nervous, stretched, or awkward recently, you're stagnating.
- **Treating the CRM as optional.** No CRM = no pipeline = no follow-up = wasted leads.
- **Burning out on the slow path.** 40 years of low-grade dissatisfaction is more expensive than 10 of concentrated grind. Compress.
