# Appointment Setting - Cheat Sheet

> **Internal stance before any dial:** abundance, not scarcity. Industry math is ~149 rejections per close. Treat "no" as the price tag of the close, not a verdict. If the voice goes tight, run a 20-rejection sprint with the goal of *collecting* 20 nos before the day ends.

---

## The funnel - five levers, one income number

| Lever | Healthy Y1 baseline | Lift it by |
|---|---|---|
| **Quantity of Outreach (QO)** | 200 dials/wk · 50/hr · 3hrs/day | Time blocks · two-phone stacking · dial discipline |
| **Appointment Set Rate (ASR)** | ~5% of pick-ups | Opener quality · niche fit · nurture warmth |
| **Show-Up Rate (SUR)** | ~1 in 3 (be pessimistic) | Confirmation messages · clear value · perceived stakes |
| **Close Rate (CR)** | ~1 in 3 of those who show | Conviction · cushion-first objection handling · clarity |
| **Lifetime Value (LTV)** | $5K+ per first close | Service quality · review cadence · referrals |

**Compounded:** 200 × 5% × 30% × 30% × $5K LTV ≈ **$5K/week → $20K/month**.

**Activity math behind one closed case:** 1 close ≈ 3 shown ≈ 9 set ≈ 180 pick-ups ≈ **900 dials**. At 50/hr that's ~18 hours of telephony. 3 hrs/day, 5 days/wk ≈ one case/week from cold alone.

## 30-second cold-call opener - three beats

1. **Opening (10-15s)** - *"Hi [name], this is Leo from [company]. I work with young professionals on financial planning. Quick check - do you have 30 seconds?"* Confidence over apology.
2. **Relevance (15-30s)** - *"Most people I speak with in your stage tell me they know they should be planning but don't know where to start."* Name the pain that's theirs.
3. **The ask (30-45s)** - *"Would Saturday 10am or Sunday 2pm work better for a 30-min Zoom - no obligation?"* Two specific times. Never "are you interested."

**What kills the call:** speaking softly · sounding scripted · *"would you be interested"* · weak tone. 80% of the outcome is energy and tonality.

## Cushion + reframe - the universal objection structure

> **Cushion first, every time.** *"That makes sense" · "Totally fair" · "I'd probably feel the same in your shoes"* - then reframe.

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Not interested" | *"Totally fair. Most people say that at first. Many find it valuable once we talk - would you give me 60 seconds to share what I do, and if it's not for you, we hang up here?"* |
| "Too busy" | *"That's exactly why - busy people without a plan compound their problems. 30 minutes now saves the 3 hours of admin you'll need later."* |
| "No money" | *"That's exactly why - when there's no buffer, the wrong $50/month decision costs you more. The plan is what frees up the buffer."* |
| "Already have policies" | *"That's exactly why - most people who already have cover don't actually know what it does day-to-day. The 30 minutes turns the policy you own into a plan you understand."* |
| "Send me the info" | *"I'd rather not waste your inbox - 15 mins on a quick Zoom and you'll know if any of it's worth your time."* |

**Skip the right-question diagnostic** when the objection is a stock reflex (above). **Run the diagnostic** when the objection is vague - *"Just so I respect your time, is that not interested in advisors at all, not interested in insurance specifically, or not the right timing?"* About 70% of "not interested" is actually timing or trust.

## The 5 silent questions every cold prospect asks before DMing you

1. **Can I trust others value your work?** → testimonials in pre/during/after format
2. **Do you actually know your stuff?** → real client problems, not jargon
3. **Why are you doing this?** → personal "why" story
4. **Will we get along?** → personality, hobbies, quirks
5. **How can I approach without feeling awkward?** → low-friction CTA - *"DM me 'plan'"*

**Audit your bio + last 10 posts.** Score each question 0-2. The lowest score is next week's fix.

## DNC screening - before any cold list goes live

1. Lead list → Google Sheet → download as CSV (filename: no spaces, no special chars; use `leads.csv`).
2. DNC Registry portal → **Check Registry → Bulk Filtering** → upload → Proceed.
3. Wait 5-10 min. Download results.
4. **NR** = Not on Registry → contactable. **DNC** → remove from sheet.

Re-screen any list older than 30 days. Calling a DNC number is a regulatory violation.

## Touchpoint intake - 4 questions that buy you 4-10 years of cadence

In the first meeting, extract:

1. **"How old are you? When's your birthday?"** → annual touch forever.
2. **"When's your POP?"** → extrapolates enlistment + ORD + uni entry (NSF prospects).
3. **"When's your ORD?"** → post-NS shift anchor.
4. **"When are you entering university?"** → semesters + graduation.

**NSF durations to memorise:** BMT 2mo · SCS 6mo · OCS 9mo · ORD ~2y from enlistment · Uni starts Aug after ORD · graduates ~May year 4.

**Calendar SOP same day:** title format `Wish [message] - [name] - [number] - [your initials]`. All-day events. Birthdays recur yearly forever. Semester wishes recur yearly × 4. One-off milestones (POP, ORD) no repeat. Colour-tag the same (e.g. Tangerine). Share to mentor.

**Working-adult swap:** work anniversary · policy-review window (11 months post-purchase) · life-event radar (engagement, kid, parent diagnosis, promotion, mortgage).

## Where prospecting sits - the five-pillar business

| # | Pillar | New-FC default move |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lead generation** | Outreach blocks, content, networking, referral asks |
| 2 | **Lead nurturing** | Personalised follow-ups, value drops, Telegram/IG presence |
| 3 | **Sales** | Fact-find, presentation, close - drilled in role-play |
| 4 | **Service** | Annual reviews, claim help, milestone touches |
| 5 | **Retention & resale** | Quarterly check-ins, life-event radar, structured re-engagement |

**Most stuck Y1 FCs are stuck at Pillar 2, not Pillar 1.** Re-read your CRM before assuming you need new names.

## The 30-day rule of pipeline gravity

Miss a day → bites you in 30 days. Miss a week → next quarter's cheque. Miss a month → 90-day slump, no idea how you got there. **Today's missed hour is next quarter's lost month.** Plant the seeds anyway.

---

## Diagnose where the leak is - when ratios slip

- **QO low** → you didn't dial enough. No script fix. More reps. Add a fixed daily block.
- **ASR low (< 5%)** → opener is the problem. Rewrite + record + listen to your own calls.
- **SUR low (< 1-in-3)** → confirmation messages are weak. Reminder + value-bump before the slot.
- **CR low (< 1-in-3)** → conviction or skill. Role-play, mentor, recorded mocks.
- **LTV low** → transactional service. Fix the back two pillars (Service + Retention).

**Skills × Traits × Beliefs lens:** is it execution (need reps), consistency (need systems), or self-talk (need reframe)? The honest answer changes the intervention.
