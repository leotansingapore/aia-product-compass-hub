# Debrief Flow - Cheat Sheet

> **The one idea:** The debrief is the meeting where you turn a fact-find into a recommendation. Default cadence: book it **one week from the close**, never *"I'll text you to find a time."* From a meeting, book a meeting. Run it as a checklist, not a re-pitch - they've already heard the why; this is the *what, the how much, and the sign-here.*

---

## Where the debrief sits in the 5-phase flow

```
Phase 0   Pre-appointment confirmation
Phase 1   Rapport + agenda + problem
Phase 2   Concept presentation (TWC / drawings)
Phase 3   Fact-find (income, expenses, assets, liabilities)
Phase 4   --- DEBRIEF (this meeting) ---
Phase 5   iPoS submission + onboarding
```

The debrief is **Phase 4**. If they didn't close in the first meeting and your priority was CI, you said: *"Now that we've closed the CI, let's set the debrief one week from now to walk through the full picture."*

If they didn't close at all, the debrief is your second appointment - same slot, lower pressure, full proposal.

---

## The verbatim booking line - same day, before they leave

> *"From the exact date we met, let's set the debrief one week from now. I'll do up the policy summary and the proposal, and we'll go through what to expect, what's covered, and what's still open. Same time next Wednesday at 8pm work, or Saturday morning?"*

Two times. Never *"when's a good time for you?"* - that buys you a 3-week delay.

**Calendar SOP that day:** event title `[Name] - Debrief - [number] - [your initials]`. 60 min block. Zoom link in the description. Reminder set 24h + 1h before. Share with mentor for shadowing if it's your first 5.

---

## The post-meeting text - send within 2 hours of the first meeting

> *"Hey [name], really enjoyed the chat just now. To recap - we talked about [hot button], your priority is [CI / hosp / retirement], and your budget is around [$X]. I'll prep the proposal and the policy summary by Wednesday. See you next [day, time]. Let me know if anything shifts."*

This is your contract. It locks the next meeting and gives them a written summary they can re-read.

---

## What you prep before the debrief - the checklist

| Item | Who does it | Done by |
|---|---|---|
| Policy summary (existing policies, mapped to planning norms) | You + VA (Ira / equivalent) | 48h before |
| GoalsMapper run (net worth, cash flow, ratios) | You | 48h before |
| Ghost Mapper / fund + fees comparison (if they have existing ILP/whole-life/endowment) | You | 48h before |
| Proposal in iPaaS Plus / iPoS - 2 budget tiers (their stated + one tier above) | You | 24h before |
| Pre-meeting reminder text | VA | Morning of |
| Zoom link tested + recording consent ready | You | 1h before |

If you walk into the debrief without these, the meeting collapses into a re-pitch.

---

## The debrief run sheet - 45 to 60 minutes

| Block | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Recap their why | 5 min | *"Last week you told me [hot button]. Still the priority?"* If shifted, re-anchor. |
| 2. Policy summary walk-through | 10 min | Show the upper block (risk management - death, TPD, CI, early CI, hosp, accident) and lower block (wealth accumulation - ILPs, endowments). Coverage now vs planning norms side by side. |
| 3. Gap on the page | 5 min | Highlight the shortfall in red on the printout / iPad. Don't tell them they're under-covered - point at the page and let them say it. |
| 4. Recommendation | 10 min | Two budget tiers. Lead with the higher tier ("ideal"), then drop to their stated budget if needed. Anchor high, settle middle. |
| 5. Comparison if they have existing policies | 5-10 min | Whole-life: BTIR / decoupling. Endowment: cash value + IR vs ProAchiever. ILP: fund + fees comparison. Hospital: medical plan comparison. |
| 6. Objections | 5 min | Cushion first, every time. *"Makes sense"* + reframe. (See objection table below.) |
| 7. Application + signature | 10 min | iPoS form. Target under 5 minutes once you've drilled it. Submit on the spot. |
| 8. Post-meeting expectations | 2 min | Free-look period, underwriting timeline, policy summary deliverable, next touch in 12 months. |
| 9. Referral ask | 2 min | *"Two people who would benefit from the same conversation - first names that come to mind?"* (See referral line below.) |

## The ABCD recap - what to map their existing policies against

| Letter | Coverage area | Planning norm |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Death + TPD | 10-20x annual income + major liabilities |
| **B** | Critical Illness (incl. Early CI) | 100K base + 3-5x annual income |
| **C** | Hospitalisation + Accident | Comprehensive shield + 5x income PA |
| **D** | Retirement + Wealth accumulation | Inflation-adjusted target (TWC retirement calc) |

You're scoring their existing coverage against A, B, C, D on the spot. The empty box is the recommendation.

---

## Cushion + reframe - the universal objection structure

> Cushion first, every time: *"That makes sense"* / *"Totally fair"* / *"I'd probably feel the same in your shoes."* Then reframe.

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| *"Let me think about it"* | *"Of course. To respect your time - is the hesitation the budget, the product, or me as the advisor? Whichever it is, I'd rather know now."* |
| *"Need to discuss with spouse"* | *"Smart. Let's lock in a Zoom with both of you next week so they can ask me directly instead of via you."* (Book the next slot before they leave.) |
| *"Already have policies"* | *"Most clients who have cover don't actually know what it does day to day. That's exactly what the policy summary I prepared is for - 15 minutes and you'll know."* |
| *"Budget tight"* | *"That's exactly why - if cash is tight, the wrong $50/month decision costs more. Let's start at the lower tier and review in 12 months when income shifts."* |
| *"S&P / crypto already"* | *"Complement, not replace. AIA's portfolio has no dividend tax, no FX risk, no estate tax, and your spouse inherits a managed portfolio - not a wallet they have to liquidate."* |

## The hot-button cross-sell sequence

If you closed one product on the spot last meeting, use the debrief to bridge to the next:

- **Closed CI -> next is hosp/accident:** *"Now that CI is locked, the missing piece is the hospital plan - CI pays you, hosp pays the hospital."*
- **Closed hosp -> next is CI:** *"Hosp covers inpatient. The bigger income risk is the 3-5 years off work during recovery. That's the CI plan."*
- **Closed term/whole-life -> next is APA / PWV:** *"Protection's done. Wealth accumulation is the second engine - 10-20% of income compounding."*

## Diagnostic rules - is this debrief healthy?

- **Recap landed?** They restate the hot button without prompting. If they don't, you're presenting too soon - re-anchor.
- **Shortfall visible?** The gap appears on the printout, not just in your speech. No paper -> no urgency.
- **Two tiers shown?** Anchor + their stated. If you only show one, you're negotiating against yourself.
- **Application opened?** iPoS form gets opened on screen by minute 30. If not, you're not really in a debrief - you're in a second pitch.
- **Referral asked?** Default ask in every debrief. Even one *"maybe"* name is worth the question.
- **Next touch booked?** 12-month review goes on both calendars before they leave. Recurring yearly event.

---

## The referral line - the only one that works

> *"Two people you care about who'd benefit from the same conversation - first names that come to mind. I'm not asking for warm intros today, just first names. I'll show you how I'd reach out before I do anything."*

The *"show you how I'd reach out before I do anything"* removes the social risk. That's the line that gets the name.

---

## Numbers worth memorising

- **Booking cadence:** debrief = 1 week from the first meeting. Annual review = recurring yearly forever.
- **Application time target:** iPoS form under 5 minutes once drilled (your KPI is **4:58**).
- **Coverage norms:** Death 10-20x income, CI 100K + 3-5x income, Accident 5x income.
- **Free-look:** 14 days from policy delivery (verbatim into the post-meeting script).
- **Tier anchor:** always show 2 tiers - their stated budget + one above. Lead with the higher.
- **VA handoff:** policy summary + new business text template -> Ira (or your VA) -> 48h turnaround.

---

## Common failure modes

- **Booking via text after the first meeting.** Loses 60%+ of the room. Book from the meeting, before they leave.
- **Re-pitching the why.** They've heard it. The debrief is for *what* and *how much*. Five minutes max on recap.
- **Walking in without the policy summary.** Without the page, there's no gap, no urgency, no close.
- **Only showing their stated budget.** Anchor higher. Never negotiate against yourself.
- **Forgetting the existing-policy compare.** If they have whole-life / endowment / ILP and you don't pull up the comparison, they default to *"let me think."*
- **Skipping the referral ask.** It's free leverage and your highest-LTV lead source.
- **No 12-month review on the calendar.** Lose this and you lose the annual touch forever - the policy goes cold the moment underwriting completes.
