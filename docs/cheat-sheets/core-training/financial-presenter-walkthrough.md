# Financial Presenter Walkthrough - Cheat Sheet

> **The one idea:** The presenter is a tool for you, not for the client. Start with the problem on paper, then open the iPad. Every flow has a fixed entry route: gap on the page -> reverse-engineer the premium -> illustrate -> bonuses-vs-charges close. The tool's three big leverage points are the **CPF Life Estimator**, the **Retirement Income Gap calculator**, and the **bonus calculator** - drill those three until they open in under 5 seconds.

---

## The tool map - what's actually in there

| Section | When to open |
|---|---|
| **Investment-Linked (APA, AIA Wealth Venture, PWV, Invest Easy)** | Phase 2 of an investment-led appointment |
| **Hybrid / Retirement (Platinum Wealth Elite, Platinum Retirement Elite)** | Older client, retirement-anchored |
| **Endowment (SmartWealth Builder, Platinum Gift for Life, Platinum Infinite Wealth)** | Education / fixed-horizon savings angle |
| **Insurance (UCC, Solitaire PA, AIA Hospital Income, HealthShield tools)** | CI / hosp / accident anchor |
| **Planning Tools (CPF Life, Retirement Lifestyle, Income & Tax, Mortgage)** | Lead magnets + Phase 1 opening conversation |
| **Total Wealth Concept (TWC)** | Holistic planning anchor at the start of Phase 2 |
| **Statistics module** | Pull out for CI / hosp urgency (73% of CI claims = cancer; 1 in 4-5 by age 75) |
| **AI chatbot per plan** | Backup when the client asks something you don't know cold |

iPaaS Plus does the numbers and benefit illustrations. The presenter does the *story*. Don't confuse them.

## ProAchiever (APA) walkthrough - in 9 minutes

| Min | What you do on screen | Verbatim line |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1 | Open with paper - $2M retirement target, $5K/mo to save vs $800/mo to invest | *"Instead of saving $5-6K/month, we can invest $800/month."* |
| 1-2 | Open APA illustrator -> key in premium + age | *"Let me show you the numbers behind that."* |
| 2-3 | Slide age slider: start 5y later -> 10y later -> 20y later | *"Wait 20 years and the same outcome costs ~5x more per month."* |
| 3-5 | Switch on **dividend mode** at age 65, premium holiday from 65 | *"From 66 onwards, this becomes a monthly income. Capital stays. Dividends come out."* |
| 5-6 | Pull up linearised return: total - premiums / years / 12 | *"For every $100 you put in today, you eventually earn ~$500."* |
| 6-7 | Open **bonus calculator** ($12K/yr tier) | *"Y1 to Y3 bonuses are ~$6.3K. Then 5% from Y10, 8% from Y20 - forever."* |
| 7-8 | Switch to **mechanics slides** -> Diversification + 10-Year Rule | *"After 10 years, historical probability of negative return is ~0% across major equity markets."* |
| 8-9 | Knowledge Assistant for any question you don't know | *"Let me ask the AI - one second."* |

**Key APA selling lines to internalise:**
- *"AIA Index Fund: ~1% actual fee, SGD-denominated, exposure to S&P / Europe / Asia / Japan / Canada / Australia."*
- *"No dividend withholding tax, no estate tax, no FX risk."*
- *"On death the plan transfers to the secondary assignee - the plan keeps compounding for them."*
- *"AIA's breakeven on charges vs bonuses is ~34-36 years. After that, AIA is losing money on your plan."*

## Platinum Wealth Venture (PWV) walkthrough - 3 entry flows

**Pick the flow that matches the client's stage. Don't run all three.**

### Flow 1 - Retirement Income Gap (the default)

1. Open **CPF Life Estimator** -> key in their age -> show projected payout (e.g. 55yo -> ~$1,694/mo).
2. Open **Retirement Income Gap calc** -> add rental, existing plans, desired lifestyle, 3% inflation.
3. Read the shortfall number out loud. *"$X/month gap. And it grows every year because inflation rises but CPF Life stays flat."*
4. Show funding options table: Fixed deposits need ~$4.38M, CPF OA needs ~$1.75M, **PWV needs ~$729K**.
5. Click through to PWV illustrator - reverse-engineer the exact premium for 5 years.
6. Toggle **reinvest dividends** vs **single premium vs yearly** to match their cash flow.

### Flow 2 - CPF Life Angle

Anchor on CPF Life (not against it), then show PWV as the supplement. Lead with the depletion math from the concept drawing - capital depletes ~age 80-81, bequest = $0.

### Flow 3 - Portfolio Consolidation

For clients with existing scattered policies. Use SG Findex + Ghost Mapper -> identify redundant / underperforming plans -> restructure into PWV. Pull the competitor's product summary inside the tool to highlight their perpetual admin charge.

### The PWV killer comparison - administrative charges

> The #1 thing to search for in any competitor's product summary.

| | AIA PWV | Most competitors (Manulife, GE, SingLife, Pru) |
|---|---|---|
| Perpetual admin / supplementary charge | **None** | 0.65% to 1% p.a. (e.g. Manulife 3-10 Flexi ~0.7%) |
| Breakeven point | ~19 years | Never - charges compound forever |
| Fund structure | 3-in-1 (Allianz + Fidelity + 1 more) | Single fund |
| Stewardship | AIA Investment Management ($328B AUM) | Agent-dependent |

The tool deep-links into competitor product summaries. Use it. Live, on the spot.

### PWV dividend illustration - the verbatim sequence

1. *"Y1 you put in $60K, dividends start in 3 months."*
2. *"Y2 you put in another $60K, dividends roughly double."*
3. *"By Y5 you're at $300K invested, ~$2,500/month dividends."*
4. **Toggle reinvest on** -> *"Or, reinvest dividends until 65 and switch to cash payout - much bigger income stream."*
5. Pull up real client payout screenshots in **Social Proof** section: quarterly dividend $1,563 on ~$84K account = 1.86% / quarter = **~7.4% annualised**.

**Critical fund-choice rule:** if the client intends to reinvest dividends, recommend the **Index Fund**, not Global Adventurous Income Fund. The 0.4% performance bonus only applies to the regular premium policy value - reinvest into GAIF and that compounding is wasted.

## Mortgage Calculator - the lead magnet flow

The mortgage calculator is a **prospecting tool**, not a product pitch. Use it as a touchpoint with fresh grads, BTO couples, and warm leads.

### The 8 inputs to drill cold

| Input | Default / Note |
|---|---|
| Property price | Client-supplied |
| Loan period | 25 years default |
| Interest rate | 2.6% default |
| Housing grants | Default none - check proximity / first-timer grants |
| Purchase month | Month of **Option to Purchase**, not key collection |
| Waiting period | ~3-4 yrs built in (BTO) |
| Age + monthly salary (both applicants) | Both required |
| CPF OA balance (both) | Determines cash top-up |

### The cash-flow story that lands

1. **Booking** (Day 0): $2-3K cash.
2. **Signing of lease** (~4 months): CPF OA covers most; small cash gap (~$12K).
3. **Key collection** (~3 yrs): balance downpayment; mortgage begins. Total cash needed at key collection: ~$60K.
4. Show **OA accumulation chart** - OA fills back up during the wait. *"You don't need to set aside as much cash as you think."*

**Conversation hook:** *"My team built a calculator that shows exactly what your HDB flat costs you month by month. Want me to run the numbers for you?"* Run it live or send the link. Use the result to open the financial planning + protection conversation.

## The bonuses-vs-charges reframe - the universal closer

Use this on ANY ILP / endowment / hybrid plan when the fees objection lands.

**APA example ($12K/yr):**
- First 10 years: ~3.9%/yr charges + welcome bonuses = ~$15K net charges
- After Year 10: charges stop, bonuses continue forever
- Breakeven: ~34-36 years. After that, **AIA is paying you more than they charge.**

**PWV example:**
- Y1-7: 3.6% of regular premium policy value (supplementary charge)
- Y8 onwards: 2.5% investment bonus (Y8-11) + 0.4% performance bonus perpetual
- Breakeven: ~19 years. After that, **policy is effectively free.**

**Verbatim reframe line:** *"AIA is hoping you cancel after 10 years - that's when they profit. If you hold long-term, my job is to make sure AIA doesn't profit off you."*

**Competitor with 1% perpetual fee math:** at 8% return over 40 years, perpetual 1% chews ~$400K+ off a portfolio. At 80 years, ~$1.3M. AIA's front-loaded structure is far cheaper at the long end.

## Diagnostic rules - is your presenter run healthy?

- **Tool stays closed first 5 minutes?** Phase 1 + concept drawing must land before iPad opens.
- **Knowledge Assistant used live?** Use it on the spot when stumped - builds credibility, not anxiety.
- **Toggles fluent?** Reinvest, premium-holiday, single-premium-vs-yearly should snap in under 3 seconds each.
- **Comparison Zone for objections?** Open it the moment the prospect mentions a competitor's product.
- **iPoS app form under 5 minutes?** Your KPI is **4:58**. Drill until it's muscle memory.

## Numbers worth memorising

- **APA bonuses:** Y1-3 = 15% / 18% / 20% = 53% total on $12K/yr (~$6.3K). Y10 = 5%. Y20 = 8%.
- **APA charges:** ~3.9%/yr first 10 years. Then stop. Breakeven ~34-36 years.
- **PWV charges:** 3.6% Y1-7. Then 2.5% investment bonus Y8-11 + 0.4% performance bonus perpetual.
- **PWV breakeven:** ~19 years.
- **PWV minimum premium:** $18,000/year.
- **PWV target dividend yield:** ~7-7.5% (real client data shows 7.4% annualised).
- **Index Fund recommendation:** if reinvesting dividends, use Index Fund - not GAIF (performance bonus rule).
- **CPF Life payouts (2025):** BRS $930, FRS $1,707, ERS $3,300.
- **Mortgage calc defaults:** 25 years, 2.6%, ~3-4yr BTO wait built in.
- **Cancer urgency:** 73% of CI claims = cancer; 1 in 4-5 Singaporeans by age 75; late-stage treatment $100-200K.

## Common failure modes

- **Opening the tool first.** Always paper drawing -> tool. Order matters.
- **Showing all three PWV flows in one meeting.** Pick one - retirement gap, CPF anchor, or portfolio consolidation.
- **Quoting fund names before drawing the shortfall.** Fund names mean nothing until the gap is visible on the page.
- **Skipping the bonus calculator.** Bonuses-vs-charges is your single best fee-objection closer. Always open it.
- **Memorising the script word-for-word.** The principles let you adapt. The tool is the structure.
- **Not bookmarking the products you actually pitch.** Bookmark APA, PWV, GPP, UCC, HSG, SFT - the rest is noise during a live meeting.
- **Forgetting the perpetual-1% math when a competitor comes up.** That math is how you close the sophisticated prospect.
