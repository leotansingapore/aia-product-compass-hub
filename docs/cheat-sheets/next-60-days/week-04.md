# Week 4: Prospecting at Volume - Cheat Sheet

> **The one idea:** Calendar full, cases pending. Most Year-1 advisors don't fail at closing - they fail at filling the calendar. Volume is the skill they never built.

> **Week 4 KPI:** 30 outreaches; 5 appointments booked.

## Market Temperature - the 4 rings + where revenue lives

```
Hot -> Warm -> Semi-Warm -> Cold
```

Typical 500-contact network composition:

| Ring | Who | % | Note |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Hot** | Parents, siblings, best friends | 4% | Exhausts fastest |
| **Warm** | Friends, ex-colleagues, relatives | 20% | Most new FCs over-index here |
| **Semi-Warm** | Old contacts not spoken to 1-5 years, referrals | **66%** | **The real market.** Least competition. Longest runway. |
| **Cold** | Strangers | 10% | Transactional. Law of large numbers. |

**The trap:** work Hot + Warm in months 1-3, close a handful, run out, panic. **Fix:** Semi-Warm is where 3-year pipeline actually lives. Start reactivating in Week 4, not Year 2.

**Warm vs cold register switch - get this right or both channels fail:**

| Dimension | **Warm market** | **Cold market** |
|---|---|---|
| Opener | Personal reference, shared history | Named pain of their niche |
| Credibility | Existing relationship | Framework + social proof |
| Tone | Vulnerable, honest | Professional, authority |
| Ask | Easy-out, no pressure | Clear CTA with value hook |

Warm runs on vulnerability. Cold runs on authority. Never mix the registers.

## The 4 Types of Prospects - 10/20/60/10

| Tier | % | Strategy |
|---|---:|---|
| **1. Definitely will buy** (family, loyal) | 10% | Relationship maintenance |
| **2. Most likely to support** (friends, relatives) | 20% | Warm outreach + relevance |
| **3. Can be converted** (anyone, with info + persuasion) | **60%** | **Where the real work is** |
| **4. Die-die will not buy** | 10% | **Avoid wasting resources** |

**Biggest Year-1 time-waste:** spending 40% of effort on the bottom 10% (*"if I can just convince this one person..."*). Redirect to the 60% middle - that's where Week 4's 30 outreaches land most profitably.

## The 6-step honest warm-market message

For Warm-ring contacts. Vulnerability beats polish.

```
1. Real connection -> 2. Honest about why -> 3. Say the awkward thing ->
4. Offer help, not pressure -> 5. Give an easy out -> 6. Relationship close
```

**Worked beats:**
1. *"Hey - hope Vietnam's been treating you well, saw the travel posts."*
2. *"I've been meaning to reach out about something a bit personal - financial planning. Honestly, I was scared to bring it up."*
3. *"I didn't want to make things weird or seem like I was reaching out just to pitch."*
4. *"If you're open to it, happy to do a quick no-obligation review."*
5. *"Of course, if it's not for you right now, I completely understand."*
6. *"Would be nice to catch up properly anyway - it's been too long. Still at Singtel?"*

## Market Survey - the warm-market phone script

A 5-minute phone call with 4 questions. Q4 is always the appointment ask. Minimum: 30 surveys with warm + semi-warm before any cold work.

| Beat | Script |
|---|---|
| **Opening** | *"Hi - is this Amir? Do you have a moment to talk? How have you been this period?"* |
| **Agenda** | *"As you may have heard, I recently got my license as a financial advisor. As part of building my practice, I'm doing a quick 5-minute survey with people I know - just 4 questions, nothing to buy."* |
| **Q1 - Coverage** | *"Do you have any insurance policies or financial plans at the moment?"* |
| **Q2 - Last review** | *"When was the last time you did a review?"* |
| **Q3 - Service** | *"How do you feel about your current advisor's service - responsive, explains clearly, regular reviews?"* |
| **Q4 - Ask** | *"You're one of the people I'd love to work with eventually. May I get in touch for a 30-minute session? You have my assurance I won't ask you to do anything you don't want to. Is that fair?"* |
| **Logistics** | *"Office, home, or Zoom? Weekdays or weekends? Thursday at 7 or Saturday at 2?"* |

**Warm Up vs Educational Fact Find:** the survey response tells you which the meeting is. If they have no plan and mostly said yes to see you again, it's a Warm Up - no iPad, no CST. Pitching at a Warm Up burns the contact.

**Objection pattern:** acknowledge -> reframe -> re-ask. Every response ends with *"would you be free Thursday or Saturday?"*

## Cold prospecting - 5 pillars

```
Niche -> Framework -> Profile -> Sharp DM -> Follow-up
```

| Pillar | What good looks like |
|---|---|
| **1. Niche** | NOT *"business owners"* - **F&B owners with 2+ outlets**. NOT *"parents"* - **parents of primary-school kids in the East**. |
| **2. Framework** | A named structure (P.I.S.A, F.A.T, A.P.E.X) - gives them a name to remember you by |
| **3. Profile** | Day 10 work matters 10x more here - the only trust signal before they reply |
| **4. Sharp DM** | Named pain + specific value + low-commitment offer (no call needed) |
| **5. Follow-up** | Every touch carries new value - never *"just checking in"* |

**Strong cold DM example:** *"Hi - saw you're running a 2-outlet F&B operation. Most owners I work with in your stage run into the same cashflow trap at the 3rd outlet expansion. I put together a 1-page audit we use to stress-test it - happy to send if useful. No pitch, no call required."*

## Unconventional cold call + cold-call KPI ratios

**The 4-stage favour opener (beats hang-ups):**
1. **Favour:** *"Hi Mark - I actually need to ask you a favour, please don't hang up. Honestly, I've called the last 50 people today and every single one of them hung up. I'm hoping you'll be the kind soul to give me one minute. I won't sell you anything you don't need - if at any point you're not interested, hang up. Is that okay?"*
2. **Research framing:** *"My team and I are trying to understand the critical financial-planning issues that professionals aged 30 to 45 are navigating. Would you mind answering a few quick questions?"*
3. **3 hot-button questions:** *"What's most challenging about managing your finances right now?"* / *"What financial goals or concerns come to mind first?"* / *"What would make you feel more confident about retirement?"*
4. **Thank + value:** *"Since you've been so helpful, I'd like to send you our Retirement Confidence Index as a thank-you - would that be useful?"*

**Cold-call KPI ratios - the numbers behind one closed case:**

| Lever | Healthy baseline |
|---|---|
| **Dials/hour** | 40-50 (50+ for 3 sustained hrs) |
| **Pick-up rate** | ~20% of dials |
| **Appointment-set rate** | ~5% of pick-ups |
| **Show-up rate** | ~1 in 3 |
| **Close rate** | ~1 in 3 of those shown |

**Compounded:** **1 close = ~3 shown = ~9 set = ~180 pick-ups = ~900 dials**. At 50 dials/hour, **18 hours of telephony per closed case**. 3 hrs/day x 5 days/wk = ~1 closed case/wk from cold alone.

**Two-phone stacking** doubles hourly QO. When two pick up, drop one, circle back next round.

## DNC SOP - non-negotiable before any cold list

Calling a DNC-registered number is a regulatory violation. Penalties are real and personal.

1. Lead list -> Google Sheet -> download as CSV (filename: no spaces, no special chars - `leads.csv`).
2. DNC Registry portal -> **Check Registry -> Bulk Filtering** -> upload -> Proceed.
3. Wait 5-10 min. Download results.
4. **NR** = Not on Registry = contactable. **DNC** = remove from sheet entirely.

Re-screen any list older than 30 days - registration status changes.

## ABCD Four Promises - first 30 seconds of every Fact-Find

| Letter | Promise | Script |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Assurance** | *"This meeting isn't to sell you anything. If we end up with a proposal, it's because I shared something valuable and you felt comfortable."* |
| **B** | **Best Interest** | *"If we do reach a proposal, my promise is that what I plan for you is what I'd do for myself in your shoes."* |
| **C** | **Confidentiality** | *"Everything you share stays in strictest confidence."* |
| **D** | **Disclosure** | *"With that said, I hope you can share openly - because the quality of the recommendation depends on the quality of what I learn."* |

4 sentences, ~40 seconds. The Fact-Find runs differently after ABCD - prospects open up faster because the frame answered their concerns before they voiced them.

## Strategic Target List + Recurring Inventory

**20 names / month minimum.** One sheet. Five columns: Name, Tag (ABC / 1-2-3), Trigger, Approach (Market Survey / Attraction / 6-step / CRAB / cold DM), Last contact.

**Current vs Recurring Inventory:**

| Type | Analogy | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Current** | Present value | ABC clients + 1/2/3 prospects on the books today |
| **Recurring** | Monthly payment | Monthly flow of new leads - content, referrals, events, collaborations |

**The cliff:** new FCs work Current hard, ignore Recurring. Current exhausts at month 9-12 with nothing to replace it.

**Recurring Inventory Budget:** ~20% of weekly prospecting time from Day 1 - content, referral asks at every Fact-Find, one new relationship-building activity per month.

## Numbers worth memorising

- **30 outreaches / 5 appointments** = 17% conversion. Above 20% scale volume; below 15% fix the script (usually the opener).
- **100-140 calls / week** for 5-7 appointments (20-28 dials/day).
- **~900 dials per closed case** end-to-end from cold alone.
- **6 first-FHRs x 40 working weeks = 240 appointments/year.** 50 clients at 30% review rate = 15 review appts = 2.5 weeks. The other 37.5 weeks come from new-lead flow.
- **0-5 cold** of the 30 outreaches - cold is optional and experimental in the first 60 days.
- **~75-85% of Year-1 revenue** comes from warm + Semi-Warm.
- **Law of Familiarity:** warm inbound 5-12 touches, semi-familiar 5-20, cold 20-50 before reply.

## Common failure modes

- **Mixing the warm/cold registers.** Vulnerable cold openers and authority-heavy warm messages both die.
- **All 30 outreaches on one channel.** A 30-cold-DM week is a drill, not a pipeline build. Mix warm Market Surveys + 6-step + Attraction + CRAB + value drops + 0-5 cold.
- **Counting *"let's meet sometime"* as a booked appointment.** A booking needs confirmed time + place/format + both sides acknowledged.
- **Padding the count.** 24 real outreaches > 30 padded ones. The skill is *"can I repeat this next week unprompted?"*
- **Quitting the call block at dial 3 or 4.** Three no's in a row = the prospect, not you. The block dies at 4 dials with zero bookings instead of continuing to 20 with one.
- **Pitching at a Warm Up.** If the Market Survey response said *"never thought about it"*, the meeting is a Warm Up - no CST, no iPad. Pitch burns the contact.
- **Skipping DNC screening.** Regulatory violation. 5-10 minute screen. Never skip.
- **Cold-call opener that asks *"would you be interested?"*** Gives them the easy no. Use 2 specific time options.
- **Working Current Inventory only.** Reserve 20% of prospecting time for Recurring from Day 1, or the month-9 cliff arrives on schedule.
