# Week 2: Pro Lifetime Protector (PLP) - Cheat Sheet

> **The one idea:** PLP is the only AIA plan that lets a prospect pay for 10 years and then walk away - the policy keeps compounding, the cover keeps running to age 100, and on Max the insurance charge zeros out once policy value crosses the insured amount.

---

## What this product is + who it is FOR

A SGD regular-premium Investment-Linked Policy issued by AIA, combining lifetime protection to age 100 with a Mercer-guided wealth engine. One premium dollar splits between protection (the floor) and investment (the engine). Evolves with life stage - protection-focused in the 20s-40s, retirement-income in the golden years.

**Ideal client (3 markers):**
- 20s-40s wanting protection + retirement income in one premium
- 30s-50s wanting a permanent legacy / estate floor underneath term-pay GPP
- Has the protection foundation (HSGM, base CI) already mapped + 10+ year horizon + accepts non-guaranteed returns

## The pitch angle - opener stems

Open with a discovery question that surfaces which job matters more - protection or accumulation.

> *"Quick one - when you think about cover and savings together, which one is more on your mind right now? Protecting the family, or building something for retirement?"*

> *"Got it. The plan shape is different depending on which side matters more. PLP is the one I reach for when both matter at once. Mind if I sketch it for you?"*

The signature analogy (verbatim from the source):

> *"100K death coverage is not just a ship, but a ship WITH a lifeboat. Two-in-one protection. A ship without a lifeboat is not secure."*

Limited-pay permanent-asset close:

> *"Pay 10 years and then stop. The policy continues. The coverage continues to age 100. The cash value keeps compounding. Put $24K in across 10 years, and the projection at age 100 is around $1.8 million."*

## Objection cluster

| Objection | Cushion -> reframe -> close-to-action |
|---|---|
| "PLP is more expensive than GPP" | *"Fair - GPP and PLP target different needs. GPP is fixed-term whole-life with booster; PLP is permanent + flexible. GPP you must pay 25 years; stop early and the plan stops. PLP lets you stop after 10 years and the policy continues to grow and protect. Same $48K in: GPP ~$300-400K at 100; PLP ~$3.4M at 100. Different mechanic, different result."* |
| "I don't need lifelong cover" | *"Lifelong is the point of the floor. After 65 if you wanted to extend a term policy for $500K cover to age 95, the SFT cost is roughly $21K/year. With PLP, after 65 the insurance is essentially free because Sum-at-Risk has gone to zero on Max. So lifelong isn't an extra you buy - it's the cost you avoid."* |
| "Buy term and invest the rest" | *"Mathematically defensible. The catch: APA $900 + SFT $100 gives roughly 10% higher returns than PLP - but the SFT only runs to 75. Post-65 SFT renewal eats the 10% advantage and you still lose lifelong cover. PLP's defence is the lifelong floor; APA's attack is the pure return. Match the plan to the client."* |
| "Why does my account look so low in Year 1?" | *"Premium charge is 80% in Year 1, 55% Y2, 50% Y3, 8% Y4, then zero from Year 5. Think buying a laptop - upfront cost, then drops to zero. From Year 10 it's 102% allocated - 2% Special Bonus added every year. Front-loading is the price of permanent + flexibility."* |
| "Competitor whole-life is cheaper" | *"Competitor whole-life is usually par - bonus rate historically 3-4%. PLP's investment engine is Mercer-guided, fully invested by Year 5, with US$12.9T AUM under advisement and 40+ years of track record. Plus PLP is the only one that lets you pause without losing cover, take partial withdrawals, and dial down coverage in retirement."* |

## Cross-sell / sequencing

| Layer | Where PLP sits |
|---|---|
| HSGM (Week 5) | The hospital floor - mandatory before layering PLP. PLP without HSGM is malpractice. |
| GPP (Week 3) | Booster years - high temporary cover. Stack: PLP permanent floor + GPP booster for the 20-25 booster years. |
| UCC (Week 4) | Standalone multi-claim CI. **Strip the LCC rider, sell UCC separately** - cleaner CI, no death-benefit erosion. |
| APA (Week 1) | Pure investment - if prospect wants only investment, pivot to APA. PLP is the hybrid; APA is the pure player. |
| Mum-to-Baby rider | For expectant clients - the highest-leverage adjacent sell on PLP. |

**Default stack:** PLP permanent floor + GPP booster + UCC for CI. PLP is rarely sold alone - its highest-leverage placement is underneath a GPP+UCC stack.

## Numbers worth memorising

1. Premium charge schedule: **80 / 55 / 50 / 8 / 0%** (Y1-Y5)
2. Special Bonus: **2% from Year 10** on regular premiums (102% allocation)
3. Min regular premium: **S$100/month**
4. Adult entry age: **18-70**; child 0-17
5. Sum-at-Risk formula: **Insured Amount + top-ups - withdrawals - policy value** (when SAR <= 0, no Benefit Charge on Max)
6. LCC max payout: **300% of Insured Amount** with **12-month Power Reset** to 100% after last admitted claim
7. SFT renewal cost age 65-95 for $500K cover: **~$21,000/year** (the cost PLP avoids)
8. Milestone increase option: up to **S$200K aggregate** before age 60, no medicals

## Common pitch failure modes

- **Pitch PLP to a pure-investment-only prospect.** Anti-market disclosure: pivot to APA. PLP needs the protection job to matter.
- **Skip the protection foundation.** PLP layered on a missing HSGM is malpractice. Book a separate appointment for HSGM first.
- **Sell Plus when Max is the default.** Max self-funds insurance over time - once policy value crosses Insured Amount, the insurance charge zeros out. Plus only wins for maximum legacy / death benefit cases.
- **Project at 8% when 6% is the discipline.** Use 6% with the prospect; the historical track record is around 7% - underpromise, overdeliver.
- **Match the portfolio above the prospect's risk profile.** Cautious prospect on Pro Adventurous = RNF audit. Match or go lower, never higher.
- **Sell the LCC rider when UCC standalone is the cleaner CI play.** LCC is additional (not accelerating) but caps at 300%; UCC has unlimited Major-stage claims with 12-month reset. Strip the LCC; sell UCC separately.
- **Forget the retention defence at delivery.** A competing FC 6 months later can pull up the Year-1 80% charge and convince the prospect they bought badly. Pre-empt at delivery; book the 30-day check-in.
