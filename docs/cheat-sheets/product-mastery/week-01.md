# Week 1: Pro Achiever 3.0 (APA) - Cheat Sheet

> **The one idea:** APA is a guaranteed-issue SGD ILP (no medicals) that gives the prospect institutional fund management plus a stacking bonus structure no DIY platform can replicate - structurally engineered to look painful for 10 years and unbeatable from Year 11 onwards.

---

## What this product is + who it is FOR

A regular-premium Investment-Linked Policy issued by AIA, SGD-denominated, matures at age 100 of the original insured, no medical underwriting. Premium buys units in chosen sub-funds; returns are not guaranteed. The prospect is buying a long-term retirement vehicle, not a trading account.

**Ideal client (3 markers):**
- Age 25-50, $4.8K-$6K annual premium band, 20-40 year horizon
- Wants to outsource investing (no time, no skill, or DIY-fatigued)
- Can commit a monthly auto-debit they will not stop

| Item | Value |

| Free-look | 14 days |

## The pitch angle - opener stems

Open with a discovery question, not a feature dump. Three verbatim starts:

> *"Quick one - when you think about retirement, is it more about not running out of money, or making sure the family is taken care of if you go first?"*

> *"Honest question - if you passed away tomorrow, what happens to your existing portfolio? Does your spouse keep it running, or cash everything out?"*

> *"I'm not here to replace what you're doing on Tiger or IBKR - that's your trading account. I'm here for the retirement bucket. Mind if I show you how the two fit together?"*

Then sketch the curve on paper - charges front-loaded for 10 years, bonuses perpetual from Y10. The prospect sees the structure, not the slide deck.

## Objection cluster

| Objection | Cushion -> reframe -> close-to-action |
|---|---|
| "I'll just buy S&P 500" | *"Totally fair - I started there too. Honest question: would you bet your retirement on US equities outperforming every other geography for the next 40 years? Over 40 years a 1-in-20-year drawdown happens roughly twice. S&P 500 vol is ~21%; APA's international portfolio is ~11%. Same equity exposure, half the volatility. Does that change how you'd think about the concentration?"* |
| "Fees are too high - 3.9% is a lot" | *"Fair, 3.9% is real and I won't dress it up. At Year 11 the charge goes to zero permanently. The 5% Special Bonus from Y10 plus 8% from Y21 are perpetual. At $12K/year premium, cumulative bonuses pass cumulative charges by Year 34. After that AIA is effectively paying you. Compare to iFAST at 1% perpetual - on a $1M portfolio that's $10K/year forever."* |
| "I want guaranteed cash values" | *"Honest reframe - do you want to guarantee a loss to inflation instead? Fixed-return options cap at 2-3%; bank cash loses ~2.75%/year to inflation. APA's structural guarantee is the capital-guaranteed death-benefit floor - your family never receives less than what you paid in."* |
| "I'll just DIY ETFs" | *"ETFs are a great tool, my own portfolio has them. The two pieces an ETF can't replicate: the Welcome + Special Bonus stack ($45K+ extra over a 40-year hold at $12K premium), and the Secondary Insured. If you go first, your spouse inherits a managed policy that AIA keeps running - not a Tiger login she has to figure out."* |
| "I want dividends now" | *"Fair. The flip - would you rather have dividends landing now when you don't need them, or 25 years from now when monthly income is the difference between working and not working? APA accumulates till retirement, then switches into GDIF for ~5% dividend yield with capital preserved. $2.4M pot at retirement throws off ~$10K/month and the capital stays for the kids."* |

## Cross-sell / sequencing

| Layer | Where APA sits |
|---|---|
| HSGM (Week 5) | The floor - protect health before accumulating. Always closed before APA. |
| GPP (Week 3) | Pure-CI + death cover layer. APA does not provide CI; GPP does. |
| UCC (Week 4) | Multi-claim CI on top - claiming UCC does not touch APA. |
| PLP (Week 2) | Alternative when prospect wants protection + investment in one. APA = pure investment; PLP = hybrid. Never both for the same client. |
| PWV (Week 7) | The pre-retiree successor. Existing APA holders aged 50+ layer PWV for retirement income; APA continues compounding underneath. |

**Sequencing rule:** the 1/3 Rule - short-term (cash), mid-term (endowment), long-term (APA). APA is the long-term third only. Always protect the short and mid first.

## Numbers worth memorising

1. $96K in over 45 years at $200/month -> $411,600 -> linearised to **$583/month** of effective income
2. Total cost of distribution on $200/month plan over 45 years: **$11/month** ("hire an investment consultant for $11/month")
3. Break-even (bonuses overtake charges) at $12K premium: **Year 34**
4. Default Adventurous Index Fund FMC: **~1.0% p.a.** (lowest on platform)
5. Multi-manager stewardship: **Wellington / BlackRock / Baillie Gifford / Capital Group**
6. Death-benefit floor: 100% of total premiums paid + top-ups - withdrawals
7. CPF Life retirement gap (today's $3K/mo lifestyle): needs **~$10K/mo at 65** -> **$2.4M pot**

## Common pitch failure modes

- **Splash the slide deck on screen.** Prospect sees 50 pages, brain shuts down. Fix: draw one diagram on paper first, slides are your reference, not the prospect's content.
- **TMI mode.** Going through every welcome-bonus tier, every fund option, every charge. Decision fatigue kills the close. Fix: give just enough information for the prospect to feel the gap, then close. Save deep mechanics for post-sale education.
- **Pitch 99% Adventurous to a cautious prospect.** Default is Self-Select Adventurous Index Fund, but match to actual risk profile - can go lower, never higher.
- **Sell IIP-20 because the bonus headline is biggest.** Commission is identical across IIPs. Right IIP is the one the prospect can stay in. Default = 10-year.
- **Argue against S&P 500 instead of reframing.** The frame is Perfect / Imperfect / Partially Perfect - question the assumption, don't attack the position.
- **Lead with policy summary instead of retirement-funding angle.** Same content, wrong sequence. Build the structural gap first, then pull the policy summary as ammunition.
