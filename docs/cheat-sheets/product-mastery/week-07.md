# Week 7: Platinum Wealth Venture (PWV) 2.0 - Cheat Sheet

> **The one idea:** PWV is a 5-year-pay ILP for pre-retirees that converts accumulated wealth (CPF OA excess, maturing endowments, lazy cash) into a quarterly dividend stream while preserving capital - the income engine that closes the gap CPF Life leaves open after 65 (no interest), after 81 (no bequest), and at any age (no liquidity).

---

## What this product is + who it is FOR

A SGD 5-year premium-paying Investment-Linked Plan issued by AIA. 100% of regular premium invested from day 1 (no upfront sales load). Pays quarterly dividends from the AIA Global Adventurous Income Fund (historical ~7%; project at 6% with clients). Death benefit is the higher of total regular + top-up premiums (less withdrawals) or current policy value - capital floor on death.

**Ideal client (3 markers):**
- Age 50-65 approaching retirement with **accumulated wealth to redeploy** (CPF excess after FRS at 55, maturing endowments, legacy ILPs, lazy cash)
- Wants quarterly income stream + capital preservation for family
- Has at least 5 years of stable premium-paying capacity (anti-market: under-50 without accumulated wealth, no 5-year capacity, or needs liquidity in years 1-7)

## The pitch angle - opener stems

Open with discovery, not the product. Pre-retirees have time, capital, and skepticism:

> *"Out of curiosity - when you picture your retirement, what does the monthly cashflow side of it look like? Have you mapped out where the income actually comes from?"*

> *"That's the question I sit on with most pre-retirees I meet. There's usually CPF Life, maybe a maturing endowment, possibly rental - but the gap between what they want and what those produce is rarely closed. Mind if I sketch out what your picture might look like, using your actual CPF numbers?"*

The 3 structural moats vs CPF Life:

> *"We're not saying CPF Life is bad - it's compulsory and provides guaranteed income. But it may not be the most efficient tool for your full retirement picture. Three things to notice: no interest after 65, no bequest after 81, no liquidity. CPF Life is an annuity, not a savings account."*

The Funding Mechanism reveal (Panel 7 of the Drawing Concept):

> *"Year 1-3 - you fund $40K from cash. Year 4 - your $144K OA excess unlocks at 55. Year 5 - surrender value from your old endowment that matures at 56. You don't need new money to fund this plan. The plan funds itself from resources you already have."*

The Healthcare Cost angle (loss-aversion hook):

> *"From age 55 to 85 you'll spend ~$412,000 just to pay for your HSGM A premium. Instead of paying that as an expense, what if we set this money aside today to build an asset? Even at 6% dividend, we cover all the premiums, have excess for lifestyle, AND leave the capital to your family when you pass."*

## Objection cluster

| Objection | Cushion -> reframe -> close-to-action |
|---|---|
| "I need to think about it" | *"Want you to think it through properly, not feel rushed. To help you think clearly - is it the amount, the timing, or the product itself?"* (Vague objections collapse when narrowed.) |
| "I want to discuss with my spouse" | *"That's how it should be done. Would it help if I sat down with both of you together? Any question your spouse has, I'd rather address directly than relay through you. When works for both of you?"* |
| "The market is too volatile right now" | *"I'd be uncomfortable with any plan asking you to lump-sum into a volatile market too. PWV is 5-year staggered premium - you buy at different price points across the cycle by design. Plus the death benefit floor means your family receives at least total premiums paid, regardless of where the market sits."* |
| "I prefer fixed deposits - they're guaranteed" | *"Your capital is also contractually protected on death here. The bigger difference is the yield - 6% conservative on PWV vs 2.5% on FD. On $200K capital that's $7,000/year - over 20 years roughly $300K of compounded growth left on the table. FDs also roll at the prevailing rate - when SIBOR drops, your income drops with it."* |
| "I'll do it next year" | *"Life rarely has a perfect moment for something this size. Cost of waiting - you're 52 today, your $144K OA excess at 55 lines up to fund Year 4. Wait a year, you're 53 starting - same plan, same outcome, but a year of compounding gone. On a 5-year staggered plan, 1-year delay reduces year-21 policy value by ~$60K."* |

## Cross-sell / sequencing

| Layer | Where PWV sits |
|---|---|
| APA (Week 1) | **The legacy-ILP restructure** - audit existing APA via SingPass. If true yield is <2% (real case: $67K paid over 7 years -> $74K = 1.4% IRR), restructure into PWV. Always document both scenarios in writing before recommending surrender. |
| HSGM (Week 5) | The healthcare cost angle reframes HSGM lifetime premium ($412K) as the expense PWV can fund. Asset, not expense. |
| Whole-life / endowments (Pru, GE, NTUC) | The **Color-Coded Policy Review** - Green = Keep, Orange = Optimize, Red = Restructure. Real cases: NTUC whole-life 1.07% yield, GE Living Assurance $48K cash vs $50K death after 29 years. Redeploy red policies' freed-up cashflow into PWV. |
| GPP / UCC (Weeks 3-4) | Cover layer - PWV is the income engine, not the cover. Keep CI / death cover separate. |
| Secondary Insured | Max age at appointment 75 - appoint for legacy continuity. |

**Funding priority (Panel 7 sequence):** (1) CPF OA excess at 55, (2) policy surrender values / maturing endowments, (3) liquid cash. Always in that order.

## Common pitch failure modes

- **Skip the Drawing Concept and pitch from slides.** PWV's irreplaceable asset is the 7-panel whiteboard sequence built live using the prospect's OWN CPF numbers. By Panel 7 the client has invested 25 minutes of attention and the conclusion feels like theirs. Slides feel generic.
- **Pitch PWV to under-50 prospects without accumulated wealth.** Wrong product. Anti-market is explicit - no 5-year capacity, no accumulated wealth, needs liquidity Y1-7. Pivot to APA.
- **Project at 7% or 8% with the client.** Discipline is 6% in client conversations - actual fund history is ~7%. Underpromise, overdeliver. Brochure case studies showing 8% are for FC reference only.
- **Quote PWV 1.0 numbers.** Always cite from **PWV 2.0 PS v1.0 042025** - canonical bonus structure (Welcome 3/4/5 = 12%, Investment 2.5% x 4 years), 7-year supplementary charge, 5-year premium term.
- **Recommend surrender of a legacy policy without documenting both scenarios in writing.** The FC who restructures cleanly earns trust that compounds. Audit via SingPass, quantify true yield, compare PWV alternative, document, then recommend.
- **Collapse the close into one big decision.** Always Two Never Three. Build micro-yeses throughout - by the close, the prospect has said yes ~15 times. *"$30K or $42K annually? Quarterly to wallet or reinvest for first 3 years?"*
- **Leave paperwork for a follow-up appointment.** Every follow-up is a future cancellation in disguise. Run the full 12-item checklist in one ~89-minute session.
