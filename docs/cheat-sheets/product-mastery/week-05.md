# Week 5: HealthShield Gold Max (HSGM) - Cheat Sheet

> **The one idea:** HSGM is the protection floor every other plan rests on - it pays the hospital so the client doesn't have to, and if it isn't in place, every CI lump sum and every accumulated retirement pot gets eaten by medical inflation (+55.8% in Singapore from 2004 to 2024) before it ever helps the family.

---

## What this product is + who it is FOR

An Integrated Shield Plan (IP) issued by AIA, layered on top of compulsory MediShield Life. Reimburses inpatient bills (minimum 6 hours hospitalisation or surgery). Three plan tiers gate ward class and hospital choice. With the AIA Max VitalHealth Pro rider, out-of-pocket co-insurance caps at S$6,000/year - converting unbounded medical risk into a known annual maximum.

**Ideal client (3 markers):**
- Anyone aged 1-75 (next birthday) - this is universal-fit cover
- No IP yet, employer-cover-only, or wants to upgrade from B-class public to private hospital access
- Has family history of cancer or other long-treatment conditions - Cancer Care Pro lifts CDL ceilings 16-fold

## The pitch angle - opener stems

Open with the four-pillar discovery question - lets the prospect rank hospitalisation as first themselves:

> *"Out of all the coverages - hospitalisation, accident, critical illness, death/disability - which one do you think is the first line of defence?"*

Most say hospitalisation themselves. Then the bridge:

> *"Whichever way the wheel turns - accident, CI, even death - there's almost always a hospital bill first. So hospital cover is the door everyone walks through."*

The travel-package analogy for the 13-month wraparound:

> *"It's a bit like a travel package - you don't just get the tour itself. The ticket there and the ticket back are covered too. Before the hospitalisation, MRI scans and specialist consults. After, follow-up consultations and chemotherapy. All covered - up to 13 months on each side under Max A with an AIA preferred provider."*

Value-stack before price (the conversion):

> *"$2 million annual limit, 13 months pre/post, unlimited claims for life, no premium loading because you claimed, Cancer Care Pro lifting CDL coverage 16-fold. How much do you think such a plan would cost per month?"*

Let them guess (most anchor at S$150-S$300). Then reveal:

> *"It's actually starting from just S$18 a month for public, or about S$70 to S$80 a month for private."*

## Objection cluster

| Objection | Cushion -> reframe -> close-to-action |
|---|---|
| "MediShield Life is enough" | *"MediShield Life is the foundation - it's great we all have it. The way it's sized - B2 and C-class wards in public hospitals - means the moment you upgrade or go private, the bill is pro-rated. A S$200,000 private bill: MediShield Life pays roughly the public-ward equivalent, and you absorb the rest. Even people who say they'll only ever go public usually take at least B Lite, because the ambulance doesn't ask which ward you wanted."* |
| "I have employer / corporate coverage" | *"Corporate cover is good while you have it. Three things to think through. First, it disappears when the job disappears - career break, retrenchment, retirement, job switch. Second, it usually excludes pre-existing conditions - anything that surfaces while you're with this employer can become uninsurable later. Third, annual caps are typically well below S$2M. HSGM is portable, lifetime, yours regardless of employer. Layer it underneath the corporate plan now, while underwriting is clean."* |
| "The rider is too expensive - why not just take the base plan?" | *"Without rider, max exposure on a S$100K bill is S$13,150 (deductible + 10%). On a S$1M bill, 10% co-insurance alone is ~$99,650. With rider, you pay 5% capped at S$6,000/year - full stop - if treated by preferred provider OR pre-auth OR A&E referral. Rider isn't a luxury - it's what stops a $1M event from becoming a $100K personal bill. ~S$60-80/month for private."* |
| "Why pay for ward-class A when B is so much cheaper?" | *"The optionality argument most people miss - if you start with Plan A and find it too expensive, you can downgrade to B with no underwriting. If you start with B and want to upgrade to A later, you face full underwriting again - and if anything has shown up on a medical report, you may not qualify. Plan A now buys the option to step down. Plan B closes the upgrade door."* |
| "What if I switch jobs / change my mind / want to leave AIA later?" | *"HSGM is portable - yours regardless of employer. If you switch insurers, the IP system handles termination on the back-end. The catch: medical underwriting. Switching is easy when you're healthy, hard when you're not - which is why locking in now matters. AIA was ranked #1 across all 6 metrics in the SMA IP Providers Survey 2022 - so the case for switching out later is usually weak."* |

## Cross-sell / sequencing

| Layer | Where HSGM sits |
|---|---|
| **HSGM is the foundation - always sells before anything else.** Without it, every other plan's payout gets eaten by medical bills. |
| Solitaire PA (Week 6) | **Always pair with HSGM in a basic-medical conversation** - HSGM covers inpatient, Solitaire PA covers outpatient + partial-disability gap. Bundle close ~S$45/month. |
| GPP (Week 3) | CI / death cover layer once the hospital floor is in. |
| UCC (Week 4) | Multi-claim CI - HSGM pays the hospital, UCC pays the client. Both needed. |
| APA (Week 1) | The HSGM-to-APA pivot (Video 30) - lifetime hospital premium ~S$300K; APA dividends in retirement can fund those premiums for life. Book as a separate appointment. |
| PWV (Week 7) | The pre-retiree variant of the APA pivot - $412K lifetime HSGM premium reframed as an asset, not an expense. |

**Default bundle:** Max A + AIA Max VitalHealth Pro A rider + Cancer Care Pro (the conviction stack for any prospect with family cancer history). For travellers, drivers, school-age kids: add Emergency Care Pro.

## Common pitch failure modes

- **Claim "fully MediSave-payable".** Wrong. MediSave AWL caps at S$300/year on the AIA top-up; rider is 100% cash. Quote cash all-in - that's what hits the wallet.
- **Reveal price before the value-stack.** Without the prospect anchoring at S$150-300/month first, the actual S$80/month has nothing to compare against. Stack the values, let them guess, then reveal.
- **Recite the 4 compliance disclosures back-to-back before signature.** Plants doubt at the worst moment. Use each as an objection reframe when it surfaces naturally. The MAS switching disclosure is the only one that requires verbatim wording.
- **Offer with-rider / without-rider as the close choice.** Fragments the decision and signals doubt. The rider is assumed in. Close is one question: *"Private or public?"*
- **Forget to mention the cap conditions.** The S$6,000 co-insurance cap only triggers if treated by preferred provider OR with pre-auth OR via A&E referral. Without one, the 5% is uncapped. Always disclose.
- **Sell HSGM to a prospect with an existing IP without checking CPF Healthcare Dashboard.** One MediSave-approved IP per Insured at any time - the system auto-terminates the old on the back-end. Verify first.
