## How to use this module

**For the learner:** tick days in the Progress Tracker as you complete them. Mon–Sat cadence; Sunday rest. Each practice day needs a Loom submission + logged weekly KPI before Week N+1 unlocks. Day 60 ends with a signed 12-month plan — no Week 11, the next 10 months are execution mode.

**For the mentor:** see [[MENTOR-PLAYBOOK|Mentor Playbook]] — condensed onboarding guide covering every practice-day submission gate, what to critique at each weekly review, and the 9 Loom artefacts you'll see over the 60 days.

**For production (internal):**
1. **[[STRUCTURE|STRUCTURE.md]]** — week arc, KPIs, coverage matrix, resolved decisions, cross-link map, branding policy.
2. **Day 1 is the voice reference** — all subsequent days match its tone, length (~900–1100 words), and structure.
3. **Fan-out is complete** — all 60 days + 10 READMEs shipped; cross-links verified (QA sweep: 456 wikilinks, 38 mermaid blocks, frontmatter + structural conventions all clean).
4. **Guest-speaker source** — D58 synthesises from the 3 guest-speaker transcripts in `_source-holos-transcripts/`.
