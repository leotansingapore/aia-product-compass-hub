# Week 6: Reading people with DISC

*Stop selling the way you buy. This week covers the 5-minute speed-read, then an opener and a close for each profile.*

DISC is a conversation aid built on William Moulton Marston's 1928 model of four behaviour patterns. Use it to adjust how you talk to people. It isn't a validated personality test, so treat every read as a guess you keep checking.

---

## Week 6 KPI

> 4-profile self-assessment + 4 recorded roleplays. Each roleplay includes the opener and the matching close for that profile.

Week 7 unlocks when all 4 roleplays, the written self-assessment, the Day 36 Loom and a 15-minute mentor review are done.

## Unlock rule for Week 7

Week 7 unlocks when you:

1. Record 4 separate 90-second roleplays, one per profile, each showing the opener and the matching close
2. Submit a written self-assessment naming your main and secondary profile and 2 gaps you need to adjust for
3. Record the Day 36 Loom reflection
4. Book a 15-minute mentor review

The mentor review is longer than usual because it's the first week your mentor watches footage with you.

---

## Sunday 30-minute review

Three prompts:

1. Which roleplay scored lowest on pace and energy? That's the adjustment you'll practise live in Week 7.
2. Which profile do you miss most often in real meetings? Usually it's your opposite on both axes (D and S, or I and C).
3. Pick 3 warm-market names for Week 7 practice, each a different profile from your own. You'll use them for the hot-button work.

---

## What changed in Week 6

DISC used to run across Weeks 6 and 7 (overview and 4 profiles, then speed-read and tailoring). The speed-read now sits in Day 31, and each profile day (32 to 35) includes that profile's opener and close. The content is the same, now grouped by profile in one week.

---

## What's coming in Week 7

Hot buttons and pitch mechanics. Week 7 pairs the profile read with the hot button, meaning what emotionally drives the buying decision. It also trains the full objection toolkit (Anchor-Disrupt-Ask, ART and the Iceberg), confident language and the 4 closes. By Saturday you'll have delivered 3 full pitches to 3 different profiles with 3 different hot buttons.

Week 7 KPI: 3 pitches delivered to 3 different DISC types with 3 different hot buttons.
