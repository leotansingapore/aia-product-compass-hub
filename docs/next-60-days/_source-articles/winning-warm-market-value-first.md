# Winning Over Your Warm Market

> **Don't explain why they should work with you. Show how you can help them.**

> **Scope:** This is the **follow-up** reframe (after silence on the first touch). For the **opening text** itself, see [[reaching-out-without-feeling-salesy]].

## The Follow-Up Trap

Initial outreach goes unanswered. Follow-up goes unanswered. You start questioning:
- *"Am I being too pushy?"*
- *"Why won't they even meet me?"*

The problem isn't the script. It's that you're **telling** instead of **demonstrating**.

## Dylan's Approach (Worked Example)

- **Specialty:** adulting + budgeting → cashflow analysis presentations
- **Follow-up that fails:** another text asking to meet
- **Follow-up that works:** shares his latest video — *shows* his expertise instead of claiming it

Result: dramatically higher response rates. Video does two things a text can't — elevates credibility and tangibly demonstrates value.

## Value-First Outreach

Stop obsessing over the follow-up sales script. Find multiple ways to deliver value through your specialty.

**Formats that work:**
- Short videos
- PDF guides
- Infographics
- Carousel posts
- Case studies

Medium matters less than value delivered.

> **Give value first. The meeting request becomes easy.**

---

**Teaching angle.** Reframe follow-up from *persistence* (more asks) to *generosity* (more value). Repeated asks feel pushy; value drops feel helpful.

**Reflection prompt.** *"List your top 3 specialties. For each, name one piece of value content (video, PDF, infographic) you could send as a follow-up instead of another 'can we meet?' message."*
