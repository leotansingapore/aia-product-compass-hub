# Instagram Selling Secrets — Eric Feng

> **Social media is the vehicle. Content is the fuel.**

Structured summary of Eric Feng's 118-slide "Instagram Selling Secrets" deck (2020). This file owns **IG strategy** (positioning, growth math, content types, DM funnel, story-selling). For IG execution (profile anatomy + 5 story categories + weekly calendar) see [[instagram-profile-and-stories]].

## Module 1 — Lay the foundation

### The 30-day goal (slides 2–5)

| Metric | Target | Driven by |
|---|---:|---|
| Followers | 1,000 | Unique positioning · Follower-growth strategy · Profile optimization |
| Conversations | 100 | IG engagement strategy · Content creation (feed + story) |
| Sales | 10 | Direct-messaging strategy |

### Respect the native language of each platform (slides 6–10)

| Platform | Modern-day analogue |
|---|---|
| Facebook | Family TV & forum |
| TikTok | Dance party |
| LinkedIn | Conference |
| YouTube | Network TV |
| **Instagram** | **Feed = magazine · Stories = reality TV** |

**Feed = magazine:** visual, trend-driven, bite-sized, aesthetic. **Stories = reality TV:** BTS moments, emotion, up-close, authentic.

### The mindset to win on IG (slides 11–12)

> **Be interesting. Be interested.** Instagram rewards interaction.

You'll do well on IG if: interesting life, willing to share, eye for design, strong interests → communities, you interact with followers, you commit time, target market skews younger (early 20s – mid 30s).

### Choose a focus — Social vs Authority (slides 13–18)

| Focus | Built on | Pros | Cons | How to execute |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Social** | Interest or Identity | Easier to grow the page · Audience knows & likes you | Harder to create sales conversations | Bio indicates profession; feed blends Passion × Profession; stories document your work |
| **Authority** | Topic or Market | Easier to create sales conversations · Audience knows & respects you | Harder to grow the page | Find unique positioning; great content + hashtags; stories stay relatable |

### Unique positioning — two approaches (slides 16–20)

**Approach 1: Vertical Mash-Up** — `Professional Subject × Your Own Twist`.
Examples: Personal Finance × Lifestyle (Budget Babe) · Personal Finance × Malaysian niche (KC Lau) · Sales × Luxurious Lifestyle (Dan Lok) · Sales × Colorful Service-based No-flaunting (Eric Feng).

**Approach 2: Horizontal Mash-Up** — `From Other Niche × Applied to Your Niche`.
Examples: Meme format (SGAG) × Insurance = funny insurance memes. Zodiac/Horoscope × Finance = money tips by zodiac.

## Module 2 — Follower-growth strategy

### Before you grow — clean up (slide 21)

Unfollow:
- Inactive accounts
- Uninspiring accounts
- Irrelevant accounts

Use "Least Interacted With" to prune. (slide 22)

### Warm-market bridge (slides 23–25)

Start with **existing customers + friends & family**. Opening line: **"Are you more active on Instagram?"** Use FB → IG Suggested (FB friends list inside IG search) + IG "Suggested For You" (Follows you / New to Instagram).

### Cold-market growth — 3-level framework (slides 26–33)

**Level 1 — discover via Social or Authority anchors:**
- **Key word** (e.g. `#gardeningsg`)
- **Location** (e.g. Chin Ling Nursery)
- **People** (e.g. `@terrypeh`)

**Level 2 — for each anchor, scan the Top 9 + Recent 9 posts.**

**Level 3 — action:**
1. Follow relevant accounts
2. Shortlist hashtags from their captions. **Target 30 hashtags** using this distribution:

| Follower size | # of hashtags |
|---|---:|
| >100K | 5 |
| 50K–100K | 5 |
| 20K–50K | 5 |
| 10K–20K | 5 |
| <10K | 10 |

**Daily budget: 30 minutes.**

## Module 3 — Profile optimization (slides 34–37)

Six elements (picture, one-liner, bio, link, highlights, modelling). Full anatomy + Why/What/How bio structure lives in [[instagram-profile-and-stories]]. Eric Feng's strategic angle:

- **One-liner** differs by focus — Authority states profession + who you help; Social states interest/identity.
- **Bio line** either teaches or connects. *"I teach service professionals how to attract quality customers using social media"* vs *"Looking to connect with fellow fitness enthusiasts to achieve goals faster."*
- **Model the good ones** — study profiles sharing similar interest, identity, topic, or market.

## Module 4 — Engagement strategy (before you post)

> **Every comment is a potential link back to your profile.** — Eric Feng

> **Want more followers? First, take care of your existing ones.**

### The 30-minutes-before-posting ritual (slides 42–51)

Three prongs, 30 min total:

| Prong | Who | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **30 hashtags** | Community hashtags | Engage Top 9 + Recent 9 (like, meaningful comment, reply to commenters). Follow new accounts. Update hashtags. |
| **30 competitors** | Accounts with similar market/industry (more followers than you) | Notifications ON. Be first to engage (like + meaningful comment). Engage their commenters. Share their content to stories. DM them. Use similar hashtags. |
| **30 active followers** | Followers who engaged your last 9 posts | Engage their latest post/stories (like, comment, reply to other commenters, share if valuable, DM if online). |

### Commenting — make it mean something (slide 42)

Respond to their CTA · share opinion/POV · share experience/story · reinforce (quote) · show reaction + explain why · ask a follow-up · tag someone valuable.

## Module 4 — Content creation strategy (feed)

### The feed-to-sales funnel (slide 53)

```
Feed    → Leads
Stories → Trust
Inbox   → Sales
```

### Four content types that do well (slide 54)

| Type | Best focus | Format cues |
|---|---|---|
| **Useful** | Authority | Carousel (<10 images, 9th slide = summary, last = CTA). 1-min videos (keep to 60 s, headline, subtitles, best part in first 5 s, repurpose interviews/Lives). Answer a question or solve a problem. |
| **Funny** | Social | Memes + GIFs (imgflip, trending). Social = insider joke; authority = express their pain. |
| **Relatable** | Social (can work for authority) | Story-based. Share emotion (happy / sad / surprising / funny / inspiring / frustrating / embarrassing). Establish common ground. Show vulnerability. |
| **Inspiring** | Authority | Inspiring moments (test on stories first) or quotes (unsplash / personal pics). Archetypes: David vs Goliath · No-one believed me · Victim to Victor · Doing the right thing · Positive impact on others. |

> **Consistency is key.**

### The 4-step content creation process (slides 63–75)

1. **Create the content** — purpose (Useful/Funny/Relatable/Inspiring) × type (single / carousel / video). Batch create. Consistency > perfection.
2. **Write the caption** — helps IG understand the post + drives interaction.
   - **Hook** — capital letters, emoji at the start.
   - **Explain** — give reasons, tell a story.
   - **Key takeaway** — lead with an emoji.
   - **Identify key actions** (optional) — bulleted with emojis.
   - **CTA** — ask a question / tag someone / reply with a word or emoji.
   - **More CTA** — "I post every day at Xpm (GMT). Follow @handle. Turn on notifications."
3. **Insert hashtags** — apply the 5/5/5/5/10 rule (table above). Mix keyword + community + ≤3 unique hashtags (your own).
4. **Post at best time** — refer to platform insights for your audience.

### Model after great content (slide 76)

30# · 30 competitors · 30 active followers → bookmark the most engaging posts → study why they worked → model for your audience.

## Module 4 — Story-Selling Strategy (the 6-story framework)

> **Story → Trust → Sales.** Stories work the familiarity effect — repeat exposure builds trust before the inbox asks for the sale.

### Seeds you plant across every story-sell (slide 96)

- **Seed 1:** Who you can help
- **Seed 2:** What problem you can solve
- **Seed 3:** You are in demand (social proof)

### The 6-box structure

```
┌─────┬────────────┬─────┬─────┬─────┬─────┬─────┬─────┐
│ 1   │ wait 3–6 h │ 2   │ 3   │ 4   │ 5   │ 6   │ 7/8 │
│ Poll│            │Story│Story│Story│T/T  │Proof│ CTA │
└─────┴────────────┴─────┴─────┴─────┴─────┴─────┴─────┘
```

1. **Poll** — problem / question / misconception. Wait 3–6 h. Post results to boost the first story in the follow-through.
2. **Story 1** — tell what happened (one point, image = 1,000 words).
3. **Story 2** — next point of the story.
4. **Story 3** — next point of the story.
5. **Teach or Tease + poll** — "Agree? Yes / No"
6. **Social proof** — client DM screenshot, testimonial.
7. **CTA** — DM sticker: "Who wants to watch the video?" / "Who needs help reviewing your critical-illness coverage?" with Me / Maybe-later options.

### PCSBTC — the written story-sell anchor (slide 97)

`Problem → Consequences → One-sentence Solution → Benefit → Testimonial → CTA`

Example (slide 97):
> *Yesterday I got on a zoom call with a customer — an engineer with XXX. He told me COVID-19 gave him a preview of what retiring with little money looks like. [**Consequences:** no-pay leave, pay cut, sky-high expenses]. During our call, I showed him a **simple but powerful strategy** and left him with clarity and peace of mind. Here's what he said after: [**testimonial**]. If you have the same problem, **PM me.**"*

### Worked example — Ted Mundy critical illness (slides 107–113)

1. Poll: "Have you watched this video?" (Yes / No) + 3 relevant hashtags
2. Story 2–4: each adds one point (one per frame), image-heavy
3. Story 5: "Critical illnesses can happen to anyone in spite of all attempts to stay healthy. Agree? Yes / No"
4. Story 6–7: social proof — DM screenshot from a client who resonated
5. Story 8: DM sticker CTA: "Who wants to watch the video?" + "Who needs help reviewing your critical-illness coverage? Me / Maybe later"

### Traffic mechanics (slides 100–101)

Each story's view count decays ~5–10% per frame. The poll at box 1 boosts the first follow-up story by doubling its reach (100 → 50 decay becomes 200 → 100). **That's the reason for the 3–6-hour wait.**
