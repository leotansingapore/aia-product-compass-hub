# Social Proof Scripts + CAR Sales Cycle

> **Field-tested word-for-word scripts** from a practicing advisor: a social-proof close, a 10-name referral ask, and the CAR framework for scaling a self-regenerating pipeline.

---

## Part 1 — The Social Proof Closing Script

> *Used word-for-word to boost closing ratio.*

**Setup:** Bring a stack of paper. Each sheet = one real client.

**Delivery:**

> *"I wanna share with you this thing. Basically I've been doing this for years, and I have had hundreds of clients all buying the same plan.*
>
> *For example — this guy A, referred this guy B. B got the plan, and referred all his secondary school friends. B referred C, C got the plan and referred D, D got the plan and referred E."*
>
> *(keep flipping papers as you name each referral link)*
>
> *"And this is a never-ending chain that goes on (flip) and on (flip) and on. All of them got the same plan I'm sharing with you today. Because they like the returns, the flexibility, and they like me and trust me, and even recommend to their friends. I stay with them for many years.*
>
> *Until this very last guy (at the end of the stack) — who referred his navy officer platoon to me. And out of the whole section, almost all of them got the plan."*

**Why it works:** **Consensus (Cialdini)** — the prospect sees that dozens of people they conceptually resemble already said yes. The physical paper stack anchors the claim; flipping sustains attention; the navy-officer-platoon close raises status.

## Part 2 — The Referral Asking Script

> *Used word-for-word — produces ≥10 referrals 95% of the time via social proof.*

**Trigger:** After close, during admin paperwork.

**Delivery:**

> *"Okay, so right now I'll be doing some admin procedures — it'll take 5 to 10 minutes. So while I'm doing this, what you can do in the meantime is this.*
>
> *Everyone comes down, without fail, they will always do this — 100% of the time. They will ALWAYS write down at least 10 names on this. They like me, trust me, support me. And they also want to help their friend. Because it's no harm coming down, learning about something.*
>
> *They write down their platoon mates, BMT mates, schoolmates, poly mates. Anyone they know — even if you're not close, just spam and write as much as you can. They write in front and behind. 10 here, 10 here, 10 here — in fact they write more. 20, 20, 20, 15, 20, 20. All the time they write at least 15 or more, because they feel it's quite beneficial.*
>
> *So while I'm doing this for you, you can also write down at least 10 or more names, and you can rest assured I'll do my best to help them."*

**Why it works:**
- **Consensus** — *"everyone does this, 100% of the time"*
- **Reciprocity** — you're doing admin *for them* right now
- **Time arbitrage** — productive use of otherwise-dead paperwork minutes
- **Specificity anchoring** — *"20, 20, 20, 15, 20, 20"* normalises the high end

## Part 3 — Optimizing the Sales Cycle

### Two Cycle States

|---|---|
| **Slow sales cycle** | Where every new advisor starts. Only bottleneck: *how to get leads.* |
| **Self-regenerating sales cycle** | Where you *must* eventually arrive. Leads generate leads automatically via referrals. |

## Part 4 — The CAR Bottleneck Framework

Once you're in the **efficient sales cycle**, you only have **3 bottlenecks**:

**C**alling · **A**ppointments · **R**eferrals

Each is a bottleneck for the others:

| Broken link | Cascade |
|---|---|
| **Not enough calls** | → fewer appointments → fewer referrals → fewer leads to call |
| **Few appointments** | → fewer referrals → fewer leads → fewer referrals |
| **Few referrals** | → fewer leads → fewer appointments → fewer referrals |

**Diagnostic rule:** Find the weakest of C / A / R this week. Fix that one. Don't scatter-shot.

## Part 5 — Tips to Optimize Each Bottleneck

### Calling Tips
1. **Use two phones** to call — doubles your dial rate, easy to set up.
2. **Only call** during calling hours — don't do any other stuff. Golden hours are precious.
3. **Edit your CRM and text clients *only after* golden hours are done.**

### Closing Ratio Tips
1. **Cialdini's 6 Principles** — Authority, Liking, Social Proof, Consistency, Scarcity, Reciprocity
2. **Social proof using referrals** — the paper-chain close above
3. **SPIN Selling** — the benefit of your solution must outweigh the cost

### Referral Asking Tips
1. Social proof. Social proof. Social proof. Social proof. Social proof.

**Teaching angle:** Most new advisors know *what* to say ("ask for referrals") but not the *exact phrasing* or the *moment* to deploy it. This doc's power is the **word-for-word timing** — the paper flip, the 5–10 min admin window. Make them memorize both.

**Pattern signature (for the referral ask):**
trigger (admin wait) → consensus (*"everyone writes 10+"*) → specificity (*"20, 20, 15"*) → reciprocity (*"while I do this for you"*) → permission-lowered close (*"rest assured I'll do my best"*)

**Reflection prompt:** *"This week, before your next signed case closes — (1) print 30 sheets representing your last 30 clients in chain order, (2) rehearse the paper-flip close aloud 3× in front of a mirror, (3) deliver it live at your next proposal meeting."*
