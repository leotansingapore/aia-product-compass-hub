# C.R.A.B. — Handling Blue Ticks & Ignored Follow-Ups

> Use when a prospect reads but doesn't reply, rescheduled more than once, or went silent after initial interest.

**C**are → **R**eason → **A**lleviate → **B**ook

---

## C — Care
Show the message is about *them*, not you.

- Acknowledge their season of life (new job, kids, travel, stress)
- Reference specifics that show attention
- Avoid pushy / transactional tone

> *"I know you've just started your new role — hope the first few weeks are going smoothly."*
> *"Congrats again on the promotion! That must come with a lot of moving pieces."*

## R — Reason
Give a compelling reason *for them* to reply.

- Tie to a benefit they already expressed interest in
- Use what they previously said to make it relevant
- What's in it for them, not you

> *"If we sort this out now, it'll take something off your plate as you settle in."*
> *"Helps ensure we don't miss the window to implement before your travel."*

## A — Alleviate
Reduce friction. Make action easy.

- Remove the need to "think" or check calendars
- Offer flexible locations/times/online
- Pre-empt time, effort, travel objections

> *"Happy to meet at your office during lunch or after work nearby."*
> *"We can do this over Zoom if your week's packed."*

## B — Book
Clear CTA, minimal effort.

- 2–3 specific date/time slots
- Format so they reply A / B / C
- Don't leave open-ended

> *"Would any of these work?"*
> *(A) Tue 23 Jan @ 12pm*
> *(B) Wed 24 Jan @ 6pm*
> *(C) Mon 27 Jan @ 6pm*
> *Just reply A, B or C and I'll handle the rest.*

---

## Full Sample Message

> *"Hey Kelly, just bumping this up.*
>
> *I know things have been really busy with your new promotion — congrats by the way!*
>
> *I was thinking if we wrap up the planning portion this week, it'll free up mental space for you to focus on work, and help us make progress on the concerns you raised earlier.*
>
> *I can swing by your office during lunch, or meet you after work somewhere convenient — whatever works best for you.*
>
> *Would any of these work?*
> *(A) 23 Jan, Thu @ 12pm*
> *(B) 24 Jan, Fri @ 6pm*
> *(C) 27 Jan, Mon @ 6pm*
>
> *Just reply A, B, or C and I'll take care of it."*

---

## Best Practices

- **Personalize** — reference past conversation details
- **Warm but professional** — not robotic, not overfamiliar
- **Don't spam** — use after *one* ignored follow-up, not five
- **3–5 day gap** before next variation if no response

## When to Use

- Message read, no reply
- Rescheduled more than once
- Communication gap after initial positive response

## Track

- **Response rate** — replies within 24–48h of CRAB send
- **Conversion rate** — % of replies that result in a booked appointment

---

**Teaching angle:** CRAB is the *single* script for every ignored follow-up. The ABC booking format (vs. open-ended "when works?") materially lifts reply rates.

**Reflection prompt:** *"Pull one prospect who blue-ticked you in the last 30 days. Draft a CRAB message for them right now."*
