# Market Survey — Full Warm-Market Prospecting Flow

> **Minimum 30 surveys to complete before application phase.**
> Memorise, internalise, naturalise.

Two survey variants depending on career status, a phone script, objection handlers, and the four pre-FHR promises.

---

## The Survey Form — Two Variants

Both are 5-minute, 4-question surveys. Only Q4 changes based on candidate status.

### Survey 1 — For Candidates *Still Thinking* of Joining
Opening line: *"I am currently interning with AIA and I might consider financial services as a career."*

### Survey 2 — For Candidates *Already Intending* to Join
Opening line: *"I am undergoing training to become a licensed financial advisor."*

### Shared Q1–Q3

1. **Do you have any insurance policies / financial plans?** (Yes / No) — if No, why?
2. **When was the last time you did a review of your policies?**
3. **How do you feel about your existing consultant's service?**

### Q4 — The Appointment Ask

**Survey 1 (thinking):**
> *"If I choose to do this, I'd like to extend my services to my closest friends. You're one of them. Will there be a possibility of meeting you for a Financial Health Review if I'm successfully hired?"*

**Survey 2 (in-training):**
> *"As part of my training, I'm tasked to conduct Financial Health Reviews. Would it be alright to meet for a 30-min session to share the work I do?"*
> *(Relate the promises: will not force, future meetings depend on value.)*

### Contact Capture (after Yes)

Full name · Address · Email · Contact · DOB · Best time to meet · Place.

## The Phone Script

### OPENING
> *"Hello, good morning, is this John? John, this is… do you have a moment to speak?"*
> *"How are you this period?"* ← rapport build

### AGENDA
> *"As you may have learnt, I'm intending to embark on the career of a Financial Advisor [or: currently interning with a financial services agency] and I'm tasked to do a simple market survey with people I know. Could you help me with 5 minutes of your time?"*

→ Proceed with Q1, Q2, Q3.

### SETTING THE APPOINTMENT (Using Q4)
> *"Thanks XXX. By the way, when I'm officially licensed [or: if I decide to embark on this practice], one of my priorities is to extend my service to my closest friends. You're one of those I highly regard.*
>
> *May I get in touch for a 30-minute session to share the type of solutions I can offer? You have my assurance I will not ask you to do anything you don't want to, and any future meetings will depend entirely on whether you feel my services would be of help. Is that fair?"*

**Then tie down logistics:**
- *"Would it be best at your office, your home, or Zoom?"*
- *"Weekdays or weekends?"*
- *"Office hours or evenings?"*
- *"Could I see you on XXXday at XX, or would XXXday at XX be better?"*

### CLOSING
> *"Really appreciate your help. Will it be alright if I can keep you posted of any promotions relevant to you? May I have your email and DOB so I can connect better?"*
>
> *(Optional: "May I have your permission to add you on Instagram / FB?")*

## Objection Handlers (Phone Stage)

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| **Not interested** | *"I can understand not being interested in something you haven't had an opportunity to see. So you can judge for yourself, would you be free for a short time on…"* |
| **Not in the market** | *"I'd have been surprised if you said you were in the market for life insurance right now. However, I have ideas that'll be handy when you're ready. Would you be free…"* |
| **No money** | *"I can understand holding down unnecessary expenses. However, I think you'd find what I have to say of interest — and of course, you'd be under no obligation. Would you be free…"* |
| **No need** | *"Of course, you'd be the sole judge of whether this idea is of value to you. Since it'll take only a short time to explain, would you be free…"* |
| **Too busy** | *"I guessed you'd be busy — that's why I telephoned for an appointment rather than calling on the off-chance. Would you be free…"* |
| **Wasting your time** | *"On the chance you'll see value in the idea, I'd be happy to spend the time with you. Would you be free…"* |
| **What's the idea?** | *"To explain it properly, I'll need to show you some illustrations and discuss personally. Would you be free…"* |
| **Is it insurance?** | *"It's the protection of your family / a savings plan with a difference that I'd like to talk about. With some illustrations I'm sure you'll find interesting. Would you be free…"* |
| **Post it out** | *"Happy to — but what I have in mind will only be useful if tailored to your individual needs. That's why I'd like to see you in person. Would you be free…"* |
| **For the obstinate objector** | *"Could we leave it this way — if I'm in your neighbourhood in the next few months, I'd like to drop in and say hello. If you have a few moments then, I'll be glad to show you what I have in mind."* |

**Pattern:** acknowledge → reframe → re-ask for the meeting. Every handler ends with *"Would you be free…"*

## The ABCD Four Promises (Before Starting FHR)

Deliver these four promises at the top of the Financial Health Review meeting:

| Letter | Promise | Script |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Assurance** | *"This meeting is not to sell you anything. I'm here to find out about you and your goals. If we proceed with a proposal, it's because I shared something of value and you felt comfortable with me."* |
| **B** | **Best Interest** | *"If we do proceed to the proposal stage, you have my promise that what I plan for you is exactly what I'd do for myself if I were in your shoes."* |
| **C** | **Confidentiality** | *"Whatever we share today will be kept in strictest confidence."* |
| **D** | **Disclosure** | *"With that in mind, I hope you can share as much as you can so I can make my best recommendation."* |

**Teaching angle.** Market Survey bridges "just entered industry" → "calendar of real FHR meetings." Ethical, low-pressure reason to call the entire warm market. The 30-survey minimum builds both the muscle *and* the pipeline.

**Drill discipline.** *Memorise → Internalise → Naturalise.* Reading off a page loses rapport instantly.

**Reflection prompt.** *"Pick 5 names from your Project 100. Decide which Market Survey variant (1 or 2) fits your career status. Book one call this week."*
