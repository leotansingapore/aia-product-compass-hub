# 5 Pillars for Cold Prospecting on Social Media

> Warm prospects have context. Cold prospects have none — you must capture attention, establish credibility, and nurture interest in a small window.

---

## Pillar 1 — Define a Highly Targeted Audience

If you market to everyone, you market to no one.

Narrow to a **specific niche**. Not "business owners" — **tech entrepreneurs** or **fitness coaches**. Specificity = credibility, authority, conversion.

## Pillar 2 — Craft a Compelling Value Proposition

Cold prospects don't care about your backstory. They care about *"why should I pay attention to you?"*

Avoid generic ("I help people grow their wealth"). Introduce a **named framework** — makes it memorable and differentiated.

**Examples:**
- **P.I.S.A** — Protect · Income · Scale · Assets
- **F.A.T** — Foundation · Accelerate · Transfer

## Pillar 3 — Optimize Your Social Media Profile

Your profile = your online storefront. Trust must land within seconds.

- **Showcase your framework** or expertise in the bio
- **Credibility indicators** — client results, industry achievements, media features
- **Social proof** — testimonials, case studies
- **Stay active** — post valuable insights at least 2×/week
- **Remove unprofessional content** — maintain polish and authority

## Pillar 4 — Craft Sharp, Value-Driven Messages

Cold DMs must be **concise, relevant, engaging**. No long-winded intros, no hard sell.

The best cold messages:
- Address a **known pain point** of your niche
- Offer **value upfront** — free ebook, industry report, insight
- Feel **warm, human, insightful** — not spammy

On LinkedIn especially, clarity + brevity + immediate value beats volume.

## Pillar 5 — Follow Up Consistently

Most cold prospects don't reply on round 1 — not disinterest, just busy / missed it. A structured follow-up sequence can **double or triple** response rate.

**Rules:**
- Don't spam *"just checking in"*
- Each follow-up must carry **new value** — an insight, a trend reference, a relevant resource
- **Mix formats** — text, voice note, short video — to stand out

---

## The Core Principle

> **Cold prospecting isn't about who sends the most messages. It's about who engages the right people in the right way.**

---

**Teaching angle:** Cold-market counterpart to warm scripts. Cold and warm use **opposite** emotional registers — warm leads with vulnerability & friendship, cold leads with authority & framework. Advisors often mix them up and fail on both.

**Critical contrast vs warm market:**
| Dimension | Warm | Cold |
|---|---|---|
| Opener | Personal reference | Niche pain point |
| Credibility | Existing relationship | Framework + social proof |
| Tone | Vulnerable, honest | Professional, value-first |
| Ask | Easy out, no pressure | Clear CTA with value hook |
| Follow-up | Texting EQ flow | Multi-format value drip |

**Reflection prompt:** *"Pick one niche you could realistically serve. Write a named framework (3–5 letters) that describes your process. Rewrite your IG bio using it tonight."*
