# 5 Questions Prospects Silently Ask Before They Message You

Every cold prospect runs through these five checks before engaging. Your content either answers them or loses the lead.

---

## 1. Can I Trust That Others Value Your Work?

Social proof. Testimonials must feel authentic and structured.

**Use the pre-during-post format:**
> *"Before working with me, Ryan felt lost about his finances. During our consultation, we built a clear strategy tailored to his needs. After implementing the plan, he now has a structured roadmap to financial security."*

**Strengthen with:** screenshots, video testimonials, real success stories.

## 2. Do You Actually Know Your Stuff?

Show expertise via **real client challenges**, broken down with practical advice. Avoid vague jargon.

**If you're new:** frame insights as "my perspective" rather than authority claims. You deliver value without overreaching.

## 3. Why Are You Doing This?

Skepticism toward advisors is default — prospects assume you're in it for commission. Break it with your **genuine purpose**:
- What defining moments led you here?
- What personal experiences shaped your financial philosophy?

Authentic storytelling → emotional connection → you become a person, not a salesperson.

## 4. Will We Get Along?

Trust = expertise + connection. Share your personality, interests, quirks. Travel, fitness, personal development — whatever you care about.

Attracts aligned prospects. Repels mismatches. Both are wins.

## 5. How Can I Approach You Without Feeling Awkward?

Most people hesitate because they don't know how to start.

**Use an offer post** — a soft CTA embedded in a testimonial:
> *"After implementing this strategy, Sarah now feels financially secure and confident about her future. If you want to explore how this approach can work for you, DM me 'plan' to learn more."*

Removes friction. The prospect has a word to send — no awkward opener required.

---

## The Payoff

Answer all five → you become **trustworthy, knowledgeable, purpose-driven, relatable, approachable**. Social presence becomes a lead-gen machine.

## Content pillar mapping

| Question | Content type |
|---|---|
| 1. Others value? | Testimonial posts (pre-during-post format) |
| 2. Know stuff? | Educational / breakdown posts |
| 3. Why doing this? | Origin story / founder posts |
| 4. Get along? | Lifestyle / personality posts |
| 5. Easy to approach? | Offer posts with DM keyword CTA |

## Reflection prompt

Audit your last 10 posts. Which of the 5 questions does each answer? Which question is your content ignoring? (Most advisors answer Q2 only and ignore Q3–5.)
