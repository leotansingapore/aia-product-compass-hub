# Your Next 60 Days — Final Structure (draft v1)

**Audience.** Newly-joined FCs — they finished the FINternship, passed CMFAS,
signed the contract. They are licensed and their income is $0 until they close
their first case. **This module is the first 60 days of their real business.**

**Cadence.** 10 weeks × 6 days (Mon–Sat). Sunday is a rest day. Matches
`first-60-days`.

**Total.** 60 days of structured content.

**Coverage (source material).**
- [[_source-holos-transcripts|HOLOS YouTube transcripts (20 sessions)]] — primary curriculum source.
- [[_source-holos-decks|HOLOS slide decks (14 decks)]] — structural backbone for each HOLOS lesson.
- [[_source-articles|Source articles (15 pieces)]] — Leo-authored + ingested third-party frameworks covering warm-market scripts, prospecting taxonomy, database segmentation + revenue math, content creation, IG profile/stories, DM funnel, texting EQ, cold prospecting, social-proof closing, full sales-prospecting-referral training workbook.
- Pulled selectively from `../first-60-days/` for continuity (Project 100, DISC basics, Closing).

---

## Design principles

1. **Execution, not theory.** They already sat through the FINternship. This
   module measures *behaviour*, not comprehension. Every week has a KPI, not
   just a quiz.
2. **One practice day per week** (Day 6, 12, 18…). The "content" of that day
   is a live drill the FC must submit before the quiz unlocks.
3. **Scripts included, not referenced.** Intent statement, referral ask, DM
   follow-up, objection responses — the scripts live *in* the day files, not
   in a separate playbook.
4. **A weekly activity target** sits in the week header. Without it, we're
   teaching a course — with it, we're running a business.
5. **Day 60 = the 12-month plan,** not a final exam. Graduation = rhythms
   locked + first few closed cases + referral loop started.

## Resolved decisions (locked 2026-04-20)

1. **Cold open — Day 1.** Bridge from first-60-days Day 60. Open the day acknowledging the FINternship is over and the real business starts now. For FCs who skipped the FINternship, the bridge reads as context, not a prerequisite.
2. **Practice-day submissions.** Every practice day (D6, D12, D18, D24, D30, D36, D42, D48, D54) ends with a **Loom link field** inside the reflection worksheet. No external app, no mentor form — just a Loom URL pasted into the day's worksheet.
3. **KPI gating — hard gate.** Week N+1 is locked until the Week N KPI is logged. Each week's KPI sits in the week header (already drafted). Day 6/12/18/… practice submission + KPI log together unlock the next week.
4. **Guest-speaker material (D58).** Not a "listing of videos." The 3 HOLOS guest-speaker transcripts are **source material** for a synthesized lesson: *"How a top producer actually runs a week."* Same format as every other day (big idea, LOs, content, worksheet, quiz). Transcripts are mined for quotes, examples, routines — not linked out to.
5. **D55 Policy Restructuring.** No MAS compliance add-on. HOLOS tactical tone is authoritative. The transcript already covers ethics inline (Don's framing on suitability); we preserve voice.
6. **Linkage to first-60-days.** Soft mention only — Day 1 frontmatter includes `prerequisite: "[[../first-60-days/week-10/day-60]]"`. No body callout, no "read this first" banner. Keeps Day 1 readable as a standalone.

## Generation conventions (derived from decisions above)

- **Frontmatter additions for every day** beyond the base template:
  - `kpi_link:` — the weekly KPI this day feeds (e.g. `"30 outreaches; 5 appointments booked"`)
  - `practice_submission:` (practice days only) — `"loom"` flag
  - `prerequisite:` (Day 1 only) — `"[[../first-60-days/week-10/day-60]]"`
  - `see_also:` (where applicable) — a list of wikilinks to related first-60-days or other next-60-days days that materially extend/prerequisite the concept. **Not a body callout** — frontmatter only, renders in metadata view. Leaner than linking in prose.
- **Practice-day worksheet field** — every practice day ends with:
  ```
  ## Submission
  Paste your Loom URL here: _________________________________
  ```
- **Week header template** — every week-N/README.md opens with the KPI target as a bold callout, then the 6 day wikilinks.
- **Unlock rule copy** — consistent across all week summaries: *"Next week unlocks when you (1) submit your Loom for this week's practice day, and (2) log this week's KPI."*

Day files are **hybrid AIA-specific / agency-agnostic**. Full rule in memory at `feedback_next_60_days_branding.md`. Quick version:
