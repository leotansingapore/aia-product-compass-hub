# HOLOS Deck — Hot Buttons

Extracted from `Hot Buttons_FINternship.pptx` (12 slides).

## Slide 1 — HOT BUTTONS

- How to identify and capitalise on them

## Slide 2 — WHAT ARE

- HOT BUTTONS?
- Something very important to a person, that is often a source of motivation behind their decision-making

## Slide 3 — ARE ALL HOT BUTTONS POSITIVE?

- No. Hot Buttons can be negative as well.
- In fact, something that might be a positive Hot Button to person A, can very well be a negative Hot Button to 	person B. Examples: Roller Coasters, Horror Films, A certain type of food etc.

## Slide 4 — THE RIGHT START

- "Personal production of financial advisors
- during the first 90 days has a direct correlation with their future success.”
- -Kinder Brothers
- DIFFERENT CATEGORIES OF HOT BUTTONS
- Loved ones
- Dreams/Goals
- Pride/Hobby/Skill
- Faith/Religion
- Needs VS Reality
- Values/Beliefs
- Bad Experiences/Fear

## Slide 5 — HOW TO IDENTIFY HOT BUTTONS?

- By asking relevant Hot Button questions
- By observing facial expressions, tonality, and energy changes when the Hot Buttons are surfaced
- Change in gaze/eyes
- Change in emotions/energy state
- Change in body language
- Change in tonality/pacing

## Slide 6 — EXAMPLES OF HOT BUTTON QUESTIONS (SOCIAL)

- What is one thing you can’t live without?
- What’s the single biggest fear you have in life?
- What’s something that keeps you up at night?
- What ongoing thing in your life frustrates you the most?
- What are the top few criteria you have when considering a travel destination?
- What are the top 3 things you look out for in a partner?
- If you could relive one experience you had in the past, what would it be?
- If you have/had a bucket list, what would be the #1 thing on the list that’s non-negotiable for you to complete?
- What are some personal values or beliefs you have that you’d never compromise?

## Slide 7 — EXAMPLES OF HOT BUTTON QUESTIONS (SALES)

- Suppose we never got you started on FP, what are the worst implications that you can imagine for your future self?
- If we manage to achieve all your financial goals that we set out to, how would you envision your dream retirement to look like?
- If I may ask, what are the top 3 things that matter the most to you when it comes to working with an advisor?
- Who would you be worried about most if you were prematurely gone one day?
- What’s the worst experience you had with financial planning or a financial advisor?
- What do you think is the most critical component of every great portfolio?

## Slide 8 — SUPPOSE YOU MANAGE TO IDENTIFY THE RIGHT BUTTONS, WHAT NEXT?

- You want to HIT those buttons as hard as possible, because we all know that action-taking is derived from emotions, seldom logic.

## Slide 9 — SUPPOSE YOU MANAGE TO IDENTIFY THE RIGHT BUTTONS, WHAT NEXT?

- To hit the right Buttons, follow this 3 part Framework-
- Part 1: Gather Intel
- Ask all relevant HB questions
- Make a conscious mental note of all the hot buttons surfaced, especially the HOTTEST ones which you’ll want to spend more effort on
- Part 2: Preparation
- Create a great angle to smoothly position your solution/product to help them achieve/avoid their buttons
- Align the benefits of the solution to fit the Hot Buttons
- Part 3: Presentation
- Tie everything up and present seamlessly. Present all the benefits to HIT all Hot Buttons
- Address and anticipate all objections

## Slide 10 — SUPPOSE YOU MANAGE TO IDENTIFY THE RIGHT BUTTONS, WHAT NEXT?

- How to specifically achieve Part 3:The Execution
- Part 1: 4 steps + Application of the Technique
- Step 1: Framing
- Step 2: More framing
- Step 3: Apply Technique
- Step 4: Motivate into action-taking
- Technique
- Visualisation. Example: “Imagine if…”, “If you could think ahead 20 years from now..”
- Make prospect the subject of the story
- Ask the RIGHT questions
