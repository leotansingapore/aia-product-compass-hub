## Slide 2 — IMPORTANCE OF ASKING THE “RIGHT QUESTIONS”

- Open the mind / Trigger interests
- Identify Priorities
- Identify the underlying objection / Find out the truth
- Overcome an objection
- Motivate prospects to take actions
- Get the person to agree with you
- Generate Positive response
- Shift the attention
- Get the person to self-reflect on something
- Closing / To Test Wate

## Slide 3 — EXAMPLE OF ASKING THE RIGHT QUESTIONS

- Question
- If a prospect asks you – “Why should I work with you?” Or “How do I know you are the right advisor to work with?”
- Answer:
- “Mr. Prospect, to date, I have more than 100 clients and at least 70% of them were once approached by other FAs to offer their services. And yet, no client of mine has left to work with someone else. All of them still stick to me all these years. What do you think could be a good reason why is that so?”

## Slide 4 — 6 GOLDEN RULES OF ASKING THE RIGHT QUESTIONS

- Rule Number 1:
- – Focus on Key Word(s) of the prospect’s answer, to include them in your next question to clarify.
- Eg: Prospect says “Bro, to be honest, I don’t believe in insurance at all, I think it’s a scam and waste of money”
- Answer: “Ok, if you don’t mind me asking, why do you not believe in insurance?” or “Thanks for letting me know, may I know the reason why is that so?”

## Slide 5 — Rule Number 2:

- – Offer few Logical Possibilities in your question, to help prospect answer more easily (towards what you want) Eg: Prospect says “Err, let me think about it” or “Err, I think its ok, I don’t really want / I’m not comfortable” First Part Answer (Using Rule Number 1 and Rule Number 2 together): “Ok, if you don’t mind me asking, which aspect of my recommendation do you need to think more about?” or “Thanks for letting me know, if I may ask, what is the one thing that you’re not comfortable about?” Second Part Answer: “Is it the monthly commitment? Or do you have other constraints that you haven’t shared with me?

## Slide 6 — Rule Number 3:

- – Use more contrast and impactful choices of words to enhance your question Example 1: “Wouldn’t you agree the earlier you start, the better it is?”
- VS
- “Wouldn’t you agree, we don’t have many years in our lives that we can afford to waste? Since our time is precious, why not we start early so that you can taste the fruits of your labour earlier ahead of your peers?”
- Example 2: “Putting your money in the bank earns you low interest, you shouldn't do that”
- VS
- “Does it really make sense to save your hard-earned cash in the bank, when interest rates less than inflation? Are you aware that you are self-sabotaging your retirement goals by doing so?”

## Slide 7 — Rule Number 4:

- – Use 2 very distinctive and contrasting options to lead prospect to choose the option you wish for him to say yes or no
- Eg: “If you were to choose, would you rather be financially burdened by hospital bills that will potentially wipe out your life savings or transfer this risk to a 3 rd party at a small fraction of the costs?
- Add on: You have always come across as a wise and sensible individual, I’m sure you know that the latter makes more sense right?

## Slide 8 — Rule Number 5:

- – Use Non-Committal words in your questions
- Eg: “Do you think I am the right person for the job?
- VS
- “Do you think there is a possibility that, I might be the right person for the job?
- Non-Committal Words: May, might, possibility, possible, can, could, is there a chance, etc.
- AVOID: Must be, is, will, would, should be, etc.

## Slide 9 — Rule Number 6:

- – Quote perspective of respectable others/experts that has differing view from him
- Eg: Prospect says “I don’t want to invest with insurance companies, ILPs are not good”
- Series of Right Questions –
- ✓ “Why do you think ILPs are not good?”
- ✓ “Would you agree investment bankers are experts in the field of investments?”
- ✓ “Would you agree that such investment connoisseurs/professionals would not have ILPs in their personal investment portfolio if they are not good?”
- ✓ “I have investment bankers that are my clients and I’m currently managing their ILP’s portfolio, what do you think could be one possible reason why is that so?”

## Slide 10 — CHECKLIST - TO CONFIRM IT IS THE RIGHT QUESTION

- Does the question lead you to your OBJECTIVE?
- 01
- Is it SPECIFIC?
- Is it LOGICAL and INDISPUTABLE?
- 02
- 03
- If you satisfy the above checklist, you are
- asking a good question!

## Slide 11 — PRACTICE ACTIVITY - ROLEPLAY 1

- Form yourself into pairs
- 1 will play the role of the consultant and the other will be the client
- A Prospect says: “I am not interested in any savings or investment plans”
- What would be the right question to ask?

## Slide 12 — PRACTICE ACTIVITY - ROLEPLAY 1

- Answer: “If I may ask, why are you not interested in investments or savings plans?”
- “Is it because of bad experience from the past investments? Or are you simply a traditional person who likes to save money in the bank?”
- Combination of Golden Rule 1 & 2

## Slide 13 — PRACTICE ACTIVITY - ROLEPLAY 2

- A Prospect says: “I already have an FA. I don’t need more”
- What would be the right question to ask?

## Slide 14 — PRACTICE ACTIVITY - ROLEPLAY 2

- Answer: “I am not surprised that you already have a consultant, most of my clients also mentioned that when we first met.”
- “However, I’m letting you know that I have no intention to replace him at all.”
- “Wouldn’t you agree when it comes to insurances, it is merely buying a ‘Peace of Mind?’”
- “Since it is for a ‘Peace of mind’ and this is something you can’t risk going wrong, surely you agree that you have nothing to lose by hearing a second opinion, correct?”
