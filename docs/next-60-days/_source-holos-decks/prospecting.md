## Slide 11 — SMALL TALK + ASK FOR FAVOUR

- You : …..small talk (common ground/ hot buttons)….. Could I ask you for a favour please?
- Friend : What is it regarding?
- You : It’s actually something I have been struggling to approach you for, as I wish to do the right thing but yet do not wish to jeopardize our friendship. May I ask you for a chance to help you review your financial portfolio? I understand you might already have a few advisors you are working with. However, I'm sure you also agree that everyone will have different forte to offer? I am rather confident to be able to value-add to your portfolio.

## Slide 12 — REDUCE FEAR OF COMMITMENT

- However you have my assurance, I will be absolutely professional and objective with my review should you give me this opportunity.
- If there are any critical gaps that requires your attention, I shall bring them to your attention and suggest possible solutions to address them, and it’s your decision to take my suggestions or not.
- On the other hand, if your current planning is already very adequate, I will also be transparent and share that with you.

## Slide 13 — REDUCE FEAR OF COMMITMENT

- Please don’t extend this opportunity to me out of mere support, I am hoping you could see me as a professional advisor who might have something good to value-add to you.
- Would that be ok? So would you be available to meet in the evening next Monday or Tuesday, at ….?
