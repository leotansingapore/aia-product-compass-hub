## Slide 7 — WHAT HAPPENS TO MY CPF SAVINGS AT AGE 55?

- When you turn 55, we will transfer your CPF savings, up to the Full Retirement Sum (FRS), to create your RA.
- For members who have set aside the FRS in the RA, the remaining will be channelled to the Ordinary Account (OA). ( Budget 2024.

## Slide 8 — WHAT HAPPENS TO MY CPF SAVINGS AT AGE 55?

- If you do not have enough in your OA or SA to reach the FRS but have used your CPF savings to buy a property with remaining lease that can last you until age 95, your CPF savings withdrawn for your property (including accrued interest) will be used to meet your FRS.
- The maximum amount you can use for this is equal to your BRS. Your monthly payouts will not increase by using your property as a pledge. When you sell your property, you will have to restore your RA up to your FRS with the sales proceed.

## Slide 9 — CPF LIFELONG INCOME FOR THE ELDERLY (CPF LIFE)

- National longevity insurance annuity scheme that provides you with monthly payouts no matter how long you live, so you never have to worry.
- CPF has set 3 different amounts that we should meet to be prepared for retirement: Basic Retirement Sum, Full Retirement Sum and Enhanced Retirement Sum.
- *Assumes male member under CPF LIFE Standard Plan, starting payouts at age 65.
