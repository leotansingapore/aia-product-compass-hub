## Slide 6 — CURRENT SITUATION

- The primary objective of policy restructuring is to optimize an individual’s or organization’s insurance coverage by replacing outdated or less advantageous policies with newer options that offer improved benefits, reduced premiums, or better alignment with current needs.
- 1. Enhance Coverage
- Upgrade to policies that provide more comprehensive or relevant coverage, addressing any gaps or outdated terms in the existing policies.
- 2. Reduce Costs
- Identify and transition to policies with more competitive premiums, potentially lowering overall insurance expenses while maintaining or improving coverage quality
- 3. Update Benefits
- Take advantage of advancements in insurance products, such as new features, riders, or benefits that are not available in older policies
- 4. Improve Suitability
- Ensure that the insurance policy aligns with the current life circumstances, financial situation, or business needs, reflecting changes in personal or organizational goals.
- 5. Maximize Value
- Increase the overall value derived from insurance investments by leveraging policies that offer better terms and conditions in the current market environment.
