## Slide 33 — WHOSE LISTENING SKILLS IS THE WEAKEST

- C is worst, followed by I, then D, and S is at least guilty because they do not have intention to reply (passive and lazy).
- I tends to talk more than listen. When they appear to be listening, they are feeling impatient because they have something to say and cannot wait to get the words out of their mouths, hence misinterpret speaker’s perspective because they jump to conclusion too soon. They also tend to miss out important details and facts, because they relate with emotions better.
- C have preconceived views about something (mono-perspective) and shuts off when the other person speaks about something that clash with their strong beliefs. They also are poor in reading “unspoken” cues or listening in between the lines because of their obliviousness and objectivity, hence guilty of being insensitive. They tend to plan what to say next in their head before the other party finishes speaking. And because they cannot multi-task, they are not listening to what the other party is saying

## Slide 34 — TIPS TO IMPROVE LISTENING SKILL

- 1) Stop preparing how and what to reply
- 2) Make conscious effort to not interrupt people or complete their sentences
- 3) Be more patient and respectful with people who speak more slowly, empathize more
- 4) Ask clarifying questions e.g. “Are you saying…? Correct me if I am wrong, so what you mean is…?”
- 5) Always maintain reasonably consistent eye contact
- 6) Put away any devices which may distract you
- 7) Listen to their emotions (concerns) and not just the words
- D if unpolished are generally very impatient people, and they want things done fast. Hence, when others speak too slowly, or beat around the bush too much, they will shut off and end your speech.
- S is usually regarded as better listeners, because of stronger empathy and generally more tolerant and patient, however it does not mean they are active and effective listeners. They just simply do not enjoy talking, so by default they just listen.
- In conclusion, negative I is the most incapable of effective listening, because they are naturally uninterested in listening (prefers to speak; more self-centered) and they cannot remember facts/details well (engineered this way; forgetful)
