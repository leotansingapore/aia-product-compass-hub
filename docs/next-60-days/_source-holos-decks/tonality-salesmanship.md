## Slide 2 — PROBLEM

- Salespeople often focus on what to say, rather than how to say it
- We are always given scripts, but almost never taught the tone to be used with it
- Misconception 1: “Tonality comes with talent.”
- Misconception 2: “Changing my tone makes me sound fake

## Slide 3 — IMPORTANCE OF TONALITY

- 10% of conflict is due to differences of opinion and 90% to the tone of voice.
- Communication is 55% body language, 38% tonality & 7% words
- Sales is the transfer of emotions
- Closing with tonality alone (without handling the objection)
- Becoming an effective communicator

## Slide 4 — Tonality is a powerful tool when mastered

- Just life a knife, one can use it for good or to harm others
- It must be used with an ethical, professional advisory process or you’ll be deemed a sleazy salesman

## Slide 7 — TONALITY OF SURPRISE

- Low intensity often used with mirroring to build rapport. Conversation will sound less like an interview
- Low: “Oh! Software engineer?” (underlying message: oh tell me more!)
- High intensity is used to create doubt (subconsciously), or build rapport with I profile
- High: “Huh really?!” (underlying message: bro for real?!)
- Conversation will reach a point of tension after high intensity

## Slide 8 — TONALITY OF EMPATHY/SINCERITY

- Effective in cushioning before making a point.
- “I understand. You’re probably feeling a little hesitant to make a decision now, because this is the first time you’re making such a big financial commitment.” (underlying message: I completely get it)
- ONLY use this tone if the objection is legit. Do NOT use at a smokescreen objection ● Bonus: Divert it instead
- “I hear what you’re saying, and I’m curious. In the first place, does this idea make sense to you?”

## Slide 9 — TONALITY OF CERTAINTY

- Most powerful tone, used in framing, closing, intent statement
- Using this tone is like cooking beef. Over-do it and the convo is gonna be dry af. Use sparingly
- ● “How I think we can move forward is through a step by step process: cashflow, emergency cash, insurance planning, medium term goals & lastly investments. Insurance takes precedence over investments and it is a non-negotiable.”

## Slide 10 — TONALITY OF URGENCY

- TONALITY OF URGENCY
- Used to start a phone call
- Breaks the usual thought pattern of the prospect
- Commands compliance
- “Hello hello? Eh Grace are you at home now??”
- vs
- ● “Hi Grace, YuChuan here, is it a bad time to talk?”

## Slide 11 — TONALITY OF DOUBT/RIDICULE

- Similar to an intense tone of surprise, creates uncertainty & makes the prospect doubt himself/herself
- Alternatively used to get a compliant reply
- “Let’s say the context is a bit different, instead of this being our first conversation, I’ve been your advisor for the past 5 years, getting you consistent returns & processing every single claim properly, you probably won’t be saying ‘I wanna think about it’ right now, right? Honestly.”

## Slide 12 — TONALITY OF REASON

- The tone that one can never say “No” to
- Generally an uptone
- Best used with the word ‘Fair’
- Alternatively used with the word ‘Probably’
- “End of the day, whether you engage my services or not, is entirely up to you. Fair?”
- “I know now you’re probably thinking, woah this guy never talk to me for 10 years……”

## Slide 13 — BONUS: TRITONAL CLOSING PATTERN (1:50)

- Tone of Certainty
- Tone of Sincerity
- Tone of Reason
- Eg: “As long as you follow this investment strategy that we crafted, trust me, becoming a millionaire is just a matter of time. Any further questions?”

## Slide 14 — It may feel uncomfortable to adopt initially, so you MUST practice

- Don’t focus on the pitch, focus on the intonations
- Tonality can be the most powerful tool in your arsenal
- Once it becomes a habit, results will naturally improve
