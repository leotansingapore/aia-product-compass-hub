# Real appointment recordings: listen-along library (52 recordings)

> What you're producing: 3 listening notes from real appointments your peers ran. Pick recordings that match the part of the appointment you're weakest at right now. Write one paragraph per recording, plus one note on a pattern you saw across all three.

## Why this matters

A script teaches you the words. Listening to a peer in a real appointment teaches you the pacing, the pauses, how they recover when something goes wrong, and the exact phrasing that moves a prospect towards a decision. Every recording here is a real opening, second or closing appointment with a real prospect, on the same products and the same objections you'll meet in your own meetings.

## Before you press play: consent and privacy

These are recordings of real people talking about their money, their families and sometimes their health. Under the PDPA, a recording of someone who can be identified is their personal data, so the library comes with rules.

- A recording belongs here only if the client agreed, before recording started, to be recorded and to the recording being used for internal training. If you can't tell whether a recording has that consent, don't use it, and tell your leader.
- When you start recording your own appointments for the library, ask at the start of the meeting, tell the client it's for internal team training, and get their agreement on the recording. If they say no, don't record. Never record a client without telling them.
- Listen somewhere private, with earphones. Not on the MRT with the transcript on your screen.
- Don't download, forward, screenshot or clip any recording, and don't share a link outside the team workspace.
- Your notes are about what the FC said and did. Leave out client names, jobs, employers, ages, health details, income and policy numbers.
- Don't mention or discuss one client's situation with another client or prospect, even without a name. "A client I listened to last week had the same worry" is still sharing someone else's story.
- Some appointments include things an FC shouldn't say, such as a projected return stated as certain. Steal the technique, not the mistake, and flag anything that worries you to your leader.

## How to use the library

1. Pick a topic below that matches what you're working on this week.
2. Open 2 or 3 recordings in that topic. Each has a summary, chapter timestamps, keywords and a full transcript, so you can jump to the part you want.
3. Take one phrase from each recording that the FC said, and copy it into your own appointment notes.
4. Submit the 3 notes and your pattern observation in the form below.

> You need to be signed in to the team's Fireflies workspace to open these. If a link gives you a 401 error, ask your leader for access.
