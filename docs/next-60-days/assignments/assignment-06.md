# Assignment 6: your signed 12-month plan

> **What you're producing:** a one-page, signed Year-1 plan with a specific FYC target, quarterly milestones, your weekly rhythm, the systems you've committed to, your one-thing focus for Month 3, and your signature. Photograph it, put it on your phone's lock screen, and send it to your mentor.

## Why this matters

Day 60 is where you stop learning the system and start running it. The first 60 days built the foundation, and the next 10 months build the business. Without a written, signed plan, most first-year FCs slide back into working whenever something comes up. The plan is the commitment that makes the next 10 months different from that default, and signing it in front of your mentor puts the commitment somewhere outside your own willpower.

Research backs the approach. Edwin Locke and Gary Latham's review of goal-setting studies found that specific, hard goals lead to better performance than vague ones. Peter Gollwitzer's work on implementation intentions found that deciding in advance when and where you'll act makes people far more likely to follow through.

## What the plan contains

Keep it to one page. The limit forces you to be specific.

### 1. Your Year-1 FYC target
One number, from the Day-2 formula:

> **Annual FYC = Appointments/week × Close rate × Case size × 48 working weeks**

Plug in your Day-59 actuals plus a realistic stretch: about 20 to 30% more appointments and a bigger case size, and a close rate 5 to 10 percentage points higher. It should stretch you and still feel believable. Remember it's a goal you're working towards. Commission income is never guaranteed.

### 2. Quarterly milestones
One line per quarter:

| Quarter | Focus |
|---|---|
| Q1 (months 3 to 5) | Consolidate the foundation: keep the Week-10 rhythm, close 3 to 5 more cases |
| Q2 (months 6 to 8) | Scale the engine: double weekly appointments, run your first client event |
| Q3 (months 9 to 11) | Deepen the book: A-tier clients and referrals |
| Q4 (month 12) | Finish strong and plan Year 2 |

Add numbers for each quarter if they help, for example *"Q1: $15K cumulative FYC, 6 closes"*.

### 3. The 5 weekly non-negotiables (from Day 58)
- [ ] Monday 3-hour calling block, protected
- [ ] Protected meeting grid, Tuesday to Thursday
- [ ] Friday weekly review: CAR scorecard, target list, three big moves
- [ ] Onboarding checklist running for every client: Day 7, 30 and 90
- [ ] Sunday 30-minute prep: pipeline, target list, calendar

### 4. The 3 systems (committed, built and running)
- [ ] Strategic Target List (Day 23): at least 20 names a month, tagged ABC and 1/2/3
- [ ] Onboarding checklist (Day 56): Day-7, 30 and 90 reminders set in your CRM
- [ ] Touchpoint calendar (Day 57): the four types (Review, Value, Celebration, Reach-out), about 7 a year for B-tier clients

### 5. Mentor and peer rhythm
- [ ] Weekly mentor check-in, 15 to 30 minutes
- [ ] Monthly peer roleplay with 2 or 3 other new FCs
- [ ] Quarterly session with a senior adviser at least 5 years ahead of you

### 6. Your one thing from Day 59
One specific, measurable behaviour you'll be able to see within four weeks. "Improve prospecting" doesn't count. Something like:
> *"I'll hold 8 seconds of silence after every loaded Fact-Find question, and track it daily."*
> *"I'll run the FACT Method word for word on every Fact-Find for 30 days until it's automatic."*
> *"I'll make 40 calls a week, every week, in a protected Monday morning block."*

### 7. Signature and date

Sign it and date it. That's your commitment on paper.

## Submission

Upload one file:
- a PDF of the signed plan (preferred), or
- a photo of the signed plan on paper, or
- a Canva or Notion document with a visible signature

Or paste a Google Doc, Canva or Dropbox link with view access into the notes field.

## Three things to do after you upload

1. **Make it your lock screen.** You check your phone dozens of times a day, so the plan will be in front of you at 10pm on a Wednesday when you're deciding whether to make that last call.
2. **Print it** or keep it in your photos, and put a copy on your desk or fridge.
3. **Read it every Monday morning** before the calling block. Keep that habit for the next 10 months.

## Sending it to your mentor

Send the plan to your mentor. You aren't asking for approval. You want a witness. A signed plan only you have seen is a private intention, and one sitting in your mentor's inbox is a commitment.

## Graduation

Submitting this assignment completes the Next 60 Days module. Here's what you have now that you didn't have 60 days ago:

- A voice: intent statement, story, tonality, frame
- A pipeline: a Strategic Target List, named warm-market contacts, referrals coming in
- A pitch: tested through at least 3 recorded pitches and real meetings
- A close: techniques matched to profiles, and objection drills you can run without thinking
- An after-sales system: onboarding ready to go
- A plan: signed, dated, photographed and read every week

From here the job is running the plan every week: every Monday call block, every referral ask, every Day-30 touchpoint and every weekly review. See you at Month 12.
