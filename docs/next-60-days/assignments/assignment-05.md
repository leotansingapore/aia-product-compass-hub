# Assignment 5: Live pitch, full integration and 8-dimension self-review

> **What you're producing:** One continuous recorded pitch (8 to 15 minutes) that pulls together Weeks 1 to 8, live to a real prospect if you can or as a simulation with a mentor if not. Then an honest 8-dimension self-review, so Week 9 targets your weak spots.

## Why this matters

Weeks 1 to 8 built up voice, the intent statement, content, prospecting, referrals, DISC recognition and tailoring, hot buttons, asking the right questions, silence, sales angles and product analysis. Day 48 is the first time you check them all together. Every part has to work for the pitch to land: story for the I, structure for the C, the hot-button callback for depth, the close for commitment.

The self-review isn't a grade. It's a diagnostic. Your 2 or 3 lowest scores decide what you prepare hardest for in Week 9 (closing and objections). A pitch with a strong close but weak objection handling gets a different Week 9 from one that stalled at the Fact-Find.

## Recording format

- Live delivery (preferred): only record with the prospect's clear agreement, given before you start, with the phone in plain view. If they'd rather not, take notes and record a 10-minute debrief video straight after instead. A recording of a prospect is their personal data, so keep it on your firm's approved storage and delete it once your review is done.
- Simulation (fine): a mentor or senior FC plays a profile and hot button you agree on beforehand, in one 8 to 15 minute take.
- Target length: 8 to 15 minutes, short enough to re-watch and long enough to show the whole pitch.
- One continuous take, no stitched segments.

## The 8 dimensions

Watch the recording twice, once for the story and once for the technique. Score each dimension from 1 to 5 with specific evidence (quote the line).

| Dimension | What to watch for |
|---|---|
| Intent statement | A structured open with hook, impression, pattern interrupt and buy-in? Scripted or natural? |
| Tonality | Certainty on the lines that carry weight, Reason on the close, Empathy on hot-button moments? |
| DISC match | Did pace, energy and content fit the profile you identified? |
| Hot-button callback | Did you explicitly quote something the prospect said earlier? |
| Angle clarity | Can you name the primary and secondary angle (W/R/L/LS)? Can you see them in the recording? |
| Feature-to-benefit | Did every feature turn into a personal benefit tied to their hot button, or did you list features? |
| Close technique | Did it suit the profile (Assumptive for D, Reassurance for I, Procedural for S and C)? Did you book a specific next step? |
| Silence discipline | Did you hold 5 to 10 seconds after loaded questions, or fill the pauses? |

## Reading your total

| Total out of 40 | What it means |
|---|---|
| 34+ | Ready for real cases, with some polishing |
| 27 to 33 | Competent, with 1 or 2 dimensions to work on in Week 9 |
| Under 27 | A foundational gap, so use Week 9 to re-record |

Nobody scores 40 on a first integration pitch. Being honest about your 3 lowest dimensions is the whole point.

## Submission

Fill in the form below: the pitch link, profile and angle, 8 scores with evidence, and your 3 lowest with Week 9 priorities.

Book a 20-minute mentor review (longer than usual, because your mentor watches the recording with you). They'll look at two things:

1. Did the pitch pull the Weeks 1 to 8 skills together, or did parts feel bolted on?
2. Where was the prospect most likely to say yes, or to drop off?

Your mentor will also flag anything you couldn't defend to compliance, such as a promised return, a "best plan" claim or pressure to sign on the spot, and you fix those before the pitch goes near a real prospect.

## Using this recording in Week 9

Week 9 is the close. Your 3 lowest scores decide where you lean hardest:

- Low close technique: prioritise Days 49 to 51 (closing).
- Low silence: prioritise Day 44. Low hot-button callback: go back to Days 37 to 39.
- Low feature-to-benefit: revisit Day 47 before the Day 54 drills.
- Low DISC match: go back to the profile day (Days 32 to 35) you missed.
