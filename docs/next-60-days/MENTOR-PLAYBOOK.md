# Mentor Playbook — Your Next 60 Days

Condensed operational guide for mentors supervising an FC through the module. Covers every practice-day gate, what to critique at each weekly review, the 9 Loom artefacts you'll see, and the 10 KPI targets to track.

Use this document as your single reference. You do not need to read all 60 day files unless a specific gap surfaces.

---

## 1. The module at a glance

- **10 weeks × 6 days = 60 days**
- **Mon–Sat cadence; Sunday rest + 30-min self-review**
- **9 practice days** (6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42, 48, 54) — each gates the next week's unlock
- **Day 60** — graduation with signed 12-month plan; no Week 11
- **Your time commitment as mentor:** ~15 min/week (weekly check-in) + 9 tape reviews (~15–20 min each)

---

## 2. Weekly KPI targets — the 10-week arc

| Week | Focus | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reset & Activate | Signed scorecard + recorded intent statement v1 |
| 2 | Your Voice I: Intent & Positioning | Intent statement v2 delivered to 3 real people |
| 3 | Your Voice II: Content & Digital Trust | 3 posts shipped + 5 DMs opened |
| 4 | Prospecting at Volume | 30 outreaches + 5 appointments booked |
| 5 | Referrals From Day One | 10 referral asks + 3 warm referrals logged |
| 6 | Reading People I: DISC | 4-profile self-assessment + 4 recorded roleplays |
| 7 | DISC II + Hot Buttons | 3 pitches × 3 profiles × 3 hot buttons |
| 8 | The Pitch | 1 live pitch recorded + 8-dimension self-review |
| 9 | The Close | 5 objection drills + ideally first case closed |
| 10 | After the Close + Graduation | Onboarding checklist + touchpoint calendar + signed 12-month plan |

**Rule:** no week unlocks until the prior week's KPI + submission artefacts are logged and you've done the tape review.

## 3. The 9 Loom / recorded artefacts

One per practice day. These are the tape artefacts you watch with the FC:

| # | Day | Artefact | Time to review |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | D6 | 90-second recorded intro | 10 min |
| 2 | D12 | 3-line log + 3–5 min Loom reflection on intent-statement deliveries | 10 min |
| 3 | D18 | Screenshot log (3 posts + 5 DMs) + Loom reflection | 15 min |
| 4 | D24 | Outreach log + Friday pipeline review screenshot + Loom | 15 min |
| 5 | D30 | Referral ask log (10 rows) + Loom | 15 min |
| 6 | D36 | 4 DISC roleplay videos (90 sec each) + self-assessment + Loom | 20 min |
| 7 | D42 | 3 pitch recordings + pitch log + Loom | 20 min |
| 8 | D48 | 1 continuous 8–15 min integration pitch + 8-dimension self-review + Loom | 25 min |
| 9 | D54 | 5 × 2-min objection drills + objection log + Loom | 20 min |

Plus **Day 60** — final 30-min session walking through the signed 12-month plan.

Total tape review time over 10 weeks: **~2.5 hours.** Plus weekly 15-min check-ins: ~2.5 hours. Roughly **5 hours of mentor time per FC across the module.**

## 4. What to critique at each weekly review

Each weekly review should focus on 1–2 things, not the whole week. Use this cheat sheet:

### Week 1 — D6 review
**Critique two things only:**
1. Did tonality shift across sections, or was the delivery flat?
2. Did the close give a *genuine* easy-out, or did it still feel like a push?

### Week 2 — D12 review
1. Were the 3 deliveries to *real prospects* (not peers or mentor)?
2. Did the buy-in land cleanly on at least one of the three?

### Week 3 — D18 review
1. Are the 3 posts answering *different* silent questions (Q1–Q5), or stuck on Q2 (expertise) only?
2. Are the DMs following the 3 non-negotiables (link back + reason to meet + easy question)?

### Week 4 — D24 review
1. Were the 30 outreaches real and substantive, or padded to hit the number?
2. Did the 5 booked appointments come from a *mix* of rings, or over-rely on one channel?

### Week 5 — D30 review
1. Were the 10 asks spoken aloud with full FACT / 10-Name structure, or shortcut over text?
2. Did the warm referrals have real endorsement, or were unendorsed names counted as warm?

### Week 6 — D36 review (tape review, longer session)
Watch all 4 roleplays. Critique:
1. Did each roleplay *actually* shift in pace, energy, and content — or did the same underlying delivery leak across all 4?
2. Which profile is the FC's native default (easiest tape) and which is their biggest counter-programming gap (hardest tape)?

### Week 7 — D42 review
1. Did the 3 pitches actually adapt across profiles, or did delivery leak through?
2. Did hot-button callbacks feel personal, or generic?

### Week 8 — D48 review (longest session, 20+ min)
Watch the full integration pitch with the FC. Score across the 8 dimensions:
1. Intent statement · 2. Tonality · 3. DISC match · 4. Hot button callback · 5. Angle clarity · 6. Feature-to-benefit · 7. Close · 8. Silence discipline
Critique:
1. Did the pitch *integrate* Weeks 1–8, or did parts feel detached?
2. Where would the prospect have been most likely to close or drop off?

### Week 9 — D54 review
1. Did each drill actually use the diagnostic (Magic Q1 minimum), or skip it?
2. Which drill's response felt most authentic? Which felt most scripted?

### Week 10 — D60 graduation (final session)
Walk through the FC's 12-month plan document. Validate:
1. Is the Year-1 FYC target realistic given Day-59 actuals (not aspirational)?
2. Are the 4 quarterly milestones specific and measurable?
3. Are the 5 weekly non-negotiables calendar-blocked?
4. Is the *one thing* from Day 59 narrow enough to drill in 4 weeks?

Sign the plan with them. Confirm the Month-3 check-in date.

## 5. Red flags to watch for

Common failure modes across the 60 days. If you see these, intervene early:

### Week-1 red flags
- FC resists recording on camera → usually means Day-4 story isn't internalised. Send them back to D4.
- Scorecard is signed but not on phone wallpaper → signal that the commitment is paper-deep, not real.

### Week-2 red flags
- Intent-statement v2 is swappable with any other FC's (the swap test) → specificity is the gap.
- FC keeps asking for "more script" → they're avoiding delivery by seeking refinement. Force them to deliver v1.5 to real prospects.

### Week-3 red flags
- All 3 posts answer Q2 (expertise) → content mix discipline issue; cover Q5 offer post + Q3 origin.
- 5 DMs are all to the same profile type → pipeline diversification issue.

### Week-4 red flags
- 30 outreaches all on one channel (30 cold DMs, say) → wrong mix; warm + semi-warm should be 75–85%.
- Appointments booked but none with Semi-Warm → lukewarm reactivation isn't happening; that's Year-2 pipeline.

### Week-5 red flags
- 10 "asks" all over text → spoken-aloud discipline isn't there.
- 3 "warm referrals" are actually 3 unendorsed names from a 10-Name batch → calling these warm is mis-training the reflex.

### Week-6 red flags
- FC scores themselves 5/5 on all 4 DISC roleplays → calibration issue; they're not seeing their own gaps. Watch a tape with them.

### Week-7 red flags
- Hot-button callbacks feel generic ("you mentioned family") → capture discipline isn't there. Send them back to D40.

### Week-8 red flags
- Integration pitch is a stitched recording, not continuous → integration skill isn't being tested. Re-record continuous.
- Self-scores across 8 dimensions are uniformly 3/5 → they're not being honest. Probe the lowest dimension.

### Week-9 red flags
- 5 drills all skip Magic Q1 and jump to response → diagnostic reflex isn't built. Re-drill.
- FC treats first case closed as the end → Week-10 onboarding risk.

### Week-10 red flags
- 12-month FYC target is 10x the 60-day run rate → aspirational, not realistic. Recalibrate.
- Onboarding checklist isn't built by Day 60 → the next client will fall through.

## 6. What *not* to do as mentor

The module is engineered so the FC builds reflexes, not dependencies. Avoid:

1. **Don't do their work.** Don't write their intent statement for them, don't call prospects on their behalf, don't draft their 12-month plan. The *act of writing it* is the rep.
2. **Don't skip tape reviews.** The tape is where gaps surface. Verbal check-ins can't see what tape shows.
3. **Don't be encouraging about padded numbers.** 24 real outreaches is worth 10 times 30 padded ones. Praise real work.
4. **Don't scope-creep the module.** 60 days is enough. Adding content dilutes the discipline.
5. **Don't grade — diagnose.** Every review is *"what's the next rep?"* not *"is this good enough?"*

## 7. The 3 check-in questions for every weekly session

Your standard 15-min weekly check-in, regardless of week:

1. **"What was this week's number?"** (Calls / Appointments / Referrals / shipped practice — whatever the KPI was)
2. **"Where's the gap between intention and execution?"** (The honest one)
3. **"What's next week's one thing?"** (Narrow it to one behaviour to drill)

Three questions, 15 minutes, every week. Simple and load-bearing.

---

## 8. Escalation

If the FC hits 2 consecutive weeks of missed KPI with no clear diagnosis:

1. **Audit their calendar.** Usually the Monday calling block has eroded. Rebuild.
2. **Audit their target list.** Usually 20 names isn't live or hasn't been combed.
3. **Audit their weekly review.** Usually being skipped.

If all 3 are live and KPIs still miss — that's a product-market-fit issue (wrong niche, wrong scripts for their specific warm-market) and deserves a longer senior-advisor conversation.

**Most missed KPIs are execution problems, not learning problems.** The content is solid; the discipline is where it breaks.

---

## 9. Beyond Day 60 — the mentor's ongoing role

The module ends Day 60. Your role doesn't.

- **Month 3 check-in** — 30 min, review their *one thing* from Day 59. Is the behaviour now reflex?
- **Month 6 check-in** — 30 min, recalibrate Year-1 FYC target against actual Q2 numbers
- **Month 9 check-in** — 30 min, prepare Q4 push + Year-2 planning
- **Month 12 review** — 60 min, full Year-1 retrospective + Year-2 plan

Without the mentor rhythm continuing into Year 1, most FCs drift. 4 touchpoints across Months 3–12 is the minimum.
