# Week 1: Reset and activate

*The first week of your career as a licensed FC. This week you turn what you studied into what you do each day.*

---

## Week 1 KPI

> Signed scorecard and a recorded intent statement.

Week 2 unlocks when both are logged, your Day 6 Loom is submitted and your mentor check-in is booked.

---

## The 6 days

- [ ] [[day-01|Day 1 — Day 1 of Your Real Career]]
  *Every prospect silently asks why they should give you a chance. Your story answers that, and the 4-part frame gives it a shape.*

- [ ] [[day-02|Day 2 — The Activity Math]]
  *FYC = appointments x close rate x case size. Close rate and case size grow with reps. Appointments you can move today.*

- [ ] [[day-03|Day 3 — Your 90-Day Scorecard + Revenue Math]]
  *Build the scorecard, learn the CAR diagnostic, tag clients ABC and prospects 1/2/3, and see what one appointment is worth.*

- [ ] [[day-04|Day 4 — Your Story (First Draft)]]
  *Write v1 of your 4-part story. Honest beats polished. Read it aloud, listen back and mark where it goes flat.*

- [ ] [[day-05|Day 5 — Tonality & Salesmanship]]
  *How you say it changes how it lands. The 6 tonalities and the three-tone close (certainty, sincerity, reason).*

- [ ] [[day-06|Day 6 — Practice: Record Your 90-Second Intro]]
  *Put the week into one recording: 4 beats in about 90 seconds, submitted on Loom.*

---

## Unlock rule for Week 2

Next week opens when you've done three things: submitted your Day 6 Loom, signed the 90-day scorecard and booked a 10-minute mentor review of the Loom.

If there's no Loom, Week 2 stays locked. There's no workaround.

---

## Sunday 30-minute review

Sunday is a rest day, but give 30 minutes of it to closing out the week. Three prompts:

1. What did you commit to this week that actually happened? Be honest. The gap between what you planned and what you did is the most useful number you have.
2. What's on the scorecard? Write your calls, appointments and referrals next to each other. They're your baseline for Week 2, where the KPI is *intent statement v2 delivered to 3 real people*.
3. Which 3 people in your warm market would be easiest to approach with the Day 6 intro? Write their names down. You'll contact them in Week 2.

---

## What's coming in Week 2

Week 2 is Your Voice I: intent and positioning. You take the 90-second intro from this week and tighten its first 30 seconds into an intent statement you can repeat. By Saturday of Week 2 you'll have delivered it to 3 real prospects.

Week 2 KPI: intent statement v2 delivered to 3 real people.
