# Day 18: Practice, 3 posts and 5 DM conversations opened

> **The one idea for today:** Week 3 counts once you've shipped posts someone can screenshot and opened DMs someone could reply to, whatever you meant to do.

By the end of today you'll have shipped 3 IG posts, each answering a different silent question from Day 13 (Q1 to Q5). You'll have opened 5 DM conversations with real prospects (mentors and peers don't count) using the texting rules and CRAB, and you'll have submitted a screenshot log and a Loom reflection. Those two things open Week 4.

---

## The bar today

In Week 1 you shipped a recorded intro, and in Week 2 you delivered your intent statement live to 3 people. Week 3 has two deliverables: content and conversations.

That's three posts and five DM conversations opened, logged and submitted by Sunday 9 pm.

There are two because content is half the pipeline (it pulls people to you) and DMs are the other half (you go to them). Post without messaging and you're hoping someone turns up. Message without posting and you're pushing with nothing to show. This week you do both.

---

## The 3 posts: what counts

Each post has to answer a different silent question from Day 13. The minimum mix:

| Post | Silent question | What to ship |
|---|---|---|
| Post 1 | Q5: easy to approach | Offer post with a DM keyword CTA |
| Post 2 | Q1 or Q3: social proof *or* origin story | Pre-during-post testimonial or 60-sec origin reel |
| Post 3 | Q2 or Q4: expertise *or* get along | Educational carousel or PPVV lifestyle post |

Why the mix: if all three posts answer Q2 (expertise), which is what most new FCs default to, you build no social proof and no connection. The 3 posts have to cover at least three of the five questions.

What doesn't count:

- A story. Stories disappear after 24 hours, so it has to be a feed post.
- A repost of someone else's content.
- A post with no call to action. If it doesn't ask the reader to do something, it isn't doing Q5's job.

Your posts count as advertising, so put them through your firm's approval process before they go live, and give yourself time for that before Sunday.

---

## The 5 DM conversations: what counts

A DM counts as "opened" when:

1. You sent a message that follows the three non-negotiable rules (link back, reason to meet, easy question), and
2. They replied with more than *"thanks"* or a single emoji, or
3. They blue-ticked you and you sent a CRAB follow-up this week.

So "opened" means there was a real exchange: at least 3 bubbles back and forth, or a completed CRAB cycle.

Examples that count:

- A value-first DM that got a reply and a short back-and-forth
- A reply to your offer post ("DM me 'plan'") that you answered and kept going
- A blue-ticked chat you sent CRAB to this week that came back with a date
- A warm contact you reconnected with in a real conversation

Examples that don't count:

- A message that was read but got no reply. It's still pending, so send a CRAB before Sunday.
- A conversation with your mentor or a peer
- An automated *"happy birthday"* that got a single *"thanks!"*

Get five real conversations by Sunday. If you're at 3 by Saturday, use Sunday to get to 5. If you hit 5 by Friday, keep going, because volume is what builds the skill.

## Submission format

### The screenshot log

Make one note (Apple Notes, Notion or a Google Doc) with two sections.

Section 1, the 3 posts:

- A screenshot of each shipped post, or a link to the live post
- A label on each saying which silent question it answers (Q1 / Q2 / Q3 / Q4 / Q5)

Section 2, the 5 DM conversations:

- A screenshot of each DM, with names and faces redacted
- One line per screenshot with the opener type (hook opener, value drop, CRAB or offer-post reply) and the outcome (replied, booked, follow-up scheduled)

That's ten screenshots and ten lines of context. You don't need to write an essay.

### The Loom reflection (3 to 5 minutes)

Record one Loom that answers three questions:

1. Which of the 3 posts did best, and why do you think it landed?
2. Which DM opener worked best for your warm market: hook-based, value-first, offer-post reply or CRAB?
3. What will your v3 fix in Week 4? (Content type, DM timing or a silent question you keep missing?)

### Mentor review

Book a 10-minute mentor check-in before Sunday 9 pm. Your mentor looks at two things only, and nothing else:

1. Are your posts answering *different* silent questions, or are you stuck on Q2 (expertise)?
2. Are your DMs following all three non-negotiables, or are you still opening with *"hey, how are you?"*

---

## The submission mistake to avoid

The common Week 3 mistake is thinking *"I posted 3 times and sent 5 DMs but none of them converted, so I feel like I failed."*

You didn't fail. Week 3 is judged on output and the quality of your reps, and results come later. Nobody closes from Day 18 output, and the week isn't built for that. It's built to show you can ship content and open conversations again and again. Results come from reps. Reps don't wait for results.

You only fail Week 3 if you:

- Don't ship 3 posts
- Don't open 5 conversations
- Ship 3 posts that all answer the same silent question
- Open 5 DMs that all broke rule 1, 2 or 3

If you shipped real output and the conversations followed the rules, you've passed Week 3. Results get measured in Month 2.

---

## Week 3 KPI: the gate for Week 4

Week 4 opens when you have all four:

- [ ] 3 posts shipped, answering different silent questions
- [ ] 5 DM conversations opened, with real exchanges that follow the three rules
- [ ] Screenshot log submitted, with 10 screenshots and 10 lines of context
- [ ] Loom reflection recorded and mentor review booked

If you're short at 6 pm on Sunday, use the rest of Sunday to finish. Week 4 opens automatically once all four are logged.

## Quiz

**Q1. To "ship 3 posts" for Week 3's gate, the posts must:**
- A) Be published on the same day
- B) Answer the same silent question for maximum reinforcement
- C) Each answer a different silent question (a mix of Q1 to Q5) ✓
- D) Be posted only on IG, never TikTok

**Why:** The gate checks your content mix as well as the count. Three Q2 posts only show you know one thing. Three posts on different questions show you can build the balanced content that prospects respond to. Q5 (the offer post) is required because that's the post that brings in DMs.

**Q2. A DM "counts as opened" for Week 3's gate if:**
- A) You sent a message that got read
- B) You sent a message AND got a real reply (3+ bubbles back and forth), OR completed a CRAB cycle on a blue tick ✓
- C) You said hi to the prospect on IG
- D) You liked their post

**Why:** "Opened" needs an exchange. A message that was only read is still pending, and it doesn't count until they reply or you've sent a CRAB follow-up and closed the loop either way. Five real exchanges show more about your skill than 20 one-way messages that went nowhere.

**Q3. Week 4 unlocks when:**
- A) You closed your first case
- B) Your posts got over 100 likes
- C) 3 posts shipped + 5 DMs opened + screenshot log submitted + Loom + mentor review ✓
- D) Your mentor approves your pitch

**Why:** The gate is built on output. You don't need a closed case, lots of likes or an approved pitch. You need to show you can ship content and open real conversations, because those are what produce results later. Gating on results would punish beginners for not having a pipeline yet, while gating on output measures what they can control today.

**Q4. Day 18 warns about this Week 3 mindset: "I posted 3 times and sent 5 DMs but none of them converted, so I feel like I failed." Why is it wrong?**
- A) It's wrong to measure outcomes at all
- B) Week 3 is judged on output and the quality of your reps, and nobody closes from Day 18 output, by design ✓
- C) Three posts are too few to measure
- D) Conversions only happen in Week 10

**Why:** Mixing up good reps with conversions is the fastest way to quit. Week 3 is there to show you can ship again and again, which has to come before any conversions, and results then follow the reps over months. Judging Week 3 by conversions is like judging a seedling by how much fruit it has.

**Q5. Which of these still "counts" as a shipped post for the Week 3 gate?**
- A) A story that lives for 24 hours
- B) A reposted quote from someone else's account
- C) A feed post with a Q5 DM-keyword CTA ✓
- D) A comment you left on someone else's post

**Why:** The gate is about feed posts, which stay up and can be found. Stories disappear after 24 hours and are a separate surface. Reposts don't build your own credibility, and comments are engagement. A feed post with a CTA stays up, you can point to it in DMs, and it keeps doing Q5's job of starting conversations.

**Q6. The mentor review on Day 18 looks at two things only. What are they?**
- A) Grammar and looks
- B) Whether the posts answer different silent questions (not all Q2), and whether the DMs follow all three non-negotiables (and don't open with "hey, how are you?") ✓
- C) Whether you closed a case this week
- D) Whether your highlights look professional

**Why:** Keeping the review narrow saves you from cosmetic feedback. Your content mix (spread across Q1 to Q5) and your DM habits (the 3 non-negotiables) are the two things that decide whether Week 4 onward builds on itself. The rest can be fixed in Weeks 4 to 10 as long as those two are right.

**Q7. A Week 3 FC ships 3 posts, and all three are educational carousels. Is the gate passed?**
- A) Yes, three posts is three posts
- B) No. The gate requires *different silent questions*, so 3 Q2 posts fail the content-mix check ✓
- C) Yes, with a warning
- D) No, the posts must all be videos

**Why:** The gate checks the mix, not only the count. Three Q2 posts show you can ship, but not that you can cover the rest of what builds trust. Go back to Day 13, find the questions you skipped and re-ship one post as a different type.
