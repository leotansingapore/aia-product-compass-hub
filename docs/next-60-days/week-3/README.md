## Unlock rule for Week 4

Week 4 opens when you have done all four of these:

1. Shipped 3 IG feed posts that answer different silent questions. One of them has to be a Q5 offer post.
2. Opened 5 DM conversations with real exchanges. Chats with your mentor or peers don't count.
3. Submitted the Day 18 screenshot log and Loom reflection.
4. Booked a 10-minute mentor review.

If nothing has shipped, Week 4 stays locked.

---

## Sunday 30-minute review

Close the week with three questions:

1. Which of the 5 silent questions is your content weakest on? Look at this week's 3 posts and the 10 before them. Whatever question is missing is the first thing you draft in Week 4.
2. Which DM opener got the best replies from your warm market: hook-based, value-first, offer-post reply or CRAB? Use that one most in Week 4.
3. Which 5 warm-market names are next? Week 4 doubles the pace, so get a longer list ready now.

Prospecting at volume. This week you opened conversations one at a time. Next week you prospect to a weekly number with the Activity Matrix and the warm-market scripts, and you'll see where cold prospecting fits.

Week 4 KPI: 30 outreaches; 5 appointments booked.
