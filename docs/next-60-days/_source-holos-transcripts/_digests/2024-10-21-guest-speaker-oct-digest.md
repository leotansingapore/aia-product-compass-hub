# Guest Speaker — October 2024 (Digest)

## Who / context
Two guests from another AWM agency (ED: Guan; upline: Wilfred, Sing Hong). **Brian** (~4yr in, chasing COT, ~10 direct) and **JY/"juny"** (~2yr in, still a student-advisor, chasing COT, promoted manager Jul 2024, ~15 direct incl. exploring). Audience: Odyssey FINternship cohort, 7:30am HOLOS session. Framing: "find the 1–2% to sharpen."

## Top frameworks / concepts taught
- **5 Levels of Competency (Hormozi)**: positive-proactive → positive-passive → neutral-passive → negative-passive → actively-expects-to-fail. Self-audit + diagnose associates.
- **In vs on the business**: 1x1 (sales job) vs 10x1 (systems, branding) vs 10x10 (team).
- **Buying hours / BSM**: calc $/hr, outsource admin, AGIA, onboarding, client servicing.
- **Pre-appointment agenda (anti-DEQ)**: state what the appointment covers before it starts. JY credits this for a closing-ratio jump.
- **ACTN (Norman Levine)**: Aspirations → Current → Take away the dream → Implied concern → Now. Works for sales AND recruitment.
- **Branding = documentation of life** (credit: Wilfred). "Fine enough to inspire, close enough to relate."
- **Estate planning as low-barrier entry to parents market**: Will + LPA + AMD → policy summary → bigger cases.

## Daily/weekly routines (if mentioned)
Brian: office 7am (posts a story), 1–2 IG stories every other day, 15–20min motivational content before sleep. JY: pre-appointment agenda on every meet. Joint FW opportunities capped at 3 per associate (Brian takes no share) to force them to value each slot. Weekly recruitment event with *pledged* bring-down count + penalty/reward. Social prospecting as a scheduled activity — "I never know who I meet." Client tiering (Gold/Platinum) verbalised with annual referral expectation. FORM (Family, Occupation, Recreation, Money) baked into prospecting messages.

Client: "If my advisor isn't a millionaire by 40, can't be my advisor — but integrity matters more." Lesson: HNW founders are more relatable than advisors assume.
- **Search-fund matchmaking (Brian)**: Connected his search-fund client with a friend's father (car SME, 8-figure exit) whose kids won't take over. No commission — plays matchmaker, farms the retirement/legacy case.
- **First associate Fang (JY)**: Recruited before any accolades. Fang joined because he liked JY as a person and saw a growth environment — you don't need to be a producer to recruit; speak from where *they* are.

## Teaching angle for Day 58 "How a top producer runs a week"
- **Weekly time audit**: ratio of *in the business* vs *on the business*.
- **Hire/deploy a BSM early** — calc $/hr, stop sub-hourly admin yourself.
- **Weekly non-negotiables**: one recruitment event with pledged bring-down, 2–3 branding posts, one second-brain review.
- **Social prospecting as a calendar block** — Brian's bar-meets-recruit story proves it.
- **Pre-appointment agenda** built into every meeting send-out — a weekly habit.
