## Top frameworks / concepts taught
- **"[Trademarked] of Life" diagnostic** — 10–15 min fact-based coverage gap tool pulled from MDRT website; non-pushy opener.
- **Three drivers of rising retirement sums** — (1) cost of living, (2) life expectancy (Google "SG lifespan"), (3) standard-of-living creep. Memorize and recite to clients.
- **CPF as "basic retirement" framing** — Don't bash CPF; position it as forced-savings floor ($1,560/mo at FRS today), then quantify the gap vs desired lifestyle using the CPF Retirement Payout Planner.
- **Gap-to-Solution close** — Use CPF Planner live with client, surface monthly shortfall (e.g. $2,800), then propose private instruments (Invest Easy / endowment / APPA) to fill it.
- **Walk-the-talk proof** — Show own dividend statements, own DCA into StashAway/Endowus/Syfe, own mortgages paid by dividends. Censor client names but show real screenshots.
- **Conditioning from the start** — Sale closes at the *beginning* of the appointment via analogies (tree + water + fertilizer = 10-yr endowment), Big-4 fund manager name-drops, and pre-empting FAQs before the client asks.

## Daily/weekly routines (if mentioned)
- **3–4 appointments/day**, typically 1–2 morning, 1 afternoon, 1 evening. Appointments run ~3 hrs; home by 10–11pm; preps next morning before bed.
- **Mon–Sat working, Sunday fully blocked** for family.
- **Years 1–2: 2 hrs/day of warm calls** every evening to friends/acquaintances. Called even 5 min before appointments. Saturdays included. Stopped warm calling end of Year 3 — pipeline became referral-driven autopilot.
- **Has a PA** for claims / non-client-facing admin only. Refuses to let PA handle client-facing work or appointment setting.
- **Referral book** physically placed in front of client during appointment; asks for 2–3 names, never more.
- **Post-sale nurture**: buys gifts for clients' parents, visits houses, asks about family members to surface next-gen leads.
