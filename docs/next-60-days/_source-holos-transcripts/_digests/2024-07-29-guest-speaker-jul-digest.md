# Guest Speaker — July 2024 (Digest)

## Who / context
Guest is "Beverly" (Bly), AIA FSC joining mid-2015, ~9 full years in at time of sharing. Track record: Superstar year one (half year), Superstar Platinum another year, 6x MDRT, 3x consecutive COT (including ranking #1 of ~600 agents in 2020). Client base ~800. Host is Kevin, who knew her pre-industry (both worked part-time, she was a top surveyor). Audience is his own district/team — this is her first external sharing. Format: Q&A, ~60 min + Q&A tail.

## Top frameworks / concepts taught
- **Acquire first, deepen later:** Close something small (PA, H&S, Careshield) to convert prospect into client; upsell and branch into family later. "Nothing is too big or too small — so long as you buy something from me, you are my client."
- **Portfolio Summary as branching tool:** After first small case, do a portfolio summary; condition it on reviewing the whole family ("if I do one for you, I need to do for your family also"). This is her core mechanism for moving from 1 cold lead to a household of clients.
- **Volume over whales:** Prefers regular premium over single premium/legacy. Reasoning: trail income, case-count resilience, less time sunk per client, and high-net-worth whales don't compound.
- **PA as trust builder + recurring income:** PA is easiest to claim; claims build trust faster than 20 years of unclaimed coverage. PA currently pays her ~$4K/month recurring.
- **Sprint-Stop cadence (not marathon):** Works hard ~6 months/year (Jul–Dec), travels/rests Jan–Jun. Allows emotional downtime but capped at ~2 months max. "When I sprint, I stop. When I sprint, I have to hit what the marathon runner hit."
- **Share-case with seniors across agencies:** Pay "school fees" by bringing seniors on joint appointments to learn closing styles for unfamiliar profiles.

## Daily/weekly routines (if mentioned)
Early-career (first 1.5 years, full roadshow): 5 days/week, 10am–10pm on the ground, 2 off days consumed by manual FHR paperwork and follow-up — ~60 hrs/week minimum. Costing $300–400/day, broke even on EPS. Current: 6 months grind / 6 months off. Still goes back to roadshow 2–3 days/month even now to acquire new blood since her 800-client base is maxed on product gaps. Every roadshow must close something small OR produce one quality follow-up lead. Prefers home appointments (gets both spouses + extended family exposure). Does not ask for referrals directly. Annual portfolio review cycle — if a gap wasn't closed this year, re-pitch next year, and the year after.

## Memorable quotes (2–4)
- "The real competitor is really yourself. No matter how well you do there will be someone better outside — my only aim is to do better than the previous year."
- "If you lose something from a relationship or family, you have to gain something from there. If not, it will really be a painful lesson without any gains."
- "You can be sad, you can be down. At roadshow take a good 1–2 hours. After that you sprint."
- "Never judge a book by its cover — even a work-permit holder, sell a small PA. Client acquired."

## Stories / worked examples (1–3)
1. **2017 breakup year:** Used the loss as fuel — refused to let emotions hit production; reframed as "gain something back." Kept hitting targets.
2. **2020 COVID COT:** Lost first 2 months (CNY travel) then lockdown. Only 7 months to run against a benchmark reduced by 40%. Had to run ~20K+/month FYC. Finished #1 in district (~600 agents).
3. **2022–2023 family rupture:** Had been financially supporting grandmother, mother, four cousins, uncles (~7–10 people on her premiums) for years; goal was a bigger house to live with grandmother. Grandmother refused and chose her son's family. Beverly cut off the family, was depressed for most of a year, still cleared Centurion and hit COT-by-income in H2 2023 on "ego" alone.

## Teaching angle for Day 58 "How a top producer runs a week"
- Model the **sprint-stop year** explicitly: 6 months full-throttle, 6 months recovery/travel — counter-narrative to "grind 12 months."
- Frame the **daily close rule** from her roadshow DNA: every activity session must produce either (a) one small close or (b) one quality follow-up lead. No zero days.
- Teach the **small-case-first → portfolio-summary → family-tree branching** as a concrete weekly workflow, not a philosophy.
- Cover the **emotional downtime cap** (2 months max) as a legitimate but bounded rule — important for newly-joined FCs who will hit their first slump.
- Use her **PA-as-foundation** stance to anchor weekly activity targets in trust-building products, not whale hunts.
