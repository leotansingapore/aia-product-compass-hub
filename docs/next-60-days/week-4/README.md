*A full calendar with cases pending comes from prospecting. Stop prospecting and the business stops with it.*

## Unlock rule for Week 5

You need all five:

1. 30 substantive outreaches across a mix of approaches: Market Survey calls, 6-step messages, Attraction Script, CRAB follow-ups and value drops. No padding.
2. 5 Fact-Find appointments, each with a confirmed time and format that both of you have acknowledged.
3. The Friday pipeline review screenshot with your CAR numbers and target list status.
4. The Loom reflection.
5. A booked 10-minute mentor review.

Soft bookings and padded outreach don't count.

---

## Sunday 30-minute review

Close the week with three questions:

1. Which approach booked the most meetings? Lean on it in Week 5, and keep two or three others running.
2. Which Market Temperature ring is most alive for you? If most bookings came from Warm, start reactivating Semi-Warm contacts in Week 5. If Semi-Warm is already working, push harder there, because for most people it's the biggest ring.
3. Is the Strategic Target List ready for Week 5? You want 20 new names, each with a trigger and an approach, by Sunday night. On Monday morning you should already know who you're calling.

---

## What's coming in Week 5

Referrals from day one. Week 5 adds a referral ask to every Fact-Find, so Week 6's pipeline doesn't rest on your outbound effort alone. You'll learn the FACT method, how to coach clients on what to say to their friends, and how the CAR loop feeds itself.

Week 5 KPI: 10 referral asks made, 3 referrals received.
