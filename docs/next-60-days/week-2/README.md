## Unlock rule for Week 3

You need all four:

1. Deliver your intent statement to 3 real prospects. Mentors and peers don't count.
2. Log each delivery in 3 lines, so 9 lines in total.
3. Submit the Day 12 Loom reflection.
4. Book a 10-minute mentor review.

---

## Sunday 30-minute review

Close the week with three questions:

1. Did all 3 deliveries happen? If one didn't, write down which one and what got in the way. Whatever blocked you this week will turn up again in Week 3, so name it now.
2. Which ingredient of your intent statement needs the most work: hook, impression, pattern interrupt or buy-in? Pick one. That's what v3 fixes.
3. Which 3 people from your warm market come next? Week 3 takes the same statement into DMs, so have another 3 names ready.

---

## What's coming in Week 3

Your voice II, content and digital trust. Week 3 turns the intent statement you've been saying out loud into something you write. You'll cover the 5 silent questions prospects check before they DM you, testimonials that people actually believe, and the DM scripts that start a real conversation.

Week 3 KPI: 3 posts shipped, 5 DMs opened.
